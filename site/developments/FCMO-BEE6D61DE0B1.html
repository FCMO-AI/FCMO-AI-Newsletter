Anthropic reports experiments in which Claude autonomously trained models to improve performance across public benchmarks covering ten categories of alignment failure, extending earlier work on automated alignment research.

Why it matters: If robust, this is evidence that AI systems can perform closed-loop safety research and model-improvement work rather than only propose ideas, which matters as model development itself becomes increasingly automated.
