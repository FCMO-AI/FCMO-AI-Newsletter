LiLiCorr correlates parallel draft marginals for faster speculative decoding

NVIDIA Nemotron researchers published LiLiCorr, a speculative-decoding method that correlates per-position marginals from parallel drafts in one network pass and turns coherence modeling into matrix operations.

Why it matters: It targets the core weakness of parallel block drafters—independent token marginals—without giving up their parallelism, making it directly relevant to high-throughput multi-token prediction and speculative serving.

Technical record

Mechanism
Correlates per-position marginals from a parallel block drafter in a single pass, expressing draft coherence through parallelizable matrix operations.
Demonstrated result
NVIDIA reports evaluation across benchmarks, serving concurrencies and input lengths, with LiLiCorr fastest in almost all evaluated settings.
Publisher claim
The authors position LiLiCorr as a broadly faster speculative-decoding system than evaluated alternatives.
