Tencent Hy4 preview combines 770B MoE capacity with sparse attention, IndexCache and native MTP

Evidence AconfirmedImpact 8/10 · Field-shifting · legacy-normalizedactive

model_or_system_release · development FCMO-1E28FC528202 · verified 2026-08-29T19:08:00Z

Importance: 8/10 — Field-shifting. Legacy v1 raw score 5/5 is deterministically normalized to 8/10. Importance measures consequence if true, not evidence quality.

Tencent released Hy4 preview under Apache-2.0 with a 770B-parameter MoE backbone, 49B active parameters per token, 1M context, Gated DeepSeek Sparse Attention, cross-layer IndexCache reuse, four-stream identity Hyper-Connections, and a native MTP layer intended for speculative decoding.

Why it matters: Hy4 is a third major open-weight frontier release in the same week emphasizing sparse long-context retrieval and richer residual routing, while also shipping native multi-token prediction. It strengthens the architectural-convergence signal but does not by itself establish that these mechanisms outperform matched dense or hybrid alternatives.

Technical record

Mechanism
A 78-layer MoE backbone: one dense FFN layer followed by 77 MoE layers with 256 routed experts plus one shared expert; top-8 routed experts are active per token. Attention uses Gated DeepSeek Sparse Attention with IndexCache to reuse sparse indices across layers. The residual path uses four-stream identity Hyper-Connections. A separate native MTP layer adds 10B total/0.7B active parameters for speculative decoding.
Demonstrated result
Released BF16/FP8 model artifacts and configuration expose a 770B backbone with 49B active parameters per token, 1M context, 256 routed experts, top-8 routing, Gated DSA, four residual streams and native MTP. Apache-2.0 licensing is present in the released artifact.
Publisher claim
Tencent reports its largest generation-over-generation capability gain and small wins over GLM-5.3 and Kimi K3 in an internal blind evaluation of 163 experts across 203 engineering tasks.
Strongest baseline
Tencent's internal blind comparison against GLM-5.3 and Kimi K3; no matched-compute architectural ablation is supplied in the release card.
Regime
Frontier-scale 770B-total/49B-active open-weight model; serving remains hardware-heavy despite sparse activation. Internal productivity evaluation is vendor-run and not an independent reproduction.
Implementation
Weights, FP8 variant, config, deployment guidance, finetuning guidance and compression tooling are released.
Compute / cost
49B backbone parameters are active per token plus 0.7B active in the native MTP layer; total stored capacity is 770B backbone plus 10B MTP. Exact training FLOPs and matched serving throughput were not found in the primary release material reviewed.
Novelty
Integrates cross-layer sparse-index reuse and native MTP into a frontier open MoE while adopting the emerging richer-residual/sparse-retrieval design family.
Reproducibility
Architecture and weights are inspectable; vendor quality claims and the isolated contribution of Gated DSA, IndexCache, iHC and MTP require independent matched-control evaluation.

Evidence claims

  • DEMONSTRATED — Tencent released Hy4 preview with a 770B backbone, 49B active parameters per token, Gated DSA with IndexCache, four residual streams, and a native MTP layer.
  • CLAIMED — Tencent reports Hy4 preview slightly ahead of GLM-5.3 and Kimi K3 on its internal 203-task expert evaluation.

Limitations / contrary evidence

  • The strongest quality comparison disclosed in the model card is vendor-run and narrowly separated from GLM-5.3/Kimi K3; it is not independent evidence.
  • No matched-compute ablation isolates the contribution of sparse attention, IndexCache, iHC or native MTP.
  • 49B active parameters still implies a heavy local serving regime; sparse activation does not make the 770B stored model a small local model.
  • Tencent explicitly notes overlong reasoning and over-verification in this preview.
