Jiangsu sets AI employment monitoring and skills-training targets

Jiangsu's provincial government office published a September 29, 2026 employment-policy notice that calls for assessing the employment effects of major policies and public investment and establishing a survey of AI's effects on jobs and skills. For labor-intensive sectors, it says the province will explore minimum human-participation standards and a displacement buffer period. The notice also sets a target of one million person-times of AI-field technical-skills training during the fifteenth Five-Year Plan period. These are announced measures and targets; the notice does not show funding, delivery, implementation or employment effects. The policy predates and is separate from China's later October 16 national AI-employment opinion.
