South Korea's Rural Development Administration says its agriculture LLM is in an internal pilot

South Korea’s Rural Development Administration (RDA) said on 12 November 2026 that it had completed an agriculture and life-sciences language model using government-provided compute and had been piloting it inside the agency since October. RDA reports training on roughly 35,000 agriculture and life-sciences papers and reports and says the model returns answers with supporting sources. The agency has begun a separate research-collaboration AI project targeting 2028, with planned support for hypothesis generation, analysis and interpretation. These are agency-reported milestones and plans; the release provides no benchmark, independent evaluation, or measured research-productivity result.
