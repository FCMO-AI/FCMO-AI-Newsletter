What changed

EuroHPC’s AI Gigafactories tender page, updated 5 November 2026, says a technical issue invalidates all draft and submitted tenders in the system for both lots; replacement submission links are forthcoming, and applicants are told not to continue current drafts. The page retains deadlines of 4 February 2027 for Lot 1 and 3 December 2026 for Lot 2. The prior Lot 1 extension was publicly reported by the Czech ministry on 26 October to allow Member States time for national budget procedures and financial commitments. This is a change to the procurement process; it establishes no consortium awards, committed investment, facility construction, operating compute or access.

Evidence

  • Demonstrated — On 2026-10-26, the Czech Ministry of Industry and Trade reported that EuroHPC had extended the bid deadline for Lot 1, the medium-scale AI Gigafactories call, to 4 February 2027, while the Lot 2 deadline remained unchanged.
  • Demonstrated — EuroHPC's current call page lists Lot 1 bids due 4 February 2027 at 17:00 Europe/Luxembourg and Lot 2 bids due 3 December 2026 at 17:00 Europe/Luxembourg; it says the Lot 1 extension allows Member States to complete national budget procedures and submit financial commitments.
  • Demonstrated — EuroHPC states that a 5 November technical issue means existing draft and submitted tenders for both lots will not be considered; applicants must recreate and resubmit under replacement links that the authority says it will announce once its technical solution is ready.

Strongest baseline

Before the 5 November update, EuroHPC displayed Lot 1 and Lot 2 submission links. It now says all existing drafts/submissions are invalid for both lots and must be recreated under replacement links; the listed lot deadlines remain 4 February 2027 and 3 December 2026.

Limitations

  • The 5 November update is an official contracting-authority notice, but the replacement submission links and controlling portal corrigendum were not accessible in this review.
  • The page supports the current deadlines and procedural notice only; no consortium award, funding commitment, facility construction, operating capacity or compute access is established.
  • The Czech ministry’s 26 October report supplies the date of the Lot 1 extension report; the EuroHPC page does not identify the formal adoption date of that deadline change.

What we still don't know

  • The new submission links and formal Funding & Tenders Portal corrigendum were not available in the inspected page. The contracting authority says replacement links will be announced after its technical solution is ready.
  • No consortium selection, signed funding commitment, facility construction or operating compute availability is established by these notices.

Why it matters

The submission reset affects the application path for a major European AI-compute procurement, while the lot-specific deadlines remain in place. The authority’s notice supports the procedural change and stated reason; it does not establish selected consortia, financing, built facilities or available compute.
