Evidence

  • Claimed — OpenAI reports that its system produced a refutation of ETH. The linked Lean scope states that, for each fixed k at least 2, a deterministic machine exactly counts satisfying assignments of canonical k-CNF formulas in subexponential time with polynomial space; the manuscript states a sharper exponent for m clauses. What we still don't know

  • The community now reports pinned independent executions and a formal port to a standard multitape-machine library with multiple checkers, but the research desk did not rerun these artifacts. The correspondence from the checked formal statements to the informal manuscript and textbook ETH remains unestablished as a theorem.

Why it matters

If the formalized and manuscript claims are correct and match standard ETH, complexity results that rely on ETH would need reassessment. A subexponential exact algorithm would not by itself establish polynomial-time solvability or settle P versus NP.
