TC260 issues general and sector guidance for AI application security

On September 17, 2026, China’s National Technical Committee 260 on Cybersecurity published four AI application security practice guides: one general guide and three for education, health, and broadcasting/online audiovisual services. The general guide, TC260-PG-20268A v1.0-202609, is organized across planning, design and development, validation, deployment, operation and monitoring, continued assessment, and retirement. The three sector guides state distinct audiences and application settings for education institutions, health institutions and technology providers, and broadcasting/online audiovisual organizations. The issuer describes these as practice guidance documents; this record does not establish binding legal force, organizational adoption, compliance, or measured safety outcomes.
