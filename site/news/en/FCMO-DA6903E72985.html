Brazil’s MGI considers stronger rules for federal workers’ use of AI tools

A Sep 14, 2026 Tele.Síntese interview reported that Brazil’s Management and Innovation Minister Esther Dweck was considering giving an existing ethical-AI-use manual for federal workers stronger force as a Portaria or presidential decree. Dweck also said a federal self-assessment found more than 150 AI solutions already in use and more than 300 initiatives under study or development. The statements document reported policy intent and ministry-reported inventory counts, not an enacted nationwide rule or audited outcomes. A bounded official MGI search found ministry-level governance and guidance but no manual-specific nationwide instrument; a full Diário Oficial check remains open. A later review of the official MGI AI page found that it labels 2025 federal indicators as 182 AI solutions in operation, 144 agencies planning to adopt AI assistants and 44 agencies with AI guidelines. These remain ministry-reported self-assessment figures; the linked dynamic dashboard provides no dated export, denominator or definitions, so they do not independently establish deployment outcomes or a trend.
