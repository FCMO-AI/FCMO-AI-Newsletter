ANEEL approves Group A electricity-market migration and Open Energy rules

ANEEL’s 27 October 2026 announcement says its board approved rule changes for Group A consumers in medium and high voltage and instituted Open Energy. The announcement describes a standardized, consent-based way to access and share consumption, billing and metering data, with an initial comparison page and standardized access due within 12 months of rule publication and API sharing within 24 months. For most simplified AS/A4 cases it reports migration deadlines falling from 180 to 90 days; other cases remain at 180 days.
