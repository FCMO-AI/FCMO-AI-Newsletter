NVIDIA and Meta announce expanded Muse work and planned AI infrastructure deployment

NVIDIA and Meta announced on October 27, 2026 that they will expand work on Meta’s Muse models and pursue additional NVIDIA infrastructure deployments for agent workloads. NVIDIA says the collaboration is intended to improve training and inference performance and total cost of ownership, and says it will invest $3 billion in Meta’s equity offering. Meta’s Oct 26 SEC preliminary prospectus—filed before the announcement—describes only collective nonbinding interest from NVIDIA, AMD and Broadcom up to $5 billion in aggregate. The announcement documents the companies’ stated plans, not hardware delivery, investment completion, measured performance or cost gains, or improved security.
