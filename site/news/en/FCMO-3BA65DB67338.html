Spain's IA360 announcement sets a 12-month roadmap for AI infrastructure, adoption and governance

On September 21, 2026, the Spanish government presented its IA360 plan as a roadmap of actions for the coming 12 months. La Moncloa groups the announced goals around strengthening technological capacity—including an AI gigafactory, Barcelona Supercomputing Center models and data centres—supporting adoption through an AI voucher for SMEs and self-employed workers and adapting secondary and vocational education, strengthening cybersecurity and oversight of frontier models, and negotiating a national social compact on AI-related work and life changes. These are official policy intentions and announced actions, not evidence that funding, construction, model development, regulation or implementation has occurred. The Spanish primary announcement and speech transcript support the bounded account; execution, budgets, dates and measurable outcomes remain open.
