UK proposes a 100 MW indicative threshold and national decision framework for data-centre projects

The Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade published an initial proposal for a National Policy Statement (NPS) for data centres in England and opened consultation through 8 January 2027. The draft describes how applications directed into the Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project (NSIP) process would be examined and decided. It says projects with at least 100 MW of rated IT load are likely to be considered nationally significant and suitable for direction under section 35, while allowing case-by-case exceptions. The proposal also sets out assessment principles for energy, environment, security and local impacts. It is a draft subject to consultation and parliamentary designation; no project decisions or effects are demonstrated.

Limitations

  • The 100 MW figure is indicative and applies to rated IT load; the Secretary of State retains case-by-case judgment and may consider exceptional lower-capacity projects.

Why it matters

A dedicated planning framework could shape how large data-centre projects in England seek nationally significant consent and how their energy, environmental and community impacts are assessed. The 100 MW threshold is indicative rather than an automatic rule, and the draft remains open to consultation; implementation and project effects are not yet established.
