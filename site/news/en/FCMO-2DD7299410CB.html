A mathematical-analysis preprint reports mismatches between OpenAI’s Navier-Stokes proof and cited Lean estimates

Bastounis, Circelli and Hansen compare OpenAI’s natural-language Navier-Stokes proof with cited Lean declarations. In Lemma 8.6, equation (8.19), they report that the paper states an m+4 derivative estimate while the cited Lean estimate requires m+5 derivatives. For equation (10.19), they report a distinct pressure-flux bound and proof route in Lean. The authors argue that these examples challenge whether the formalization faithfully verifies the announced prose. This is a source-reported analysis, not an independent refutation of the underlying blow-up theorem; it is an October 6 preprint without established peer review or specialist consensus in this check.
