Evidence

  • Demonstrated — Japan’s Cabinet decided on 2026-10-20 to establish an AI and Digital Reform Promotion Council to coordinate cross-government work on social transformation through AI/digital use, including reviews of socioeconomic rules and institutions, AI/digital infrastructure, and AX/DX implementation.
