WHO report maps ethics oversight across three forms of AI-related health research

A World Health Organization report dated 21 July 2026 examines ethics review and oversight for three categories of AI-related health research: health research using AI with data, research conducted with AI tools, and research about AI tools and technologies. Its official overview describes gaps in existing standards and ethics-review capacity, complementary roles for funders, journals and regulators, and equity risks affecting low- and middle-income countries. The report offers non-exhaustive considerations for strengthening oversight; it is guidance, not a binding regulation, and this review did not inspect the full PDF or verify implementation effects.
