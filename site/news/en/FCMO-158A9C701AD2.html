Anthropic proposes a growth-linked equity stake for a new public-benefit fund

Anthropic announced that, subject to stockholder approval, 5% of its shares will be committed to a new wholly owned Anthropic Public Benefit Fund LLC. The company says its board may add 2% of shares for each 50% increase in company value above a baseline it has not disclosed. It says the LLC will not use its equity for lobbying or political giving, and says it will describe governance, valuation milestones and a public-benefit report in coming months. This announcement documents the company’s stated plan; it does not establish approval, transfer, fund operations, distributions or outcomes.
