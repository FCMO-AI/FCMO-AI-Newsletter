PolyGraph reports plan-buffered CUDA Graph capture for adaptive speculative decoding

The PolyGraph preprint proposes one maximum-grid CUDA Graph with GPU-resident updates for changing speculative-decoding shapes. Its abstract reports a single-H800 trace-driven prototype that replaces 64 shape-specific graphs with one, gives warmed P50 latency of 17.7 vs 17.8 ms and cuts graph-build time from 3,915 to 93 ms. The author also reports a P99 benefit that depends on padding waste. This is a bounded synthetic-trace result, not a production serving demonstration; independent reproduction remains open. The arXiv page says the v1 was submitted on 14 August 2026 despite the identifier prefix 2610, an unexplained metadata discrepancy.

Evidence

  • Demonstrated — The arXiv record identifies PolyGraph as arXiv:2610.23861v1 and gives a version-1 submission date of 14 August 2026. The record's 2610 identifier conflicts with that date; no explanation was available in this review.
  • Claimed — The author describes a plan-buffer mechanism that captures one maximum-grid CUDA Graph and writes the active shape to GPU memory before replay; the abstract says this reduces shape-specific graphs from 64 to one using a 28-byte update. On a trace-driven single-H800 prototype with Qwen2.5-3B dimensions, the author reports warmed step latency of 17.7 vs 17.8 ms P50 and graph-build time reduced from 3,915 to 93 ms.
  • Claimed — The abstract reports that the P99 advantage over a fixed maximum-padding control is negligible at zero padding waste and reaches 18.7% at 100% padding waste.
  • Not established — The abstract limits the evidence to synthetic traces on one GPU and states that the work is not a production serving system; independent reproduction, production integration and cross-model or cross-hardware transfer are not established.

Strongest baseline

Warmed baseline with shape-specific CUDA Graphs for step latency; MaxPad fixed-draft control for isolating padding waste.

Limitations

  • Performance claims are author-reported and based on synthetic traces on one H800.
  • The result is a custom-kernel prototype and does not establish production-serving benefit.
  • P99 improvements over maximum padding depend on padding waste and are negligible at zero waste.
  • The paper's reported August 14 submission date conflicts with the 2610 identifier prefix; this remains unexplained.

What we still don't know

  • The full paper, implementation and trace data were not inspected in this activation; the reviewed source was the official abstract and version history.
  • All measurements are author-reported for one H800 synthetic-trace prototype; no independent reproduction or production serving evidence was reviewed.
  • The official arXiv page reports a v1 submission date of 14 August 2026 while the identifier prefix is 2610; no authoritative explanation was found.

Why it matters

Adaptive speculative decoding changes workload shape during generation, while CUDA Graph replay expects fixed launch configurations. If independently reproduced beyond the reported synthetic single-GPU setup, a plan-buffer mechanism could lower capture overhead without padding every step to a maximum shape. The abstract shows little P99 benefit when no padding work is wasted, so the gain is workload-dependent.
