China issues multi-agency finance guideline for three science and technology hubs

A guideline dated 19 October and published by China's Ministry of Science and Technology the next day was jointly issued by 11 central agencies to support finance for innovation hubs in Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei, the Yangtze River Delta and the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area. It sets 2030 and 2035 goals and directs central-local science funding, regional venture-capital funds, patient capital, a relending pilot for R&D-intensive private SMEs, cross-region finance and data coordination, technology-transfer finance, bond support and selected cross-border services. Regional authorities are asked to prepare implementation rules. The document establishes policy directions, not committed amounts, implementation, capital uptake or economic outcomes.
