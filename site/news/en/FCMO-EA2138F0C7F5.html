Mingbird reports improved artifact completion by small local models on a bounded agent benchmark

The authors compare a local-first agent harness with three stock harnesses across four small open models and 18 self-built tasks, reporting an average task score of 0.886 for Mingbird versus 0.631, 0.479 and 0.405 for the named baselines. A separate 278-task tau-squared comparison reports 0.856 under a common user simulator. The paper publishes per-cell results and discusses artifact checks, finish gates and anti-loop controls. These are author-reported benchmark results: the primary matrix uses one machine, one counted run per cell and tasks co-developed with some harness mechanisms.

What actually changed

The authors compare a local-first agent harness with three stock harnesses across four small open models and 18 self-built tasks, reporting an average task score of 0.886 for Mingbird versus 0.631, 0.479 and 0.405 for the named baselines. A separate 278-task tau-squared comparison reports 0.856 under a common user simulator. The paper publishes per-cell results and discusses artifact checks, finish gates and anti-loop controls. These are author-reported benchmark results: the primary matrix uses one machine, one counted run per cell and tasks co-developed with some harness mechanisms.

Claim vs. evidence

  • CLAIMED — On the paper’s 18-task primary benchmark, the authors report an average score of 0.886 for Mingbird versus 0.631, 0.479 and 0.405 for three named stock harnesses across four tested small models.
  • CLAIMED — The paper reports a score of 0.856 on a separate 278-task tau-squared comparison using a common user simulator.
  • CLAIMED — The paper’s mechanism counts include 40 finish-gate refusals for missing named artifacts and 34 loop interceptions across 288 published cells; the authors caution that mechanism incidence is not causal attribution.

Strongest baseline

Three named stock harnesses and a separate common-simulator tau-squared comparison.

The caveat that matters

  • Primary benchmark is self-built with one machine and one counted run per cell; task families are correlated.
  • Some mechanisms were developed with the primary task set in the loop, so ablation estimates are in-sample and directional.
  • The external tau-squared comparison has protocol deviations described by the authors and is not a fully independent replication.
  • Scores do not establish broad general intelligence, user-valued work quality or production reliability.

What remains unknown

  • No independent multi-run replication on independently authored tasks.

FCMO Lens

If reproduced on independently designed tasks, artifact-aware stopping and feedback controls could make small local models more useful for practical agent work. The available comparisons do not show broad capability transfer or isolate the effect of individual harness mechanisms.
