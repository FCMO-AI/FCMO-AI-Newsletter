Limitations

  • The evidence is a BigHat-authored report about its collaboration with Anthropic; the reported numerical outcomes have not been independently reproduced in this review.
  • Results cover two antibody families and selected optimized variants; they do not establish broader transfer to other targets, molecules or laboratories.
  • BigHat’s statement that comparable lab cycles take 3–5 times longer is not a matched quantitative comparison in the reviewed source.
  • One Anthropic researcher, Claude, BigHat scientists, automated laboratory equipment, Reccy and iterative assay refinement were combined; no causal ablation isolates the model contribution.
  • Affinity, stability and aggregation measurements are preclinical engineering properties and do not establish drug safety, efficacy or clinical benefit.
  • BigHat and Anthropic report different totals for variants made/tested (1,232 versus about 1,010); their counting basis is unresolved.
  • Anthropic’s October 27 account says BigHat made and tested about 1,010 variants in about ten weeks (lines 40–43); BigHat’s October 27 report says 1,232 antibodies were synthesized and comprehensively tested in ten weeks across seven rounds. Neither reviewed account reconciles the totals or defines a shared counting denominator. Preserve both as partner-reported figures; do not select or average them.
