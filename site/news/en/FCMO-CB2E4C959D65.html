Aleph Alpha releases Kolibri, German-English open-weight model

Aleph Alpha says Kolibri became publicly available on 3 October 2026 as a downloadable Apache 2.0 model focused on German and English. Its model card describes a mixture-of-experts model with 78.1 billion total parameters and 3.46 billion active per token, 1,048,576-token extended context, and a recommendation to use no more than 262,144 tokens for serving efficiency and complex tasks. The company publishes model weights, a technical report and deployment instructions. Performance, serving-cost advantages, compliance sufficiency and sovereignty outcomes remain provider claims; this review did not independently evaluate them.
