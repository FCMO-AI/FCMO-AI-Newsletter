SoftBank completes its USD 30B OpenAI follow-on investment and reports 13% ownership

SoftBank Group says it executed the final USD 10 billion tranche of its previously announced USD 30 billion OpenAI follow-on investment on October 1, 2026, completing the commitment. The company reports cumulative OpenAI investment of USD 64.6 billion and an approximately 13% ownership interest. SoftBank says the final tranche was funded from foreign-currency senior-note proceeds and it canceled the remaining USD 10 billion of an associated bridge facility. This establishes a major investor’s announced capital commitment and financing, not OpenAI’s resulting compute capacity, research spending, or technical progress.
