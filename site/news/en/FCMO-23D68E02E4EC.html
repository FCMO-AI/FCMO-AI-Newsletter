A 66-configuration study reports model–harness ranking reversals across agent tasks

Li et al. report 66 model–harness configurations across TUA-Bench, ALE-CLI and a 63-task non-H100 subset of Terminal-Bench 4. Rankings reverse by harness: on the paper’s Terminal-Bench subset, Claude leads GPT-6 Astra by 7.94 points under OpenHands but trails by 30.16 points under PI. GPT-6 Astra scores 60.32% at $4.66 per task under PI versus 52.38% at $19.94 under DeepSeek Harness. The authors release adapters, evaluation code and 6,204 scored trajectories. These are author-reported results from one counted run per task; harness defaults and effective reasoning budgets differ, so the comparison does not isolate a causal harness effect or establish ranking stability.

Claim vs. evidence

  • CLAIMED — The authors evaluated 66 model–harness configurations across TUA-Bench, ALE-CLI and a 63-task non-H100 Terminal-Bench 4 subset, and report that model rankings vary by harness and task collection.
  • CLAIMED — On the paper’s Terminal-Bench 4 subset, Claude leads GPT-6 Astra by 7.94 points under OpenHands but trails by 30.16 points under PI; GPT-6 Astra scores 60.32% at $4.66 per task with PI and 52.38% at $19.94 with DeepSeek Harness.
  • CLAIMED — The paper releases adapters, evaluation code and 6,204 scored task trajectories, enabling artifact inspection but not independently validating the reported outcomes or billing.

Strongest baseline

Within-paper matched task identifiers compare harnesses for a given model; the comparison does not equalize all harness defaults or reasoning budgets.

The caveat that matters

  • Results are author-reported; the research desk did not independently rerun the benchmarks or validate provider invoices.
  • Each task contributes one counted run after reruns; no run-to-run variance or ranking stability is established.
  • Harness context management, retries, compaction, turn limits and effective reasoning budgets differ; this is not a matched intervention isolating harness causality.
  • The task subsets and reward conventions are local to this campaign and are not direct reproductions of upstream leaderboard results.

What remains unknown

  • Independent matched multi-run reproduction and ranking uncertainty are unavailable.
  • The paper uses harness-specific defaults and high-effort labels that may not imply equal reasoning budgets.

FCMO Lens

The reported reversals show that model-only rankings may not predict the performance or API cost of a deployed agent configuration. The result supports evaluating model, harness and intended task together, while leaving matched-budget causal effects and repeated-run uncertainty unresolved.
