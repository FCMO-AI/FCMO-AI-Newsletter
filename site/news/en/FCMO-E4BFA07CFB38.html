Evidence

  • Demonstrated — Beijing’s Municipal Science and Technology Commission and Zhongguancun Science Park Administrative Committee published a call on 15 October 2026 inviting proposals for Beijing scientific data centers serving AI-enabled research.
  • Demonstrated — The notice says projects may run for up to three years, must connect to the municipal science-data sharing platform, and should provide data-service systems with search, API calls and integrated compute support. It requires company applicants to identify funding sources and provide supporting funds at no less than a 2:1 ratio to municipal science funding.
  • Demonstrated — The official construction requirements specify AI-Ready data-processing pipelines, tools for preprocessing, feature extraction and annotation, multimodal processing and trustworthy evaluation; they also call for traceable and reproducible research-process datasets, tiered data access, and audited security controls. These are project requirements, not demonstrated operating capabilities.
  • Not established — The solicitation and construction requirements do not establish which projects will be selected, final budgets, operational data-center capacity, delivered data access or research outcomes. A sample value in the application template is not an announced grant cap.

Strongest baseline

The call says it implements Beijing’s 2026–2028 AI-for-science plan. It does not quantify current data access or compare expected center capacity with a measured baseline.

Limitations

  • This is a government solicitation and a set of construction requirements, not evidence that any proposal has been selected or that a center is operating.
  • The application form contains sample/placeholding entries; no program grant ceiling or award amount was established.
  • No actual center count, data volume, user access, compute capacity or research outcome is specified by the inspected materials.

What we still don't know

  • No proposal selections, final budgets, operating data centers, delivered data and compute services, or research outcomes were established in the notice or application materials.

Why it matters

The proposal joins AI-ready scientific datasets with research-process traces, reusable access controls, platform integration and compute services. If funded and implemented, that infrastructure could support reproducible research and model training/evaluation across several scientific fields. The solicitation does not establish awards, actual access or research effects.
