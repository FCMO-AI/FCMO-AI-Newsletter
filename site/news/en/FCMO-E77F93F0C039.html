Anthropic revises its Usage Policy with explicit rules for deceptive campaigns and high-risk physical actions

Anthropic’s updated Usage Policy, marked effective November 12, 2026, adds explicit requirements for users connecting its models to hardware capable of high-risk physical actions: a qualified person must be able to observe and stop equipment, which must enter or hold a safe state if the person intervenes or service connectivity is lost. The revision also has a separate prohibition on deceptive campaigns and artificial activity, including fake personas, concealed sponsorship and networks of ostensibly independent sites. Comparison with Anthropic’s archived September 15, 2025 policy confirms the physical-action controls are new to this document. This is a provider usage policy, not a law; enforcement and resulting safety outcomes are unverified.
