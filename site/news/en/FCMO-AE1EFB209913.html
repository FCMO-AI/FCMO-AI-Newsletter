Unisound releases U2-Flash and reports model participation in its training loop

Unisound released U2-Flash on September 15 and lists it on its MaaS platform. The Chinese-language company announcement reports a sparse MoE model with about 266B total parameters and 10B active per token, and publishes coding benchmark, latency and token-use claims. It also says the model contributes to task generation, trajectory analysis and correction, and training-system inspection and repair. Unisound frames this as an initial step toward recursive self-improvement, while stating that changes stay inside human-set sandboxes and verification standards. No independent benchmark, code or model-weight audit was inspected, so the performance and training-loop account remains company-reported.
