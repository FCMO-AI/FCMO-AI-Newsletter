What changed

A September 2026 preprint formalizes token-level credit using three stated regularity conditions and proposes Policy Aligned Critic Training (PACT), which trains the critic after the actor and applies importance-sampling correction. The authors report 72.87% average accuracy across four agentic mathematical-reasoning benchmarks, 8.80 and 13.16 percentage points above GRPO and PPO, respectively; on SWE-bench Verified they report 67.4%, 2.4 points above PPO. These are paper-reported results under the authors’ training and evaluation setup, not independent confirmation of a general post-training improvement.

Evidence

  • Demonstrated — The authors published a paper defining token-level credit through Completeness, Prefix Consistency and Neutrality, and proposing Policy Aligned Critic Training with actor-then-critic updates and importance-sampling correction.
  • Claimed — Across four agentic mathematical-reasoning benchmarks, the authors report 72.87% average accuracy for PACT, 8.80 points above GRPO and 13.16 above PPO.
  • Claimed — On SWE-bench Verified, the authors report a PACT pass rate of 67.4%, 2.4 points above PPO, 2.0 above GRPO and 3.8 above SAO.
  • Demonstrated limitation — The numerical outcomes are reported by the paper’s authors; this record does not establish independent reproduction, equal compute or transfer across model families and training budgets.

Strongest baseline

The paper’s reported comparisons are against GRPO and PPO on the four mathematical benchmarks and against PPO, GRPO and SAO on SWE-bench Verified. Exact training-budget and variance parity require full protocol audit.

Limitations

  • All numeric outcomes in this record are paper-reported and were not independently reproduced in this activation.
  • The abstract provides benchmark deltas but not the complete baseline, compute, seed-variance and evaluator audit needed to determine robustness.
  • Theoretical characterization does not alone establish that the proposed update ordering causes the reported empirical gains.
  • No general result across model families or production training runs is established.

What we still don't know

  • The theoretical and empirical claims have not been independently checked; exact training budgets, variance across seeds and baseline parity need confirmation.
  • The reported results do not establish that critic alignment transfers across model families, task distributions or training budgets.

Why it matters

A principled account of token-level credit and a critic update aligned to the changed policy could affect RL post-training design if the reported gains reproduce across controlled training runs and model families.
