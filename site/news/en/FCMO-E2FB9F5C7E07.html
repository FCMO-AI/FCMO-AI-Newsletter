Fujian reports an AI-integrated public employment service hub operating in Xiamen

Two Fujian provincial government reports describe the Fujian Employment Call Center Xiamen Hub, an AI-integrated public employment-service platform, as online from June 14, 2026. The HRSS report describes text and voice job-search, résumé and policy tools, six dedicated employment-service agents, and handoff to local human service staff. A later Development and Reform Commission report describes the same hub and reports cumulative visits through July 27. The reported usage counts conflict: the July HRSS case gives 19,263 visits in the first week, while the August report gives 17,500 cumulative visits through July 27. Neither source defines the counters or reconciles them. These are government-reported deployment and activity claims; neither report establishes completed job placements, service quality, cost savings, equitable access, or causal employment effects.

Why it matters

If the reported service is operating as described, Xiamen is using AI tools within an end-to-end public employment channel that combines job-search, employer intake, policy assistance and human case routing. This is a meaningful implementation case for AI in labor-market services, but the published reports do not show that the system improves employment outcomes or outperforms existing services.
