OpenAI models and Codex enter Google Cloud’s Model Garden for enterprise customers

OpenAI and Google Cloud announced that OpenAI frontier models and Codex workflows will be offered through Google Cloud’s Model Garden. Google says GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna are entering private preview for selected enterprise customers; OpenAI says general availability is planned in the coming weeks. Purchases will go through Google Cloud Marketplace and may draw down existing GCP spend commitments. The channel could make procurement easier for some current GCP customers, but the announcements provide no model pricing, customer uptake, cost savings, performance comparison or market-share evidence. OpenAI says Microsoft remains its primary cloud partner.

Why it matters

Cloud availability is a distribution and procurement mechanism: enterprises already using Google Cloud can evaluate OpenAI models and Codex through a familiar model catalog and marketplace, with the companies saying purchases can use existing GCP commitments. If widely adopted, this could expand customer access and intensify competition among cloud providers for model-serving workloads. The announcements do not measure adoption, total-cost changes, or effects on cloud and model-provider market shares.
