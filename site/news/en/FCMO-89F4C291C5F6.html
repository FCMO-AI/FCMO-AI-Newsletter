Singapore commits S$30 million to extend ASEAN cyber centre’s AI-risk capacity-building

At the October 14, 2026 ASEAN Ministerial Conference on Cybersecurity, Singapore’s Minister for Digital Development and Information announced a further S$30 million over five years to extend and enhance the ASEAN-Singapore Cybersecurity Centre of Excellence. She said the next phase will prepare participants for incident response, critical-infrastructure protection, AI-related cyber risks, and cyber diplomacy, and will work with industry and academia. This is an official funding commitment and programme announcement; the speech does not establish disbursement, detailed AI-specific programming, or outcomes.
