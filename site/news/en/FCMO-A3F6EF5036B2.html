A preprint separates abstention and noise filtering as two functions of attention gates

An arXiv v2 paper argues that softmax attention lacks two distinct functions: abstaining when no context item is useful and filtering interference within values it reads. The author specifies separate mechanisms and reports matched training comparisons from 10M to 350M parameters in which gating gains shift from mostly abstention at the smallest scale to filtering contributing about as much at the largest; the effects are largely additive. Interference-injection experiments and checks on pretrained models up to 20B are also reported. This is one author-team study; downstream capability gains, production impact and independent reproduction remain unestablished.

Evidence

  • Demonstrated — The arXiv v2 paper defines two functions standard softmax attention lacks: abstention when no key is worth reading and filtering interference within read values. It specifies separate mechanisms for each and explains how a value gate can combine both effects.
  • Claimed — In matched models trained from scratch at 10M–350M parameters with paired seeds, the author reports that gating gains are almost entirely due to abstention at 10M, while filtering contributes about as much as abstention at 350M; the two benefits are largely additive.
  • Claimed — Value-read interference injection tests reportedly show distinct protection from abstention and filtering, and the paper says similar patterns appear in pretrained models up to 20B parameters.
  • Not established — The paper does not establish that the decomposition improves downstream capabilities or production-model performance; independent reproduction was not reviewed.

Strongest baseline

Matched standard softmax attention without a gate or explicit abstention mechanism, trained with the same data order and seed; combined variants are also compared against the sink-logit-only model.

Limitations

  • Single author-team arXiv v2 paper; no independent replication reviewed.
  • From-scratch matched training comparisons cover 10M–350M parameters and three paired seeds per variant; pretrained checks do not constitute independent reproduction.
  • The paper measures training and mechanistic outcomes, not a general downstream capability improvement or production deployment effect.

What we still don't know

  • Training and injection results have not been independently reproduced in this review.
  • The reported mechanistic gains do not establish downstream capability improvements, quality under other training regimes or production-model impact.

Why it matters

The distinction offers a mechanistic way to interpret attention gating: a head may need to choose not to read, and it may separately need to suppress interference in the content it reads. If reproduced, the scale-dependent decomposition could inform attention architecture comparisons. The paper does not establish downstream capability improvements or production impact.
