  • Not established — The inspected paper does not establish broad real-world generalization, production reliability, competitive latency, total inference cost, or independent replication across labs and hardware.

Strongest baseline

The paper compares AMP1 with listed VLA and WAM policies on RoboLab-120 and reports 49.2% and 42.9% respectively; settings are paper-specific and were not independently matched here. On physical trials it reports 49/108 for FLUX 3 Action and 30/108 for pi0.5.

Limitations

  • All benchmark and physical-trial outcomes in this record are paper-reported and not independently reproduced by the research desk.
  • Physical evidence is 108 trials across twelve tasks and three Franka Research 3 instances; it does not establish general deployment performance or reliability across robot types and environments.
  • The paper reports that AMP1 is not yet practical because a robot spends much of each episode waiting for model reasoning; complete task latency and monetary costs were not established here.
  • The authors report benchmark object and verifier artifacts and analyze failures; the degree to which these affect cross-paper comparisons remains an evaluation caveat.
  • The Apple implementation license is custom and third-party benchmark assets have separate terms; this activation did not adopt or execute the code.

What we still don't know

  • No independent reproduction of RoboLab-120, CaP-Gym, memory results or the 108 physical trials was performed.
  • Physical-trial raw data, complete per-trial records, and scripts were not independently inspected in this activation.
  • The paper identifies slow model reasoning but does not provide a complete matched latency and monetary-cost comparison in this review.

Why it matters

If the reported results hold in their stated regimes, a generalist model acting directly in a feedback loop could change robotics-control choices and reduce dependence on task-specific demonstrations or prebuilt skills. The evidence is a single paper and a bounded set of tasks, hardware and models; it does not establish broad deployment, low latency or economic viability.
