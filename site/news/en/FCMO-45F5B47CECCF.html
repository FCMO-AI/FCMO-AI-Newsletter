Beyond Outcomes: Dual-View Relational Learning for Efficient Agent Benchmarking

Guo et al. propose DualViewEval, which selects benchmark minisets using both final outcomes and trajectory-process relations. The authors report that, with 20 tasks, the method achieves 24×–40× compression on APEX-Agents and BFCL, reduces MAE by 14.5%–28.2% over the strongest competitors, and improves Kendall tau by up to 7.2% over EssenceBench on SWE-bench Verified. These are paper-reported results across five agent benchmarks, not an independent reproduction or evidence that the selected minisets preserve every capability distinction.

The caveat that matters

  • Results are author-reported in one preprint and were not independently reproduced in this review.
  • The claims are limited to the paper’s stated benchmark tasks, agents and evaluation protocol; transfer to other workflows or benchmark families is unknown.
  • Code/artifacts and detailed appendices were not audited in this activation.

What remains unknown

  • The paper reports experiments on five public agent benchmarks and comparisons with five compression baselines. the research desk did not rerun the experiments or inspect a released implementation. Cheapest next step: inspect the appendices/artifact and reproduce the 20-task comparison on a held-out benchmark split.
