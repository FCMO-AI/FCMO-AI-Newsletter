Qwen releases Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo checkpoint with a saved 8-step schedule

Qwen released Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo on 9 October 2026 for text-to-image generation and image editing. Its official model card describes an accelerated checkpoint using the same 7B visual-generation architecture as Qwen-Image-2.1, a saved eight-step sampling schedule and prefix KV caching for reuse of text and reference-image context. Qwen also announced hosted Pro and Turbo APIs. The inspected materials report no matched latency, compute-cost or image-quality comparison; the step count alone does not establish a speed or efficiency gain.
