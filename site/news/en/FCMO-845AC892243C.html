Mistral releases improved Mistral Large 4 weights with updated coding and cyber claims

Mistral says it released an improved Mistral Large 4 checkpoint as open weights on Oct 29, following an Oct 6 public preview and three additional weeks of reinforcement learning. The model card lists a 1.05T-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 49B active parameters per token (52B including embeddings and output layers), and the checkpoint is listed on Hugging Face under a modified MIT-style license that requires a separate license for model-as-a-service businesses. Mistral reports updated coding, cybersecurity, finance and browsing results, including 48% on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index with 47% fewer tokens per task than its stated comparison, and 51% on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index. These are vendor-reported results; the full technical report is not yet available and no independent evaluation or local model run was conducted here.

What changed

Mistral says it released an improved Mistral Large 4 checkpoint as open weights on Oct 29, following an Oct 6 public preview and three additional weeks of reinforcement learning. The model card lists a 1.05T-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 49B active parameters per token (52B including embeddings and output layers), and the checkpoint is listed on Hugging Face under a modified MIT-style license that requires a separate license for model-as-a-service businesses. Mistral reports updated coding, cybersecurity, finance and browsing results, including 48% on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index with 47% fewer tokens per task than its stated comparison, and 51% on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index. These are vendor-reported results; the full technical report is not yet available and no independent evaluation or local model run was conducted here.

Evidence

  • Demonstrated — Mistral’s Oct 29 newsroom post announces release of improved Mistral Large 4 weights after an earlier Oct 6 public preview and three additional weeks of reinforcement learning. The linked official Hugging Face checkpoint page is publicly listed; it identifies the checkpoint as mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B.
  • Claimed — Mistral reports that Mistral Large 4 scores 48% on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index while using 47% fewer tokens per task than the leading open-weight comparison cited by Mistral; it reports a score of 41% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, 13 percentage points above the public preview with 30% fewer output tokens.
  • Claimed — Mistral reports a 51% score on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index and a 96.6% average refusal rate on selected malicious-cyber prompts; the linked model card describes the checkpoint as a 1.05T-total-parameter MoE with 49B active parameters per token (52B including embeddings and output layers).
  • Claimed — Mistral says it observed potential uplift for real attacks during monitored live traffic and is modifying its guardrails; it offers a specialized cybersecurity variant to vetted enterprise design partners under contractual safeguards. These are Mistral’s account of its own observations and access policy.
  • Not established — No independent benchmark evaluation, model execution, full training/evaluation report, or production cost measurement was obtained in this review; the reported token reduction does not by itself establish lower wall-clock time, energy use or total cost.

Strongest baseline

For coding-agent efficiency, Mistral reports a 48% Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index score, effectively tied with GLM-5.3, using 47% fewer tokens per task on average. For Terminal-Bench 4.0 it reports 41%, up 13 percentage points versus its public preview with 30% fewer output tokens. These are the vendor’s summaries of benchmark results and are not an independent comparative audit.
