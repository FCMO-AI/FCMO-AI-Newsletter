Anthropic publishes a Claude-attributed subexponential #3-SAT algorithm claim with Lean materials

Anthropic contributed a version-one preprint on October 27 describing a deterministic algorithm that counts satisfying assignments for fixed-width CNF formulas in subexponential time, including #3-SAT, and attributes the core algorithm to an internal Claude research model. Anthropic’s official repository includes the paper and a Lean 4 formalization. GitHub reports that the repository’s exact commit passed its `verify (eth)` CI workflow, which the README describes as a pinned Lean build, Comparator check and nanoda recheck. This repository-hosted result was not independently rerun here. The README explicitly says machine checks do not establish that the formal statement matches the paper; independent mathematical review remains open. The result is asymptotic and does not establish practical SAT performance. An independent external formal-methods audit later reports replaying Anthropic's exact pinned Lean proof with Lean, Comparator and nanoda, a fresh-environment replay, and a Lean bridge from the formal statement to textbook-style ETH in Anthropic's own machine model. The auditor explicitly leaves paper-to-statement and machine-model/input-encoding correspondence to prose and does not certify the paper's mathematical argument. This strengthens the reported reproducibility of the formal artifact, not the truth status of the theorem.
