Anthropic reports 17 scorpion-toxin binder designs meeting lab assay criteria

Anthropic reports that two contract research organizations tested 211 Claude-designed protein candidates against five scorpion neurotoxins and that 17 met its stated binding criteria. Its released dataset assigns seven binders to Cn2, four to AaH II, four to AaH I and two to LqhIII; none met criteria for Ts1. The result is a first-party report with a public assay dataset, not an independent reanalysis. Binding does not establish venom neutralization, safety, antivenom efficacy or clinical benefit.
