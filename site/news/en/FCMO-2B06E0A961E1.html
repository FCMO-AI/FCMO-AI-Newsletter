UK Budget 2026 allocates £40 million to a National Agentic Security Unit

HM Treasury’s Budget 2026 announces £40 million to establish a National Agentic Security Unit. It says the unit will develop capabilities to detect, investigate and respond to rogue or misaligned agentic incidents, integrating monitoring and incident-response functions. The announcement sets out a government plan; it does not establish that the unit is staffed, operational or effective. The Budget also announces adjacent investment in AI security, biosecurity and cyber defence, but those are separate programmes.
