China’s 2026–2030 agriculture plan sets policy directions for AI, datasets and compute

China’s State Council 2026–2030 agricultural and rural modernization plan directs faster agricultural AI innovation and application, expansion of application scenarios, agricultural/rural data infrastructure and large, multimodal, high-quality datasets with data standards. It also calls for domestic-software and hardware development, improved computing capacity and an integrated sky–air–ground agricultural observation network. The Ministry of Agriculture’s page is dated 2026-09-07 and says the online text is abridged; the notice itself states that the State Council issued the plan on 2026-05-26. These are central policy directions, not funded commitments, local deployments or measured effects.
