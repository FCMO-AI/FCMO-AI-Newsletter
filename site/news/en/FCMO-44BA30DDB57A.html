Anthropic opens Claude Duet, a proactive work-agent preview

Anthropic opened a US waitlist research preview of Claude Duet, a standalone and web-based assistant designed to monitor connected work accounts, initiate task updates and continue work in a hosted environment while the user is away. The company says Duet pauses for approval before consequential actions such as sending messages, paying, signing or deleting. The announcement describes more than 6,000 connectors and plugins.
