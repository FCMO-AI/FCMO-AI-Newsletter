Why it matters

Power supply is becoming a design constraint for large compute campuses. This builder-reported milestone shows an early wind segment entering generation in a project intended to serve a major compute base, making the physical build worth tracking. Fourteen turbines are only the first portion of a 75-turbine segment, and the available source does not demonstrate sustained electricity delivery or an economic or reliability effect.
