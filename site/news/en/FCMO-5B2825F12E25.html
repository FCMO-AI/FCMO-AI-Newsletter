Evidence

  • Demonstrated — Anthropic opened an early research preview of the Model Hardware Standard on Aug 27, 2026. Its announcement describes a model-agnostic driver/interface with device discovery, read/write primitives and device metadata, accessible through MCP, CLI or APIs. The standard was not yet open source at announcement and Anthropic said it was working with partners on safety evaluations.
  • Claimed — QuEra reports that, on a dedicated laser testbed, its AI-developed controller recovered on target in 695 of 700 trials (99.3%) across seven disturbance classes, with 100 timed trials per class. Recovery took 0.9–5.4 seconds for wavelength-preserving faults and about 10–14 seconds for the hardest frequency-hop faults. QuEra also reports automatic recovery of all 43 mode hops observed during several weeks of testing in a working lab.
  • Claimed — QuEra says the relock controller was developed with Claude through MHS, but the deployed relock code is a deterministic standalone script with AI out of the loop at runtime. QuEra says the development and validation remained human-orchestrated, within human-set safety bounds, and MHS enforces device-declared bounds, interlocks and emergency stops at the hardware interface.
  • Claimed — In a separate tuning workflow, QuEra reports roughly 12,500 evaluations with no laser mode hops, residual frequency noise reduced fivefold, zero unattended lock drops in a roughly 19-hour soak versus a prior rate of 1.6 per hour, and an independent out-of-loop measurement that found an approximately thousandfold suppression of a hidden resonance near 220 kHz and a linewidth-type metric change from 58 to 21 kHz. The tuning workflow still uses AI at runtime; QuEra describes live-machine deployment as future work.

Strongest baseline

QuEra compares the AI-developed relock controller against its earlier fixed recovery script, which it reports recovered about 58% of cases and took around 150 seconds per attempt. The newer controller is reported at 99.3% across seven injected disturbance classes. The account does not provide an independently audited, trial-level matched comparison or raw logs; it also distinguishes this recovery result from separate in-loop tuning gains.

Limitations

  • Performance figures come from QuEra's first-party case report and have not been independently reproduced or audited in this review.
  • The evidence concerns one dedicated testbed and one laser system; live deployment on QuEra processors is described as future work.
  • The relock controller is AI-developed but runs as a deterministic standalone program with AI out of the loop. It does not establish an autonomous model making live control decisions.
  • The separate tuning workflow remains AI-in-the-loop, is bounded by device safety limits and was conducted with close human orchestration and physical reality checks.
  • QuEra reports a prior fixed script at about 58% and 150 seconds per attempt, but the reviewed account does not provide raw trial-level data or an independently audited matched protocol for comparing the two controllers.
  • Anthropic described MHS as an early preview and said it was collaborating with partners to develop safety evaluations before open-sourcing the standard; the reviewed announcement does not establish open-source availability or cross-vendor conformance.

What we still don't know

  • The QuEra controller, trial counts and tuning results are participant-reported; no independent lab reproduction or audit of raw logs was inspected.
  • The reviewed pages do not provide the MHS specification, driver implementation or relock controller source needed to audit interface semantics and safety enforcement.
  • The pilot does not establish transfer to other laser systems, devices, labs or live processor fleets, and the relock controller runs without AI at runtime.

Why it matters

If independently confirmed, the results suggest a model-assisted development process can produce a fast, bounded controller for a delicate instrument task and reduce overnight expert callouts. The current evidence is one company testbed; the relock script runs without AI, while tuning still uses AI and the safety boundary is implemented below the model. General physical reasoning, safe transfer to other devices and live quantum-processor deployment are not established.
