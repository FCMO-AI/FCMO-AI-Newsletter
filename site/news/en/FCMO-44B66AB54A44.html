Renmin University publishes report of a proposed China-linked international AI cooperation framework

On November 1, Renmin University’s official news site published a China Daily-sourced report about a whitepaper release at the October 31 Beijing AI+ Conference. The report says Renmin University’s Gaoling School of AI and the China-SCO AI Application Cooperation Center were core compilers, with 24 other institutions listed as co-authors. It summarizes a proposed framework combining capability building, application cooperation and governance coordination, with models, compute and corpora described as shared technical infrastructure.
