AMD researchers report a temporally stable one-step diffusion method for global illumination

An AMD research group describes a screen-space global-illumination method that uses a one-step latent diffusion model with scene and lighting conditions, plus a Temporal VAE decoder that reuses reprojected prior-frame information to reduce flicker. The authors train on synthetic Blender Cycles interior scenes and report improved PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS and FovVideoVDP scores over the compared methods on their test scenes; a compact variant is also shown on real photographs using inverse-rendered inputs. The public technical article does not provide a measured runtime figure, and the research desk did not inspect or reproduce the full ECCV paper results.
