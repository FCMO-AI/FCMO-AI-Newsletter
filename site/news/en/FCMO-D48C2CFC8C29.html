Evidence

  • Demonstrated — The European Commission’s 15 October 2026 announcement says it launched the RAISE AI in Science Enablers pledging initiative at the Dublin AI in Science Summit to mobilize private-sector and philanthropic support for European researchers working across AI and science.
  • Claimed — The Commission says the initiative gathers voluntary in-kind contributions, including access to AI models, computing capacity, research software licences, data, training and other resources. It names Novo Nordisk Foundation, NVIDIA, Elsevier, Sanofi, Frontiers, Mistral and Google DeepMind as initial participants; the announcement gives no aggregate valuation or delivery schedule.
  • Claimed — The same announcement says Mistral AI and CERN announced a partnership to develop what the Commission describes as a future leading foundation model for physics. The announcement does not specify model size, compute, datasets, release terms, timeline or performance results.
  • Claimed — The Commission says the initiative will receive an annual review, with commitments and progress to be presented at future European AI in Science Summits. This is a stated monitoring plan, not evidence that contributions have been delivered or used.

These are announced pledges, not verified delivery or use.
