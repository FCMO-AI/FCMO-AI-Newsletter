METR review says Anthropic’s August risk assessment offers limited assurance about monitoring and misalignment

METR published an external review of the misalignment section in Anthropic’s August 2026 internal Risk Report, covering models through 15 July. METR says it agrees the risk was low for those covered models mainly because their capabilities were limited, while it is not confident the report’s methods would reliably identify higher risk. It argues the covert-capability benchmarks provide little assurance about evasion of production monitoring, says later incidents weaken several arguments, and recommends end-to-end monitoring red-teaming and expanding the assessed risk pathways. METR describes this as a relatively lightweight review based only on the August report: the authors were engaged before Anthropic’s 15 October evaluator announcement and did not have the access described there. These are METR’s judgments, not independent measurements of current risk or proof of general monitoring failure.

METR published an external review of the misalignment section in Anthropic’s August 2026 internal Risk Report, covering models through 15 July.
