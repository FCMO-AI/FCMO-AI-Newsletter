China establishes National Data Group as a central enterprise focused on data-resource development and AI support

China announced the Oct 20 establishment of National Data Group Co., Ltd. in Xiongan as a central enterprise whose principal business is the development and use of data resources. The National Data Administration republished Xinhua reporting that the company is intended to help realize the value of data resources, support AI development and expand the data-industry ecosystem, working with relevant government departments, localities and industry.
