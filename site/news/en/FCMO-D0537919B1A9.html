UK and Google DeepMind report early PlanAssist results as live pilot expands to 15 councils

The UK Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government says early PlanAssist testing with Barnet, Camden and Dorset cut officer time on key householder-application assessment tasks by 58%, and says this is helping halve routine processing times from eight weeks to four. It is expanding live testing to 12 more councils for a 12-month phase. Google DeepMind separately reports that officers approved more than 70% of generated reports without edits. These are provider-reported early measures: the sources do not disclose sample sizes, denominators, comparison design or how assessment-task time maps to end-to-end processing time. Planning officers retain review and decision authority; wider availability depends on successful testing.

The present evidence supports a bounded pilot result and expansion, not system-wide processing-time reductions or faster housing delivery.
