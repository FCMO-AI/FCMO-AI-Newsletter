Qwen opens a two-week Qwen4-Max-Preview with multimodal input and a 1M-token context

Qwen’s official Chinese platform documentation lists Qwen4-Max-Preview as a multimodal model with text, image and video input, default deep thinking, tool calling, structured output and compatibility with OpenAI Chat Completions, Responses and Anthropic Messages APIs. The documented context limit is 1M tokens and the maximum output budget is 393,216 tokens shared by reasoning and the final answer. Preview access is Token Plan only, not pay-as-you-go, from Nov 11 through Nov 25, 2026 (UTC+8); the documentation says the preview will be retired afterward. These are provider specifications and availability terms, not measured quality, speed or long-context effectiveness; no comparative evaluation was inspected.
