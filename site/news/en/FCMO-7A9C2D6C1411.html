Anthropic’s Oct 19, 2026 Frontier Safety Roadmap update sets two future policy-publication targets: a proposal on government support for embedded third-party evaluator funding, model access and independence by Dec 15, and a framework on targeting the pace of AI development, including recursive self-improvement risks, by Jan 15, 2027.
