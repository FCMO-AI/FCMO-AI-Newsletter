UK and Canada renew AI security partnership, expanding standards and evaluation cooperation

On 13 October 2026, the UK and Canadian governments announced renewal of their AI security partnership. Their statements prioritize interoperable AI security standards, information sharing on frontier risks and capabilities, regular technical exchanges, and exploration of staff exchanges and coordinated evaluations by the UK AI Security Institute and Canadian AI Safety Institute. The announcement describes intended cooperation; it gives no signed operative text, budget, delivery timetable or evidence that coordinated evaluations have begun.
