Brazilian education council opinion on national AI-use guidelines is homologated

Brazil’s Ministry of Education homologated National Council of Education Opinion CNE/CP 15/2026 on national guidelines for using AI in education; the homologation dispatch appeared in the Federal Official Gazette on Nov 11. The opinion lays out guidance on human oversight of consequential decisions, risk-sensitive safeguards, student-data protection and age-specific use. Its annex contains a project resolution, and the opinion says a corresponding resolution is intended to provide normative effect. The reviewed sources do not establish publication of that standalone resolution, implementation or educational outcomes.
