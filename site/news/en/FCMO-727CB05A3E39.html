Mexico’s Chamber of Deputies docket lists a federal AI governance and technology-transfer bill pending in committee

Mexico’s official SIL docket index lists a Chamber of Deputies initiative presented by José Alejandro Aguilar López (PT) on 15 September 2026 and pending in the originating committee as of that date. Its official summary describes a proposed federal risk-based governance framework for AI development, commercialization and use, and a technology-linkage program to transfer university knowledge to micro, small and medium enterprises. This is a proposal, not enacted law. The full bill text and a later committee status were not directly inspectable in this activation.
