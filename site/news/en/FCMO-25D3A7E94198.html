China creates multi-user renewable-power direct-connection rules that prioritize compute facilities

On 2026-05-20, China's National Development and Reform Commission and National Energy Administration issued Notice 688 establishing a framework for multi-user direct renewable-electricity connections. It defines projects that route renewable generation through dedicated lines and substations to multiple distinct legal entities, with project arrangements for grid-connected or off-grid supply. The notice names compute facilities among priority contexts for green-power demand and sets design, matching, reliability and settlement requirements. This is evidence of the policy framework, not evidence that compute facilities have applied, been approved, received power under the framework or improved their costs or operating performance.

Why it matters

The framework makes direct renewable supply available as a defined multi-user project form and explicitly identifies compute facilities as a priority use case.
