Langfang consults on four linked AI-economy vouchers in a policy draft

On 2026-09-04, Langfang's Development and Reform Commission opened consultation on a draft that proposed four linked AI-economy vouchers: annual caps of RMB 10 million for compute, RMB 4 million for tokens, RMB 2 million for data and RMB 4 million for models. The proposal also describes eligibility and subsidy rules across the four categories. This is a draft consultation notice, not an adopted policy; the reviewed material does not establish implementation, spending or outcomes.
