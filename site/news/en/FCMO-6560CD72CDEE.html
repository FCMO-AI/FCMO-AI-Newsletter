Anthropic reports six METR evaluators onboarded with employee-like access and publication rights

On October 15, 2026, Anthropic reported that it had onboarded six METR employees with company hardware, office space and access to internal tools and systems roughly comparable to those used by its risk-assessment teams. Anthropic says METR may publish analyses of its risk levels, incidents and practices; Anthropic cannot veto publication but may require redactions within narrow predefined categories, and METR may characterize those redactions publicly.
