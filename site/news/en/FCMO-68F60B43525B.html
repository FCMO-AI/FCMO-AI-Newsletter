Anthropic reports a bacteriophage-derived vault structure and engineered nanocapsule

Anthropic reports the first cryo-EM structure of a vault-like particle formed by a bacteriophage protein in a prokaryotic laboratory system. In its 4 November article, the company says its team searched about 15 billion predicted proteins, tested 15 candidates, observed seven vault-like assemblies, and confirmed four by cryo-EM. It describes a 96-copy phage-derived structure and an engineered variant with about 20% more enclosed volume. Anthropic also reports that Claude helped with sequence search, construct design, isolation-method development and microscopy processing under human direction; a purification workflow took about 90 minutes rather than about three days. These are first-party claims. The particles were made in E. coli; their natural function and formation in native hosts remain unknown, and the linked technical report and independent reproduction were not available in this review.
