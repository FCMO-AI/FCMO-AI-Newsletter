California finalizes a long-term insurer solvency planning rule that includes AI among emerging risks

On October 27, 2026, the California Department of Insurance announced that the Commissioner had finalized a Long-Term Solvency Planning Regulation, effective October 26. The department says California-domiciled insurers must assess how climate risks, artificial intelligence, cyber threats, technological disruption and other developing risks and opportunities could affect financial strength, operations, investments and underwriting over time, and provide forward-looking information to regulators. Enhanced requirements apply to insurers writing more than $50 million in direct annual premiums nationwide; the first plans are due January 1, 2028. The linked final text and statement of reasons were not readable in this review, so these details remain attributed to the regulator. No AI-specific control, compliance outcome, market effect or global standard is established.
