MIIT sets national design for inclusive compute services aimed at Chinese SMEs

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology issued a national action notice for inclusive computing services for small and medium-sized enterprises. The notice sets a 2028 goal to cover at least 10 of 15 SME industry classes and directs work on pooled and edge compute, storage and network access, flexible card-hour, core-hour and Token billing, compute banks and marketplaces, and encouragement for local compute, storage and network vouchers. These are official policy directions and targets; the notice does not establish funding, awards, SME usage, or economic effects.
