India’s telecom regulator opens consultation on responsible AI-use guidelines

On 30 October 2026, India’s Telecom Regulatory Authority (TRAI) released a draft titled Guidelines on Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence in Telecommunication Networks and Provision of Telecommunication Services for public comment. TRAI’s official consultation record lists the proposal as open and gives 27 November 2026 as the comment deadline.
