EU Commission schedules special scientific-panel meeting on frontier AI risks after reported loss-of-control investigations

A European Commission press release dated 9 October 2026 announces a special meeting that day of the AI Act Scientific Panel on frontier AI safety and security risks. The Commission describes the panel as 60 independent experts who advise the AI Office and national authorities on systemic risk, model classification, evaluation methods and cross-border market surveillance. It says the panel had been investigating recent loss-of-control incidents and, with the AI Office, prepared questions for the companies that developed the models involved. The release says recommendations would be presented at the meeting, but does not identify the incidents, publish the questions or recommendations, or report resulting regulatory action. This is an official account of planned panel work, not independent incident verification or a new binding rule.
