Simons Foundation, Anthropic and OpenAI launch a $15M program for mathematics in the AI era

The Simons Foundation announced a joint $15 million effort with Anthropic and OpenAI to help mathematicians evaluate AI-assisted results and shape how the field uses AI. The foundation will manage awards, including an open call for workshops and related initiatives; the two labs will provide AI tools and computing resources to supported researchers, including in basic science. This establishes a funding and access program, not evidence that AI-generated mathematics has been validated or that the promised resources have been delivered.
