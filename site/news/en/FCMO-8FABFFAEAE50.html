Google DeepMind pilots cryptographically isolated double-blind frontier-model evaluations

Google DeepMind introduced a double-blind evaluation design for proprietary frontier models in which external evaluations remain inside a cryptographically secured environment so model developers cannot inspect test content before evaluation.

What changed

Google DeepMind introduced a double-blind evaluation design for proprietary frontier models in which external evaluations remain inside a cryptographically secured environment so model developers cannot inspect test content before evaluation.

Evidence

  • Demonstrated — DeepMind reports piloting a double-blind evaluation of a proprietary frontier-class model using a cryptographically isolated environment.

Strongest baseline

Conventional proprietary-model evaluation where developers may know or gain access to evaluation content.

Limitations

  • A single pilot does not establish that all contamination, evaluator leakage or adaptive optimization channels are eliminated.

What we still don't know

  • Cross-lab replication and adversarial review of the protocol are not yet established.

Why it matters

Benchmark contamination and evaluator leakage are increasingly serious at the frontier; a double-blind protocol offers a concrete institutional mechanism for making proprietary-model evaluation more trustworthy.
