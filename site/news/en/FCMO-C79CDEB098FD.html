Why it matters

Model-as-system products can shift the inference tradeoff from choosing one model to coordinating several and synthesizing their outputs. Pareto 26.9 is a bounded commercial example of that design and publishes both wins and losses across a small benchmark slate. Without task-level cost measurements, independent quality evaluation and fuller selection details, this release does not establish a general cost-performance advantage over single models.
