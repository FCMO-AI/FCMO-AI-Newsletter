What actually changed

Semakin et al. test whether language models can rank molecular conformers from 3D coordinates using relative-energy labels. Their benchmark covers 810 geometries from 27 small molecules. Table 1 reports GPT-6 Astra at mean reliable-pair Kendall τ 0.67 and Claude Opus 5 at 0.61, above UFF (0.23) but below MMFF94 (0.93) and GFN-FF (0.72). The result is a bounded author-run preprint benchmark, not evidence that language models replace molecular mechanics or generalize across chemical space.

Claim vs. evidence

  • CLAIMED — The authors evaluate frontier language models on 810 conformer geometries from 27 small organic molecules, using DFT-derived relative energies and three shuffled prompt orders; the scoring set retains reliable conformer pairs with energy separation of at least 2 kcal/mol.
  • CLAIMED — In Table 1, mean reliable-pair Kendall τ is 0.67 for GPT-6 Astra and 0.61 for Claude Opus 5, above UFF at 0.23 but below MMFF94 at 0.93; GFN-FF scores 0.72. These are author-reported results from the stated benchmark.
  • CLAIMED — The authors report stronger model performance on structures with well-defined hydrogen-bond interactions and persistent difficulty on many ring-strain and torsion-dominated cases; the result does not establish general 3D physical understanding or experimentally useful molecular design.
  • DEMONSTRATED — The authors publicly provide benchmark data, raw inputs, precomputed responses and plotting/reproduction code; reproducing model calls requires the documented dependencies and access to relevant model APIs.

Strongest baseline

MMFF94 (mean reliable-pair Kendall τ 0.93), with GFN-FF at 0.72; strongest reported language-model scores are GPT-6 Astra 0.67 and Claude Opus 5 0.61.

The caveat that matters

  • This is an author-run preprint benchmark; no independent reproduction was found or performed in this review.
  • The sample contains only 27 molecules, mostly small QM9 structures, with a restricted conformer-ranking task; broader chemical-space generalization is unmeasured.
  • Only three prompt-order shuffles and one response per model per molecule are reported; API sampling settings vary by provider.
  • Kendall τ ranking agreement is not an energy-error measure and does not establish molecular design or experimental utility.
  • Cross-model correlations with release date and API cost are descriptive; model families and benchmark observations are not independent samples.

What remains unknown

  • No independent reproduction of the reported conformer rankings was located or run.
  • The benchmark is limited to 27 mostly small molecules and does not establish transfer to larger or chemically diverse scaffolds.
  • The single-response-per-model-per-molecule design and API/model-specific sampling settings limit uncertainty estimates and strict cross-model comparability.
  • The ranking task does not establish accurate energies, experimentally validated structures or utility in molecular design.

FCMO Lens

A language model’s ability to rank some small-molecule conformers from coordinates could inform hybrid AI chemistry workflows. The comparison also places the capability in context: in this benchmark, the best tested models trail established force-field baselines, and the 27-molecule regime does not test broad chemical generalization.
