China’s CAC proposes cooperation on intelligent-internet infrastructure and agent interoperability

On 4 November 2026, China’s Cyberspace Administration published a cooperation initiative on intelligent internet development. Its eight proposals address network and compute infrastructure, agent interoperability standards and lifecycle trust, application ecosystems, cross-border data, safety, digital skills and multilateral governance dialogue. The text calls for open, non-discriminatory international standards for agent interoperability and a verifiable, traceable trust framework covering agent discovery, negotiation and execution.
