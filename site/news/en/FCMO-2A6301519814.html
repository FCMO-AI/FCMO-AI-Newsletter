Australia publishes 18 AI-incident recommendations and accepts them for implementation

On November 10, 2026, Australia's Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet published 18 recommendations from a rapid review of government arrangements for AI-driven cyber incidents. The recommendations include considering mandatory, time-bound AI-safety reporting, exploring criminal-law options for serious misconduct, and strengthening inter-agency response and cybersecurity. The Government said it accepts the recommendations and has begun implementation work. The recommendations are not themselves binding law, and the reviewed sources do not establish completed implementation or measured improvements in incident response.
