China opens 2026 green-compute facility recommendations with energy, carbon, water and utilization criteria

A five-agency Chinese notice opens recommendations for 2026 national green-compute facilities. It requires at least one year of stable operation, specified energy-efficiency and green-data-center levels, renewable-electricity share at least the provincial responsibility weight, and measured energy, water and compute-utilization information; ordinary facilities generally need at least 3,000 standard racks and 60% rack occupancy, with stated exceptions. Applications are due 16 November 2026 and the agencies say they will review submissions before publishing a recommended list. These are eligibility and screening rules, not evidence of facility compliance, selection outcomes or improved AI-compute economics.
