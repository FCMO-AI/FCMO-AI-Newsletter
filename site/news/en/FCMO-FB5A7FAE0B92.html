Reuters reports Nippon Life’s ¥2T infrastructure-finance target includes US data centers

Reuters, citing Nikkei Asia, reports that Nippon Life plans to direct ¥2 trillion toward infrastructure project finance, including US data-center construction, with project cash flows used for repayment. The report describes US project finance as offering spreads above 2% on average and says the insurer aims to double its outstanding balance by fiscal 2035. Reuters said it could not independently verify Nikkei’s report and had not immediately reached Nippon Life. The ¥2 trillion figure covers infrastructure finance that includes data centers; it is not established as a data-center-only commitment, realized lending, or completed projects.
