China and the African Union launch a joint mechanism for a 2026–2030 science partnership

China’s Ministry of Science and Technology and the African Union Commission held their first joint science-and-technology cooperation committee meeting in Wuhan on Nov 11, 2026. China’s minister said the committee would jointly implement the China–Africa Science and Technology Partnership Plan Enhancement Action for 2026–2030, including joint research and technology demonstrations.
