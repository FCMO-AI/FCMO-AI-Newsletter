What actually changed

Circuit Condensation repeatedly prunes low-attribution causal edges and trains a LoRA adapter through the surviving graph, accepting a cut only when held-out task performance and a general-capability gate survive. The paper reports substantially smaller faithful circuits than frozen discovery across four behaviors and eight language models.

Claim vs. evidence

  • DEMONSTRATED — The paper reports condensed circuits smaller than frozen EAP-IG in 30 of 32 task-model cells, averaging an 8.1x reduction at comparable accuracy.
  • DEMONSTRATED — Using the same controller without weight updates produced larger circuits in 29 of 32 cells, supporting post-training rather than search alone as the source of most reduction.
  • SPECULATIVE — Training models for causally concentrated behavior will make broad frontier-model capabilities generally interpretable and auditable.
  • DISPUTED — Circuit Condensation directly reduces ordinary model inference cost or checkpoint size.

Strongest baseline

Frozen EAP-IG under the same graph/data/accuracy-selection rule, plus EAP, EAP-GP, random ranking, ACDC where tractable, and a frozen-weight C0 controller using the same ranking, pruning, acceptance and stopping process without LoRA healing.

The caveat that matters

  • The study targets four narrow token-level behaviors rather than broad capabilities such as coding, open-ended reasoning or agent planning.
  • The largest tested model is Qwen3-4B; scaling behavior in frontier MoE or hybrid-attention systems is unknown.
  • The adapted network is not identical internally to the original; output-distribution and error-tracking checks support correspondence but do not prove a unique reconstruction of the original mechanism.
  • The greedy controller does not guarantee a globally minimal circuit, and two task-model cells do not beat the frozen EAP-IG circuit at the seed median.
  • Capability retention is guarded by a held-out perplexity ratio and task accuracy, not an exhaustive broad-capability evaluation.
  • The intervention graph closes edges with counterfactual activations, so circuit-size reduction should not be interpreted as parameter or runtime compression.
  • Docstring completion on Qwen3-0.6B and agreement on GPT-2 are exceptions where the condensed circuit is larger than the frozen EAP-IG circuit at the seed median, showing that not every behavior/model pair is readily condensable.

What remains unknown

  • No independent reproduction is present for circuit-size, faithfulness or capability-retention results.
  • It is unknown whether broad distributed behaviors can be concentrated without unacceptable capability drift or very large audit cost.

FCMO Lens

Most mechanistic-interpretability work treats a model's internal causal organization as fixed and searches for a readable subgraph. This work instead makes legibility a post-training target: computation is deliberately rerouted into a smaller behavior-specific graph that becomes cheap enough for exhaustive subset and pairwise causal checks.
