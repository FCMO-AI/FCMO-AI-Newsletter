Hubei expands its 2026 consumer subsidy basket to include embodied, cooking, exoskeleton and companion robots

On 2026-09-30, Hubei’s Commerce Department issued an effective notice revising the province’s 2026 consumer-goods trade-in subsidy categories from 2026-10-01. The ten-category list now explicitly includes embodied service robots, cooking robots, exoskeletons, companion robots and robot dogs; it also reshapes smart cleaning and kitchen categories and adds cameras/drones, age-support products and smart furniture. The notice says other requirements remain unchanged. This establishes an official eligibility change, not consumer take-up, robot sales, subsidy outlays or incremental demand.
