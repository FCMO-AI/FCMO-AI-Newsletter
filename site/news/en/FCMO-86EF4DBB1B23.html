JERA, Dell and RHAELM plan up to 400 MW of AI data-center infrastructure in Chiba

On 2026-10-01, JERA, Dell Technologies and RHAELM announced an MoU to develop a standardized AI-infrastructure model in Japan. They named JERA’s Chiba thermal power station as the first planned site and said they would develop up to 400,000 kW of AI data-center infrastructure there using an integrated model for power generation, electricity infrastructure, cooling and AI compute. The partners expect total project investment above US$15 billion across land, power infrastructure, facility construction and AI compute, and target operation around 2028. These are plans reported by the counterparties; the announcement does not establish financing close, permits, construction, energized capacity or actual IT load.
