Brazil’s ANPD proposes carrying AI training-data safeguards into its 2027–28 regulatory agenda

Brazil’s data-protection authority ANPD is consulting on its proposed regulatory agenda for 2027–2028. A Portuguese-language draft technical note says the generative-AI and personal-data action would continue with adaptations and identifies issues for further rulemaking: whether legitimate interest can support personal-data use in training, development and operation; safeguards for sensitive data used in training; and transparency, governance and data-subject rights. Public input was listed as open through 2026-10-16. This is a draft planning direction, not an adopted rule or confirmed legal obligation.

What actually changed

Brazil’s data-protection authority ANPD is consulting on its proposed regulatory agenda for 2027–2028. A Portuguese-language draft technical note says the generative-AI and personal-data action would continue with adaptations and identifies issues for further rulemaking: whether legitimate interest can support personal-data use in training, development and operation; safeguards for sensitive data used in training; and transparency, governance and data-subject rights. Public input was listed as open through 2026-10-16. This is a draft planning direction, not an adopted rule or confirmed legal obligation.

Claim vs. evidence

  • DEMONSTRATED — ANPD’s public page states that public input on the proposed 2027–2028 regulatory agenda is open from 2026-09-01 to 2026-10-16.
  • CLAIMED — In a draft technical note for the 2027–2028 agenda, ANPD says the generative-AI and personal-data regulatory action is retained with adaptations and identifies proposed topics: legitimate-interest grounds for personal data used in training, development and operation; safeguards for sensitive training data; and transparency, governance and data-subject rights.

Strongest baseline

The ANPD’s existing 2025–2026 agenda already included AI and personal-data work; the draft describes continuation with adaptations rather than a newly adopted substantive rule.

The caveat that matters

  • The agenda and technical note are draft planning documents under consultation; they do not create a binding requirement.
  • The final agenda may change after public input, and the note supplies no final rule text, implementation schedule or measured compliance/economic impact.
  • ANPD’s reported text-analysis results are agency-reported and do not independently establish the prevalence of AI-related regulatory issues across Brazil.

What remains unknown

  • The final 2027–2028 agenda and the resulting legal instrument, if any, are not yet established.

FCMO Lens

If carried into the final agenda and later rulemaking, the proposal could influence how developers and deployers assess personal data used throughout generative-AI development and operation in Brazil. Its stated topics connect training-data legal basis and sensitive-data safeguards with transparency and individual rights. The open consultation gives affected parties a defined near-term point to shape the regulator’s priorities.
