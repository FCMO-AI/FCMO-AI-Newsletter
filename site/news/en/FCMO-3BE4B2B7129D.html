OpenAI announces acquisition of Pierre Computer Company and its developer tools

OpenAI announced on 4 November that it will acquire Pierre Computer Company, a software company behind Code Storage and developer tools including Trees and Diffs, which OpenAI says are already used in the Codex app. OpenAI says Pierre's open-source projects will remain open-source and Code Storage customers can continue using the service after closing while it works to bring features into Codex. The transaction has not closed and remains subject to customary conditions including regulatory approvals; price, final terms and post-close outcomes were not disclosed.
