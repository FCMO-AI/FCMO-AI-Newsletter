Evidence

  • Demonstrated — Anthropic filed a preliminary Form S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on October 26, 2026, proposing an initial public offering whose share count and price were left incomplete in the reviewed filing.
  • Claimed — The filing reports $11.6 billion in revenue and $1.0 billion in operating income for the three months ended June 30, 2026, versus $787 million in revenue and a $1.2 billion operating loss in the same 2025 quarter; it reports $112.4 billion in cash and $19.3 billion in short-term investments as of June 30, 2026. The six-month 2026 financial figures are unaudited.
  • Claimed — The filing says consumption-based revenue represented 80% of total revenue for the six months ended June 30, 2026, and customer sales through cloud-provider marketplaces represented 48% of total revenue in that period.
  • Claimed — Anthropic describes long-term compute agreements with minimum-purchase or take-or-pay obligations, a move toward leased accelerators in dedicated facilities, and conditional partner financing facilities. It reports capacity of up to $42 billion under a Broadcom arrangement for 2026 lease obligations, up to $30 billion from Google (none issued by June 30, 2026), and up to $20 billion from Amazon; separately, it reports $10.2 billion aggregate principal of notes issued to two accredited investors by the filing date. Maximum facility amounts are not the same as amounts issued, drawn, paid or converted into operational capacity.

It proposes 825,000,000 Class A shares total, of which 801,351,354 would be sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 by existing stockholders, with an expected price range of $70–$78 per share and proposed Nasdaq ticker ANT. Limitations

  • The S-1 is preliminary; the proposed share count and offering price are blank, and the registration had not become effective in the reviewed filing.
  • Six-month 2026 financial figures are unaudited; results are issuer disclosures.
  • The filing attributes much of the reported net loss to non-cash fair-value remeasurement of convertible notes, derivative liabilities and SAFEs; net loss must not be treated as operating cash burn.
  • The filing describes conditional partner facilities and up to amounts that are not equivalent to actual issuance, cash draw, spending or delivered compute.
  • Aggregate long-term compute commitment and payment amounts are blank in reviewed preliminary passages.
  • Cloud-provider marketplace revenue share is not the same as compute-provider share or compute utilization.
  • No evidence reviewed establishes future revenue persistence, total return on compute investment, or the final offering outcome.
  • The Nov. 10 share count and $70–$78 expected range are proposed offering terms; no final price, effective registration, completed sale or realized proceeds are established.

  • The filing provides no supplier-level compute delivered/used, utilization, workload economics or matched unit-cost comparisons.
  • The registration and proposed IPO were not complete in the reviewed preliminary filing.
  • The Nov. 10 roadshow price range and share count are preliminary; the registration statement is not effective and the offering has not closed.

Why it matters

The roadshow gives the first stated share count and expected price range, but neither establishes final valuation or proceeds: registration is not effective and the offering has not closed.
