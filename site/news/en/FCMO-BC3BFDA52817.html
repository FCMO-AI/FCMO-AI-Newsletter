Anthropic launches Cyber Mission for critical infrastructure and open-source security

Anthropic announced a Cyber Mission on October 8 with two initial programs: a Critical Infrastructure Defense Program that pairs frontier Claude models, on-site engineers and threat research with trusted providers, and OSS Scanner, an opt-in service offering periodic model-generated vulnerability reports to open-source projects without human review. Anthropic names 11 founding infrastructure partners and says several are already working with Claude on vulnerabilities and customer fixes. Dragos separately confirms its participation and says it plans to develop detections, engineering guidance and shared knowledge through the program; it reports no completed outcome. It expects OSS Scanner’s true-positive rate to exceed 90%, while acknowledging that reports may contain errors such as incorrect severity ratings. Anthropic says projects must be able to triage the findings and that it will continue human-verified coordinated disclosures for others. These are company-reported program details and expectations; no independent scanner audit, adoption measure, or net risk reduction was inspected.

Claim vs. evidence

  • CLAIMED — Anthropic announced a Critical Infrastructure Defense Program pairing Claude models, on-site engineers and threat research with trusted providers; it named 11 founding partners and says work with several partners is underway.
  • DEMONSTRATED — Anthropic launched an opt-in OSS Scanner that sends periodic, free, model-generated vulnerability reports to enrolled projects without human review; Anthropic says the service is for projects able to triage findings.
  • CLAIMED — Anthropic expects OSS Scanner's true-positive rate to exceed 90%, while acknowledging that some reports may contain errors; this is a provider forecast, not an independently audited rate.

  • CLAIMED — Dragos separately confirms that it joined the Critical Infrastructure Defense Program and says it intends to turn program learning into improved detections, engineering guidance and shared knowledge; no completed program outcome is reported.

Strongest baseline

Anthropic says its earlier Project Glasswing scanned hundreds of projects with humans triaging many findings; OSS Scanner changes this for enrolled projects by sending unreviewed model reports. No matched accuracy or remediation baseline was provided.
