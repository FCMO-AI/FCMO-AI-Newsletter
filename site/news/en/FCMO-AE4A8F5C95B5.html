China's CAC announces a two-month campaign against misleading military content, including AI-generated fabrications

A Chinese-language notice dated October 12 and published by the Cyberspace Administration of China on October 15 announces a two-month, multi-agency campaign against improper marketing and sensationalized military-related content online. The ten listed categories include AI-generated fake military dramas, stories and videos.
