Evidence

  • Claimed — The paper reports that truth-informed planning gains 0.157 mean coverage over support balancing, while a naive estimator recovers 0.005. Selecting among candidate partitions on a separate split gains a mean 0.060 over support balancing, with the direction reproduced in 59 of 60 model effects across three intent-routing datasets and two architectures. Additional application settings include tool calling, content moderation, lesion classification and recommendation.
  • Demonstrated — The arXiv record identifies the paper and its submission/revision metadata. This confirms that the preprint was posted, not that its experimental claims have been independently established.

Strongest baseline

Support balancing, a naive estimator and split-sample candidate partition selection under the paper’s fixed group-order assumptions; reported 0.060 mean-coverage gain is not an independent reproduction.

Limitations

  • All quantitative findings are author-reported; no independent reproduction was inspected.
  • The 0.060 coverage gain is reported for the paper’s candidate-generation and split-selection procedure; the abstract reports direction in 59 of 60 model effects, while individual magnitudes vary.
  • The method fixes a group order; adaptive or unconstrained partition search creates additional selection and multiple-testing concerns.
  • A certificate under the calibration distribution does not establish validity under distribution shift, and a statistical precision label may omit the harm that matters in deployment.

What we still don't know

  • No independent reproduction of the PDF Tables 1–3 or the reported 59/60 direction count was inspected.
  • No deployment-shift test or evaluation of whether the certified outcome matches real harm was inspected.

Why it matters

A safety gate can have strong precision among allowed outputs while being unable to certify some groups or tools at all. Measuring availability alongside precision and coverage helps expose the amount of deployment surface the evidence actually supports, while preserving the distinction between statistical certification and real-world safety.
