China's MIIT sets network-admission and distribution controls for agent-enabled terminals

China’s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology published notice 工信厅信管〔2026〕42号 on 6 November; the notice is dated 24 October. It covers agents preinstalled on phones, tablets and wearables. The original Chinese text calls for compatibility checks and compatible-device lists, risk assessment in network-admission testing, advance plans and ministry review for live over-the-air upgrades, and app-distribution checks that include developer declarations on agent invocation. The notice also refers to the agent-interconnection national-standards series: the SAMR catalogue lists GB/Z 185.2-2026 (identity code) and GB/Z 185.3-2026 (identity management) as current guidance, while Parts 8 (audit) and 9 (transaction) are still listed as drafting projects. This establishes published documents and their catalogue status, not detailed implementation, enforcement or outcomes.
