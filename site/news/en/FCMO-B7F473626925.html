Lean formalization reports global weak-solution existence for unforced 2D Navier–Stokes

A 27 September 2026 preprint reports a Lean 4 formalization of the Galerkin construction for global Leray–Hopf weak solutions of unforced two-dimensional Navier–Stokes on arbitrary bounded open domains with no-slip boundaries. It also states a finite-horizon forced result on rectangles. The linked public repository identifies PDEIdeas.Paper and documents a lake build command. These are author-reported formalization and artifact details; the research desk has not independently built or audited the code.
