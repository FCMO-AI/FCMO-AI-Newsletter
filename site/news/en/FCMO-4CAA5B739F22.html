Lei and Ren detail a Navier–Stokes blowup profile construction with admissible stress and flat remainder

Zhen Lei and Xiao Ren present a detailed construction of smooth axisymmetric self-similar profiles related to OpenAI's proposed forced Navier–Stokes blowup proof. In the arXiv record, v1 was submitted on 28 September and v2 revised on 29 September; the authors describe the work as expository and say it will not be submitted to a journal. They report matching five profile moments, constructing an admissible stress and obtaining a remainder that vanishes to infinite order on fixed similarity sectors. The paper is Part I: it identifies cancellation of the remaining residual by oscillatory pulses as a companion Part II step. It therefore supplies a bounded profile-construction result and an inspectable stage of the argument, not a completed finite-time blowup theorem or resolution of the Clay problem.

Claim vs. evidence

  • DEMONSTRATED — Lei and Ren submitted Part I of a paper describing a self-similar profile construction for forced Navier–Stokes on 28 September 2026; arXiv records a v2 revision dated 29 September 2026.
  • CLAIMED — The authors report smooth axisymmetric profiles whose associated stress and radial shear satisfy an admissible cone condition, with a residual consisting of a stress-divergence term and a remainder flat to infinite order on fixed similarity sectors.
  • CLAIMED — The authors identify cancellation of the residual by oscillatory pulses as the work of a companion Part II; Part I does not itself establish a complete finite-time blowup theorem or resolve the Clay problem.

FCMO Lens

A detailed external construction of a technically central profile component makes a highly consequential proposed proof more inspectable and identifies which remaining step carries the theorem-level burden. If its construction and compatibility conditions withstand specialist review, it could aid evaluation of the broader Navier–Stokes claim.
