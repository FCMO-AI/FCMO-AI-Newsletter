EU Commission adopts a proposed PUE/WUE rating scheme for data centres and opens a separate minimum-standards consultation

On 21 September 2026, the European Commission adopted delegated-regulation text C(2026)3472 final establishing a common data-centre sustainability rating scheme using reported energy and water indicators, including PUE and WUE classes. The text provides for electronic labels generated from operator-reported data, with an annual schedule beginning 15 August 2027. The Commission also opened a separate 12-week consultation on possible minimum performance standards, due 14 December 2026, with a legislative proposal planned for Q2 2027. The inspected regulation PDF still has blank final EU number and Official Journal date fields; scrutiny, entry into force, deployed labels and effects are not established.
