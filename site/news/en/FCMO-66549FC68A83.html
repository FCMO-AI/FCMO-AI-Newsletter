Czech cabinet approves a national AI Council and draft AI Act implementation bill

On 9 November 2026, the Czech Ministry of Industry and Trade said the government approved a proposal to establish a national AI Council and a draft bill implementing the EU AI Act and AI Omnibus. The ministry says the Council would coordinate national AI strategy and cross-government work, while the draft bill would assign supervisory roles and provide for an AI regulatory sandbox. This records the ministry’s announcement of cabinet approval; the formal resolutions and draft bill were not available in this review, and the proposal is not enacted law.
