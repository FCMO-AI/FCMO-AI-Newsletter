Innovate UK opens three conditional BridgeAI AI deployment grant competitions

Innovate UK opened three BridgeAI Deployment Catalyst Phase 1 grant competitions on October 21, 2026, covering decision-critical systems, operational systems, and assurance and compliance. Each competition states up to £5 million in grants, with individual project requests of £100,000–£200,000 for consortia led by an eligible UK AI SME with a deployer partner. Phase 1 is intended to develop deployment use cases, assess readiness and demonstrate solutions in representative settings; it does not fund live deployment. Innovate UK describes a potential three-phase programme of up to £52 million, conditional on UKRI Full Business Case approval and sufficient high-quality applications. No awards or deployment outcomes are established by the calls.
