METR proposes voluntary external assessments of internal frontier AI risks

METR published an October 14 proposal for voluntary external assessments of risks from internal frontier AI development. It proposes public reports based on staff interviews and access to internal datasets and systems on company devices, covering model alignment, monitoring and control, and AI R&D acceleration. Developers would negotiate scope and access and could redact sensitive material; METR says the exercises are not audits or compliance oversight, exclude security audits and pre-deployment evaluations, and cannot provide confident safety assurance. The page says several exercises are ongoing, including an Anthropic assessment, but reports no resulting outcome.
