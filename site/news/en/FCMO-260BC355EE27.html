OpenAI reports engineering-task gains and early industry workflows with AI models

In a 3 November 2026 report, OpenAI says an unnamed newer internal model averaged 0.875 on BenchCAD 2.0, compared with 0.594 for GPT-6 Astra at maximum reasoning effort. BenchCAD 2.0 documents 100 held-out cases across six tasks involving CAD reconstruction, assemblies and PCB connectivity, scored against references without an LLM judge in an offline sandbox.
