OpenAI revises its Model Spec around five values and expanded context and agency guidance

OpenAI published a dated Model Spec revision on Oct 29, 2026. Its announcement says the public behavior guidance is reorganized around five values and adds guidance on context, oversight, agency and side effects while retiring much detailed style guidance. The official changelog lists the notable changes; comparison with the Aug 18 version confirms that “Control and communicate side effects” moved from Root to User authority in the new contents.
