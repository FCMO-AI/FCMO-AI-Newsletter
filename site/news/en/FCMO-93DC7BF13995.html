A Lean-formalized search target for the open unforced Navier–Stokes problem

A September 2026 preprint defines a positive energy defect for a periodic-cube, unforced Leray–Hopf solution at fixed viscosity and reports a Lean 4 formal reduction: the defect is equivalent to a uniform positive lower bound on time-averaged energy flux through all sufficiently fine dyadic Fourier shells. The paper gives necessary admissibility conditions and argues that finite Galerkin computation cannot witness the required uniform ceiling; its 12 pseudo-spectral runs at 128³ and 256³ meet a temporal flux condition but fail to sustain the floor toward the Kolmogorov scale. This is a bounded mathematical search specification, not an unforced singularity result. The authors state that their Lean library contains no Navier–Stokes object and does not connect the reduction to a separately formalized Leray–Hopf theory; the target remains open.
