European Commission opens expression of interest for cellular dual-use drone detection

On 11 November 2026, the European Commission opened an expression of interest, closing 28 February 2027, to collect proposals and information on testing and deploying cellular-based dual-use drone detection. The consultation includes AI-based detection of connected drones, cellular sensing for non-connected drones, and integration with counter-drone response systems. EU Member States are invited to submit proposals with private-sector consortia, and Ukraine may participate. The Commission says it will consider a separate funding call after reviewing submissions; the current notice is not a funding award or deployment.
