Brazil opens consultation on AI ethics guidance for human-subject research

On 15 October 2026, Brazil’s Ministry of Health opened a public consultation, scheduled through 31 March 2027, on three INAEP documents for ethical review of AI use in research with human participants: a guidance document, the MARIAH risk-assessment matrix, and a reference notebook. The Portuguese materials cover health and human/social-science research and recommend context-sensitive, proportionate review; MARIAH supports human committee deliberation and does not automatically approve or reject studies.
