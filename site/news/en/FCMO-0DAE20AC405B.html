Brazil MGI issues advisory AI transparency guides for federal public services

Brazil's Ministry of Management and Innovation in Public Services published two implementation guides for AI transparency in federal public services: an AI System Card for documenting system purposes, processing, limits and risks, and a Transparency Design Guide for notifying users when services use AI. The complete 37-page Design Guide v1.0 scopes recommendations to federal direct, autarchic and foundational administration and says it does not regulate AI uses or replace legal duties. MGI describes the wider self-assessment framework as orienting. Publication is established; agency uptake, binding effect and public-service outcomes are not.
