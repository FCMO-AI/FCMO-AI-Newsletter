OmniFysics-Nano-V2 reports gains on physical-understanding benchmarks

Evidence

  • Claimed — The preprint describes OmniFysics-Nano-V2 as a 4B-parameter omni-modal model trained with explicit static and dynamic physical supervision across image, video, audio, speech and text inputs.
  • Claimed — The authors report scores of 45.09 on FysicsEval prediction, 3.26 on FysicsEval reasoning, 98.27 on FysicsEval understanding, 50.07 on PhysBench, 54.61 on PAI-Bench, 40.82 on QuantiPhy and 59.42 on PhysUniBench. In the paper's comparison table, these exceed the listed Qwen2.5-Omni-7B scores on those benchmarks; results elsewhere in the paper are mixed and do not show universal superiority.
  • Not established — The paper-reported benchmark scores do not establish independent replication, broad physical reasoning, physical-robot competence, or general superiority over larger multimodal models.

Strongest baseline

For the seven physical-understanding scores summarized here, the paper's comparison is Qwen2.5-Omni-7B; baseline and metric details vary by benchmark. The paper reports mixed leaders across general multimodal benchmarks.

Limitations

  • All benchmark values are reported by the authors and were not independently reproduced.
  • The paper reports mixed performance across its general multimodal benchmark suite; the listed gains concern selected physical-understanding benchmarks.
  • Embodied evaluation is simulator/engine-in-the-loop and does not establish physical-robot deployment.
  • No independent code audit, matched external reproduction, or cost validation was performed.

What we still don't know

  • The reported benchmark scores have not been independently reproduced with pinned model, prompt and dataset versions.
  • The embodied results are simulated and do not establish physical robot capability.

Why it matters

A compact omni-modal model with reported gains on physical-understanding tests could make multimodal physical reasoning cheaper to study and deploy. The evidence is benchmark-specific and author-reported; mixed general benchmark results and simulated embodied evaluation constrain what follows.
