OpenAI's revised Raising Concerns Policy adds direct escalation of AI safety concerns to its Foundation board committee

OpenAI's Oct 30 policy says personnel may escalate good-faith AI Safety Concerns directly to the OpenAI Foundation's Safety and Security Committee (SSC), or through the Integrity Line, where Compliance escalates them to the SSC. The policy states the SSC has full authority and any requested resources to address such concerns. It describes this pathway as a channel for input and consideration, not a formal investigation. The published policy establishes a stated process; it does not show that reports were made, that the route has been used, or that decisions or safety outcomes changed.
