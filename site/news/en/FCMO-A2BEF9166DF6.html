ONS reports UK Q3 growth while leaving AI/cloud contribution unquantified

The UK Office for National Statistics’ first estimate puts real GDP growth at 0.7% in 2026 Q3. Information and communication output rose 2.1%, with computer programming and consultancy up 4.4%. ONS says the sector has been the largest driver of GDP growth across 2026 and that many businesses reporting the largest turnover increases are involved in AI- or cloud-related activity. The release says the exact turnover impact is difficult to quantify; it does not measure an AI-specific contribution or establish that AI caused the sector’s growth. The GDP estimate is preliminary and subject to revision.

  • Claimed — ONS says information and communication has been the largest driver of UK GDP growth across 2026 and that many businesses reporting the largest turnover increases are involved in activities related to AI and cloud computing.
