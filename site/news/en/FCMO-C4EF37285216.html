Nscale S-1 reports 461,000 active and contracted GPUs and $103.4B in active and contracted TCV

Nscale's September 18, 2026 SEC registration statement reports that as of August 31 it had approximately 25,000 active GPUs and 461,000 active and contracted GPUs, with $2.6 billion of active and $103.4 billion of active and contracted total contract value (TCV) under long-term take-or-pay customer contracts. The filing defines TCV as revenue contracted over the committed term of signed agreements, excluding optional extensions, renewals and unexercised capacity. These figures provide an issuer-reported denominator for contracted AI infrastructure scale, but they do not establish that contracted GPUs are installed or delivered, or that the stated contract value will be recognized as revenue or collected.

What actually changed

Nscale's September 18, 2026 SEC registration statement reports that as of August 31 it had approximately 25,000 active GPUs and 461,000 active and contracted GPUs, with $2.6 billion of active and $103.4 billion of active and contracted total contract value (TCV) under long-term take-or-pay customer contracts. The filing defines TCV as revenue contracted over the committed term of signed agreements, excluding optional extensions, renewals and unexercised capacity. These figures provide an issuer-reported denominator for contracted AI infrastructure scale, but they do not establish that contracted GPUs are installed or delivered, or that the stated contract value will be recognized as revenue or collected.

Claim vs. evidence

  • CLAIMED — Nscale's SEC filing reports approximately 25,000 active GPUs and 461,000 active and contracted GPUs as of August 31, 2026; active-and-contracted is not synonymous with installed or delivered capacity.
  • CLAIMED — The filing reports $2.6 billion active and $103.4 billion active and contracted TCV under long-term take-or-pay contracts as of August 31, 2026. The issuer defines TCV as contracted revenue over signed agreements' committed terms and excludes optional extensions, renewals and unexercised capacity; the figure is not recognized revenue or cash collected.

Strongest baseline

The filing compares active and active-and-contracted TCV of $2.6B and $103.4B as of August 31, 2026, with $0.5B active and $38.0B active-and-contracted TCV as of December 31, 2025. The filing also reports approximately 25,000 active GPUs against 461,000 active and contracted GPUs.

The caveat that matters

  • The figures are issuer-reported in an SEC registration statement and were not independently audited by this review.
  • 461,000 active and contracted GPUs do not mean 461,000 GPUs were installed, delivered, powered or utilized as of August 31, 2026; the filing separately reports approximately 25,000 active GPUs.
  • $103.4 billion active and contracted TCV is the issuer-defined contract value across committed terms, not recognized revenue, collected cash, profit or a measure of realized compute output.
  • Contract execution depends on financing, site construction, power availability, deployment schedules, customer acceptance, concentration and performance; this review did not reconcile those risks against each contract.

What remains unknown

  • The aggregate filing does not provide a contract-by-contract reconciliation from contracted GPUs and TCV to installed capacity, customer acceptance and recognized revenue.
  • No independent audit of contracted GPU delivery, active capacity, utilization or contract-level performance was examined in this activation.

FCMO Lens

A $103.4B active-and-contracted TCV base and 461,000 active-or-contracted GPU count, if delivered as described, would represent a very large infrastructure commitment and make deployment, financing, customer concentration and contract execution central to the AI compute supply outlook. The filing gives a concrete issuer-reported denominator, while the active-versus-contracted split and the long committed terms require careful separation from live capacity, recognized revenue and realized economics.
