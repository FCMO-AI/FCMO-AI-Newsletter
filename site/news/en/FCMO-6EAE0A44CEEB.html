NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe with multilingual speech tasks and public model artifacts

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe on October 28, 2026, with public model artifacts. Its model card and technical report describe an open-weight offline speech model supporting transcription in 40 languages and additional tasks including speaker attribution, translation and audio question answering. The report specifies BF16 weights and one H100 for its accuracy evaluations, and reports a 4.71% mean WER on nine public English Open ASR datasets. NVIDIA's launch article instead reports 5.33%; the discrepancy remains unexplained. These are company-reported results, not an independent reproduction. NVIDIA's fastest serving figure uses a separate implementation it says is not yet in the open-source release.
