Google DeepMind announces Genie 4 as a multimodal, real-time interactive world-model research preview

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4 on Oct 27, 2026 and said a research preview had begun for trusted testers, with initial access through Project Genie for Google AI Ultra subscribers and early-access partners of the Gemini API and Google AI Studio.
