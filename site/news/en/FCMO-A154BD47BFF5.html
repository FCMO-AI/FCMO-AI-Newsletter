Google releases Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash with 15-second video and conversational editing

Google announced Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash on 5 November 2026 and says it is generally available through the Gemini API and Gemini for business. Google reports native video generation and editing up to 15 seconds, compared with 10 seconds for Omni 1.1, multi-turn conversational editing and a 30% price reduction to $0.07 per second for 720p generation. Google also claims stronger reference adherence and consistency, but the announcement provides no independent comparison. The release identifies continuing limitations for fast sports action, rapid script changes and complex physics; actual billed cost and quality across independent workloads remain unestablished.
