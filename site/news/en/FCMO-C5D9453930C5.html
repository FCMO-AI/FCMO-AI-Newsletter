Anthropic releases Haiku 5.5 with reported computer-use gains and lower per-token prices

Anthropic announced Claude Haiku 5.5 on 7 October 2026 (model ID claude-haiku-5-5) and says it is available on its platform and major cloud providers. The release positions it for high-volume, cost-sensitive and speed-sensitive work. Its benchmark table reports 72.4% on the OSWorld 2.1 offline subset, versus 15.7% for Haiku 4.5 and 48.9% for GPT-6 Luna. Anthropic lists API prices of $0.10/$0.50 per million input/output tokens for prompts up to 100,000 tokens, and $0.50/$2.50 above that threshold. These benchmark comparisons are provider-reported; the linked system card was not inspectable in this activation and no independent evaluation was performed.
