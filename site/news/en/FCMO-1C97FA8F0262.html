Guidance-TTT trains a compact strategic guide while keeping the solution executor frozen

A 5 October 2026 preprint proposes Guidance-TTT, which updates a compact 8B or 14B model to suggest high-level changes while a stronger GLM-5.2 executor remains frozen and implements each proposal as a complete candidate. An executable verifier scores candidates; an archive and PUCT selection supply parents for later attempts. Authors report results on Polyomino Packing, Lasso path optimization, AHC058 and TriMul, including 91.89 on Polyomino, 0.1739 on Lasso, an AHC058 score of 850,082,731, and 1,129 μs TriMul latency. These are author-reported outcomes against heterogeneous prior references, not matched replications. Key model-scale comparisons use one trajectory, implementation failures affect verifier rewards, and full code/data artifacts have not been independently audited here.

Claim vs. evidence

  • CLAIMED — Guidance-TTT updates a compact guidance model that proposes parent-conditioned strategic edits while a stronger GLM-5.2 executor remains frozen and implements complete candidate solutions.
  • CLAIMED — Authors report results on Polyomino Packing (91.89), Lasso (0.1739), AHC058 (850,082,731) and TriMul (1,129 μs), with heterogeneous task-specific baselines.
  • CLAIMED — A separate Polyomino configuration reports about 58% lower service cost when adapting 8B guidance rather than a 120B model, with scores 83.19 and 83.72; this does not establish general cost reduction.
  • DEMONSTRATED — Single trajectories, differing protocols and candidate implementation failures limit conclusions about general search productivity and cost.

Strongest baseline

Task-specific published references including CORAL, TTT-Discover, OpenEvolve, SimpleTES and public eligible Triton leaderboard entries. Their protocols, budgets, hardware and result aggregation are heterogeneous; matched frozen-guidance ablations on Polyomino and TriMul are the most relevant internal controls.

The caveat that matters

  • Baseline protocols, candidate budgets, hardware, and score aggregation differ across tasks; headline comparisons are not uniformly matched.
  • AHC058 reports a single official submission score rather than repeated-run means; TriMul timing is configuration-specific and compares selected eligible Triton entries.
  • Key model-scale comparisons have one trajectory, leaving search randomness as an alternative explanation.
  • Verifier reward combines strategic proposal quality with executor implementation validity; invalid candidates can receive zero reward even when the guidance idea is useful.
  • A separate 58% service-cost result compares one Polyomino configuration and does not establish general economics.
  • No independent reproduction was performed; linked GitHub run-manifest retrieval failed in this bounded review, and external hash-bound payloads remain unaudited.

What remains unknown

  • No independent multi-seed reproduction under matched guidance controls and equal total candidate/API/GPU budget.
  • Full external run archives and hash-bound payloads were not fetched or verified, and full cost accounting against all baselines is incomplete.

FCMO Lens

Separating strategic proposals from full solution implementation could reduce the cost and credit-assignment burden of test-time search: a small model learns which change to try while a stronger fixed model handles implementation. The reported results span four coding/scientific optimization tasks, but mixed baselines, single-trajectory scaling results and invalid executable candidates leave the generality and economics unresolved.
