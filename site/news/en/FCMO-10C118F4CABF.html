Australia announces proposed mandatory frontier-AI protections and a targeted safety regulator

On Nov 10, 2026, the Australian Government announced plans to legislate mandatory AI safety and security protections through Australian AI Standards for frontier AI companies seeking to operate in Australia. It said it would propose a targeted regulator working with the Australian AI Safety Institute and listed obligations covering security controls, risk management, independent evaluation, incident reporting, published frameworks and updated standards. The release says further details and consultation will follow; the proposal is not legislation in force and no implementation or outcome has been established.
