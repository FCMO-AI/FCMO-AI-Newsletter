What changed

Meta authors describe EvoPilot, a human-gated agent workflow for experiments spanning asynchronous training, publishing, serving and evaluation systems. In one 37-day Video Deep Dive campaign, an initial autoresearch comparison appeared to show a 22-point offline hit-rate decline, but review found the control and treatment used different effective output depths. A matched comparison later reported a 3.20-point offline gain for enabling the interaction head, and a separate seven-day randomized online test reported a 0.66% relative increase in the product’s VDD slice of Good Search Result Rate for Retention. The operational analysis includes nine of twenty rounds with complete records; results are reported by the system’s authors and do not establish cross-organization performance.

Evidence

  • Claimed — EvoPilot uses human-approved rounds, isolated workers, a versioned domain skill and typed adapter, durable experiment records, artifact-based comparison checks, and human review to admit a control-treatment comparison.
  • Claimed — In a 37-day Video Deep Dive campaign, the authors found a primitive autoresearch comparison with a reported 22-percentage-point offline hit-rate decline had an effective output-depth mismatch: control K_eff=3000 and treatment K_eff=600, although both declared K=600.
  • Claimed — After correction, a matched-lineage comparison at K=3000 reported a 3.20-percentage-point offline increase when the interaction head was enabled with candidate depth changing from P=3000 to P=6000; the authors state that the head-off control is invariant to P.
  • Claimed — A separate seven-day randomized online experiment reported a statistically significant 0.66% relative increase in the Video Deep Dive slice of Good Search Result Rate for Retention; retained guardrail outcomes were unavailable.
  • Claimed — The longitudinal campaign inventory contained 20 approved rounds, but only nine had complete operational records; paired replay admitted all ten clean fixtures and blocked all ten corresponding mutated faults.

Strongest baseline

For the offline interaction-head comparison, control and treatment use the same trained checkpoint and K=3000; the head-off control uses P=3000 while the head-on treatment rescored candidates at P=6000. The paper states that P does not affect the head-off output. Online, the randomized control and treatment kept the remainder of the serving system fixed; accounts were the assignment and analysis unit.

Limitations

  • The paper is an author-reported case study from one company and retrieval product; it does not establish that EvoPilot generalizes across organizations or experiment systems.
  • Only 9/20 rounds had complete operational records; 11 were excluded from operation-rate denominators, and the earlier campaign lacks a complete attempt denominator.
  • The 10 clean and 10 mutated replay fixtures do not establish field recall or false-block rates; some identity controls were added after the study and evaluated only by replay.
  • Offline confidence intervals, online guardrail outcomes and cross-organization replication are unavailable.
  • The online GSRR result estimates the selected interaction-head treatment, not the causal effect of EvoPilot.

What we still don't know

  • No independent replication or implementation of EvoPilot outside the authors’ Meta retrieval deployment was inspected.
  • The replay tests do not establish how often guards detect real-world invalid comparisons or block valid ones.
  • Offline uncertainty and online guardrail outcomes are absent from the retained evidence described in the paper.

Why it matters

The case shows how a completed model experiment can still support a false conclusion when realized evaluation settings differ from their declared values. Pair-level artifact checks, retained failure records and human approval corrected the comparison in this deployment, while the paper also reports limits in its own controls and records. If repeated elsewhere, this protocol could improve long-horizon model experimentation; one company’s case study cannot establish general effectiveness.
