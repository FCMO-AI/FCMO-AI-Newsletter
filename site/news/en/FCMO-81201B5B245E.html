Datang’s Zhongwei compute campus solar phase is reported operational; wind supply remains unverified

A report hosted by the National Energy Administration’s Northwest Office says China Datang’s 500-MW photovoltaic plant for the Zhongwei Cloud Base entered operation on 2026-05-02. It describes a dedicated-line physical-supply arrangement for incremental data-center load and bilateral power-market transactions for existing load. The article says the separate 1.5-GW wind phase was planned for full grid connection in September 2026 and that 2.29 TWh per year could meet campus demand after the first phase is fully operational.
