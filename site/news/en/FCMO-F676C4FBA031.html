China extends suspension of six export-control measures covering rare earths, batteries and graphite

China’s Ministry of Commerce and General Administration of Customs announced on Nov 9, 2026 that the suspension of six 2025 export-control announcements will continue from Nov 11, 2026 through Jan 10, 2027. The Ministry’s official 2025 announcement index identifies those measures as controls on superhard materials; rare-earth equipment and inputs; medium and heavy rare earths; lithium batteries and artificial-graphite anode materials; certain foreign-produced rare-earth items; and rare-earth technology. The notice documents a time-limited change in the status of these measures. It does not show resulting shipment volumes, licensing outcomes, prices, or effects on AI hardware and compute supply.
