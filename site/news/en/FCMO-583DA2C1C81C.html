SEMV reports improved multimedia verification and safer reuse of corrected cases

The authors introduce SEMV, a multi-agent multimedia verification system that represents evidence as provenance-bearing support and attack arguments, routes human corrections to affected stages, and gates memory reuse through verification and conflict retention. On the 1,700-record COSMOS test set, the Qwen3.6 variant reports 91.88% accuracy versus 89.10% for the strongest directly comparable full-test baseline (unpaired two-proportion z-test p=0.006); other backbones show smaller or nonsignificant differences. In a Qwen memory ablation, unrestricted updates show 5.7% negative transfer, versus 0.2% when verification and conflict retention are added. The authors also report 96.7% correction of initial errors and 52.8% compute savings in a constructed contestation benchmark. These are author-reported results: COSMOS is controlled binary image-caption consistency, MV2026 evaluates only 10 validation and 10 private-test cases, and the 50 CTR episodes come from 10 independent source cases. No independent reproduction was conducted.

Evidence

  • Claimed — On COSMOS full test (n=1,700), the authors report Qwen3.6 SEMV accuracy of 91.88% versus 89.10% for the strongest directly comparable full-test baseline; the paper reports an unpaired two-proportion z-test p=0.006.
  • Claimed — In the Qwen3.6 memory ablation, the authors report negative transfer of 5.7% with unrestricted memory updates and 0.2% after adding verified consolidation and conflict retention.
  • Demonstrated limitation — The paper states COSMOS is controlled binary image-caption consistency; MV2026 includes 10 validation and 10 private-test cases, and CTR has 50 episodes from 10 independent source cases. These evaluations do not establish open-world professional fact-checking effectiveness.

Strongest baseline

For the full COSMOS test, the authors compare against the directly comparable Tran et al. baseline at 89.10%; the paper uses an unpaired two-proportion z-test because baseline record-level predictions are unavailable.

Limitations

  • Author-reported preprint results; no independent reproduction was conducted.
  • COSMOS is a controlled binary image-caption consistency benchmark, not open-world fact-checking.
  • MV2026 has 10 validation and 10 private-test cases; CTR has 50 episodes derived from 10 independent source cases.
  • The compared improvements vary across backbones; the Gemma4 accuracy difference is not statistically significant in the paper's test.

What we still don't know

  • No independent reproduction or audit of the author-run COSMOS results and memory ablation was performed.
  • Generalization to open-world professional verification and independently adjudicated longitudinal corrections is not established.

Why it matters

Evidence provenance, localized correction and safe reuse of prior cases are central to dependable multimedia verification. SEMV offers a concrete integrated design and reports controlled gains, while its small open-world and contestation evaluations leave effectiveness in professional fact-checking uncertain.
