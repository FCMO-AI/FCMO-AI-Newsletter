China publishes GB/Z 290-2026 guidance on AI-generated content in academic publishing

China’s State Administration for Market Regulation and Standardization Administration announced on 4 November 2026 that they had approved 37 national standardization guidance technical documents, including GB/Z 290-2026, titled “Guidance on boundaries for use of AI-generated/synthetic content in academic publishing.” The National Standard Information Public Service Platform lists the document as current and gives its publication date as 30 October 2026. The announcement and catalogue establish the document’s issuance, title and status; the text’s provisions, adoption and effects were not assessed.
