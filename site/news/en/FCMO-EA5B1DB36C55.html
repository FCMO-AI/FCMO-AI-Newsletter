UK announces a multi-part AI security, capability and economic-research package

On 24 October 2026, HM Treasury announced a multi-part UK AI programme spanning security, sovereign capability and economic research. The release lists £165 million for a National AI Biosecurity Initiative (including a £100 million AI Biosecurity Lab and £65 million for biosurveillance and medical countermeasures), an additional £120 million for the AI Security Institute, £60 million for a National Cyber Shield and £70 million for a National Exposure and Real-Time Vulnerability Centre. It also lists additional support for the Sovereign AI Fund and its R&D Procurement Scheme, chip-design training, an AI Economics Institute and local partnerships. OpenAI and Anthropic are said to offer up to £200 million in AI-model credits for the Biosecurity Lab; this is separate from government funding. The release records announcements and stated plans, not completed appropriations, delivery, operational capacity or measured effects; it says the broader package will be announced with the following week’s Budget. A same-day Cabinet Office release further says the Lab is planned as an independent nonprofit with £100 million in government funding and up to £200 million in company commitments, largely compute/model credits, trusted access and technical support; its operating terms and delivery remain unestablished. The Cabinet Office also says the planned nonprofit will have a scientific advisory committee, with further details to follow, and identifies planned work with UKHSA, APHA, Dstl and MHRA.
