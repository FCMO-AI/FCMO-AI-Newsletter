India’s ESTIC 2026 draft vision reports Physical AI targets through 2047

On 29 October 2026, the Press Information Bureau reported the release of the National Vision Document for India’s Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave (ESTIC) 2026. Its account of the Department of Science and Technology’s autonomous-systems, Physical AI and robotics session describes recommendations to strengthen 25 National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems technology hubs for the mission’s next phase beginning in January 2028, build testbeds and India-specific datasets, support indigenous hardware with patient capital, and emphasize safety-by-design, hands-on training, early customer engagement and viable business models. PIB calls the proposed milestones a draft vision: pilot validation by 2030, wider deployment and manufacturing by 2035, and global competitiveness by 2047. A separate PIB release says stakeholder consultations had considered a possible Physical AI Mission. These sources do not establish an approved or funded mission, implementation owners, or delivered results; the full vision PDF was not located in this review.
