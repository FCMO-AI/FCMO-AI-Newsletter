Japan’s energy-conservation regime adds data-centre reporting and a PUE threshold for eligible new builds

Japan’s Agency for Natural Resources and Energy (ENECHO) describes additional Energy Conservation Act measures for data-centre operators, including reporting and public disclosure from FY2026 submissions and a PUE≤1.3 standard for eligible new sites from FY2029, assessed after two operating years. What actually changed

Japan’s Agency for Natural Resources and Energy (ENECHO) describes additional Energy Conservation Act measures for data-centre operators, including reporting and public disclosure from FY2026 submissions and a PUE≤1.3 standard for eligible new sites from FY2029, assessed after two operating years. Its guidance lists scope thresholds and exclusions. This is an official policy design and reporting obligation; it does not show facility compliance, achieved efficiency, capacity, or AI workload costs.

Claim vs. evidence

  • CLAIMED — ENECHO’s official guidance and FAQ describe FY2026 data-centre reporting and a PUE≤1.3 condition for eligible new sites from FY2029 after two operating years.
  • DEMONSTRATED — The reviewed sources provide policy requirements and reporting descriptions, not measured compliance, achieved efficiency, compute capacity or economic outcomes.

Strongest baseline

Before this change, data-centre operators were subject to general energy reporting and a weighted operator-average PUE≤1.4 target for FY2030; the guidance adds facility-level reporting and a separate threshold for eligible new sites.

The caveat that matters

  • The full operative texts of Ordinance No.81 (26 Dec 2025) and No.59 (1 Jul 2026), plus the separate benchmark notification, were not compared line-by-line with the guidance.
  • The ENECHO guideline PDF tail was not retrieved; only its first 1,000,000 of 1,219,854 bytes had been inspected in the prior review.
  • No FY2026 report, compliance, enforcement, achieved PUE, electricity use, project cost, grid/water effect or AI workload economics was inspected. These are not prerequisites for the bounded policy-description claim.

What remains unknown

  • Exact operative text of the amended ordinances and benchmark notification has not been fully compared with ENECHO guidance.
  • No facility compliance, achieved PUE, energy, cost, or compute impact data are available in the reviewed sources.

FCMO Lens

Data-centre energy reporting and a future efficiency condition can affect design, site economics and operating choices for a subset of Japanese compute infrastructure. The public record gives operators several years to adapt, while facility-level costs and effects remain unmeasured.
