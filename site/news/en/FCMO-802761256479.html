Huawei chair says domestic AI-compute demand exceeds supply, limiting full overseas expansion

At Huawei Connect in Shanghai on September 17, rotating chairman Eric Xu told Reuters that Huawei could not produce enough AI-computing equipment to meet Chinese demand and was not planning a fully fledged international expansion. Reuters also reports Xu said Huawei supplies a limited number of countries where demand is particularly strong. These are attributed company statements, not independently measured production, demand, shipments or export volumes. Reuters reported that Huawei provided no data for Xu’s separate claim that Ascend had surpassed Nvidia’s China market share; the research desk excludes that unsupported market-share claim. The reported constraint bears on near-term availability of China-developed AI compute, but its magnitude, duration and cause are not established.
