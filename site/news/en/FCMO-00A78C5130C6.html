Baidu releases Unlimited OCR 2 with open weights and long-document parsing claims

Baidu announced the October 27 release of Unlimited OCR 2 and made model weights and code available through its official repository. The repository describes sparse reference sliding-window attention (SR-SWA), which limits text-visual attention to a fixed working-reference set, and includes an evaluation runner for MPDocBench and MarathonDocBench. Baidu reports scores of 84.6 and 77.1 on those benchmarks and says the model parsed more than 3,600 pages in one request. These are vendor-reported results; this review did not reproduce them or audit the full benchmark protocol.
