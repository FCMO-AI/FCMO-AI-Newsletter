China issues an eight-ministry framework to expand AI products, services and consumption scenarios

China’s Ministry of Commerce and seven other departments issued an implementation opinion for “AI + consumption.” Its 17 measures cover consumer AI products and robots, home and elder-care services, tourism, hospitality and education, retail, e-commerce, logistics, demonstration clusters, an application-scene guide/open list, fiscal and consumer-credit support, infrastructure, standards and safety. The MOFCOM page marks the opinion currently effective. This is evidence of official policy intent and specified measures; it does not establish appropriations, deployment, consumer uptake, subsidy redemption or economic effects.

Strongest baseline

The MOFCOM document itself is the primary evidence for policy issuance and stated measures; it contains no outcome baseline.

The caveat that matters

  • The opinion establishes policy intent and assigned measures, not their completion or enforcement.

What remains unknown
