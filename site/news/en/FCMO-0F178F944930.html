Anthropic and Accenture announce a directly funded embedded evaluation partnership

On Sept. 18, 2026, Anthropic announced a non-exclusive partnership with Accenture’s Faculty business to evaluate and red-team models, assess alignment, and test safeguards. Each organization said it expects to invest at least $1 billion over five years to build capacity. Evaluators are intended to work inside AI companies with access comparable to employees, including observation of training and deployment decisions and direct staff interviews. Anthropic said access and reporting standards, and a settled funding model for independent evaluation, do not yet exist; it will directly fund this partnership while exploring pilots with nonprofit evaluators. This is evidence of an announced organizational model and planned investment, not evidence that evaluations are operating or independent in practice.
