Evidence

  • Demonstrated — Insilico Medicine announced on 6 November 2026 an open-source release called Longevity Grok / Personal Longevity Superintelligence, developed for the Grok Bot platform.
  • Claimed — The company describes a multi-agent system for organizing longitudinal health records, predicting biomarker changes after proposed lifestyle interventions, scoring follow-up outcomes, assisting physicians with structured context, and supporting opt-in communal aging research. It says the system coordinates up to 30 specialized agents.
