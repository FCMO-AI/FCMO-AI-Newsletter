Apple announces a staged China rollout of Apple Intelligence with selected Alibaba Qwen features

Apple's mainland China Newsroom says selected Apple Intelligence features will enter the latest iOS developer beta from Nov 4 and ship to users with iOS 27.3 in December. Apple says Apple-built generative models power the initial feature set and that Alibaba's Qwen will be available in selected Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence workflows for text and image understanding and content creation. The announcement excludes the next-generation Siri features shown at WWDC 2026, and says support on other platforms depends on necessary approvals. It is a prospective product/distribution announcement, not evidence that the service is already live or that Qwen powers all Apple Intelligence functions.
