What changed

Nanbeige LLM Lab has published Nanbeige4.3-33B as a 33B BF16 model under Apache 2.0 and positions it as a teacher for a planned smaller model. Its model card reports results across coding, workplace-agent and reasoning benchmarks, using the same stated inference hyperparameters and harness per benchmark across listed models. The table is mixed: Nanbeige leads on many listed tasks but trails a comparator on SWE-Bench Pro and GDPval, and trails Gemma4 on GPQA Diamond. These are lab-reported results, not independent measurements.

Evidence

  • Demonstrated — The Nanbeige LLM Lab Hugging Face repository exposes a 33B-parameter BF16 checkpoint with an Apache 2.0 license and instructions for Transformers, vLLM and SGLang.
  • Claimed — Nanbeige reports that, at its highest thinking effort and using the same stated inference hyperparameters and harness for each benchmark, Nanbeige4.3-33B scores 80.9 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, 60.3 on SWE-Bench Pro, 84.8 on GDPval and 89.5 on GPQA Diamond. The same table shows it below Qwen3.8-27B on SWE-Bench Pro (61.7) and below Gemma4-31B on GPQA Diamond (91.3).
  • Claimed — Nanbeige describes the 33B model as a teacher intended to support distillation into smaller models, including a planned 3B model.

Strongest baseline

The model card compares Qwen3.8-27B, Muse-Glimmer-30B and Gemma4-31B, but evaluation scripts and all model-specific settings are not fully exposed in the inspected page.

Limitations

  • Benchmark scores and the comparative claim are reported by the model developer; no independent matched reproduction was located in this review.
  • The card states shared inference hyperparameters and harness per benchmark but does not provide all runnable evaluation code, raw outputs or complete model-specific settings in the inspected page. It names different harnesses and judge methods in footnotes, including GPT-6 Sol judging SWE-Atlas-QnA and Claude Code for NL2Repo.
  • Results are not uniformly best: the reported SWE-Bench Pro score trails Qwen3.8-27B, GDPval trails Qwen3.8-27B, and GPQA Diamond trails Gemma4-31B.
  • A 33B BF16 checkpoint is a substantial deployment artifact; the card’s cost-effective framing is not accompanied here by measured serving cost, latency, energy use or hardware requirements.
  • The official Hugging Face model-repository history lists the initial commit at 2026-10-24T02:49:18Z; this establishes repository availability, not necessarily a separate announcement timestamp. The release therefore predates the activation’s approximate 36-hour freshness cutoff.
  • The developer’s own table reports Nanbeige4.3-33B below Qwen3.8-27B on SWE-Bench Pro (60.3 vs 61.7) and GDPval (84.8 vs 86.4), and below Gemma4-31B on GPQA Diamond (89.5 vs 91.3).

What we still don't know

  • No independent matched evaluation or reproduction of the model-card benchmark results was located during this review.
  • The model card does not expose all benchmark code, task-level outputs or complete model-specific inference settings needed to audit every comparison.
  • No measured serving cost, latency, energy use or hardware requirement was found to substantiate the card’s cost-efficient framing.

Why it matters

An openly downloadable 33B agent model with a published comparison profile may broaden the set of systems researchers and developers can inspect and run. The card’s mixed results make task-level comparisons more informative than its broad superiority framing; independent matched evaluation is still needed.
