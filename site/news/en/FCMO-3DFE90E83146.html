China’s labor-skills center issues a cross-sector digital-intelligence capability framework

China’s Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security Vocational Skills Appraisal Center issued a General Digital Intelligence Capability Framework on Nov 3, 2026, announced in a Nov 9 notice. The center says it is intended as a reference for skills training and assessment, curricula, vocational-school teaching, public employment services and employer skill standards, for audiences including job seekers, students, workers, job changers and flexible workers. The notice says it has six domains and 22 units and responds to rapid AI and digital change. The linked framework contents were not retrieved, and no adoption or employment outcome is established.
