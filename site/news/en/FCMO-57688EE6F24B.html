China sets cross-sector science and AI-plus priorities in a top-level productivity policy Opinion

An October 9 Opinion issued by the Communist Party of China Central Committee and State Council sets 19 tasks for developing new-quality productive forces. It calls for AI to change research methods, AI-plus deployment in traditional industries and new terminals, stronger compute/algorithm/data supply, application pilots, and monitoring and governance for safe, reliable and controllable AI. These are adopted policy directions; the inspected release gives no implementation results or complete budget and schedule for the AI measures.
