Data4 and EDF announce a long-term nuclear electricity allocation for French data centers

EDF and Data4 announced a 12-year Nuclear Production Allocation Contract (CAPN) for Data4’s French data centers. EDF’s French release says the agreement allocates Data4 a 40 MW share of EDF’s operating nuclear fleet, with costs and production-volume risks shared, and forecasts first deliveries from 2026 at about 230 GWh per year. Data4 describes it as the first CAPN signed by a French data-center operator. The sources establish the announced contract terms, not actual Data4 deliveries, metered volumes, the contract tariff, or allocation to AI workloads.
