Argentina requires additional energy and firm power for large AI data-center loads

Resolution 264/2026 establishes XDEM rules for large new or expanded loads. XDEM-DATOS requires 80% monthly energy coverage and normally 115% firm-power coverage.

What actually changed

Resolution 264/2026 establishes XDEM rules for large new or expanded loads. XDEM-DATOS requires 80% monthly energy coverage and normally 115% firm-power coverage.

Claim vs. evidence

  • DEMONSTRATED — Monthly eligible energy coverage is at least 80%.
  • DEMONSTRATED — Data-load firm-power coverage is normally 115%.

Strongest baseline

General large-user rules are supplemented by XDEM-specific obligations.

The caveat that matters

  • Exporting grid areas may receive a justified firm-power exception.
  • Eligible supply can include qualifying existing gas-generation expansion.
  • Registration alone does not authorize operation.

What remains unknown

  • Implementation outcomes unknown.
