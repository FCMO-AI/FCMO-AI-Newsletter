Canada announces a staged equity investment of up to C$1.6 billion in Cohere

On Oct 29, 2026, Canada announced a planned commercial equity investment of up to C$1.6 billion in Cohere, to be made in stages alongside private investors. The government release says Cohere committed to at least 1,000 new Canadian technical jobs and C$1.7 billion in additional Canadian compute infrastructure by 2030. These are announced future terms; the release does not establish disbursement, completed hiring or delivered compute. A prior C$725 million Cohere/CoreWeave compute project is separately documented, and public records reviewed do not reconcile any overlap between the new job and compute commitments and Cohere’s Oct 30 aggregate plan.
