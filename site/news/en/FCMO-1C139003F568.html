Beijing seeks proposals for disruptive AI technology projects

A 23 October notice from the Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission and Zhongguancun Administrative Committee solicits reserve projects across three AI technology tracks: inference chips, disruptive algorithms and frontier large-model technology. The chip track seeks an integrated chip/software/model-adaptation solution validated by at least two real user organizations. The algorithm track seeks a new architecture tested at 10,000,000,000 or more parameters against a best same-scale Transformer baseline. A third track targets adaptive or self-improving large-model systems. Beijing had already solicited a non-Transformer architecture at this scale in 2025, and the enterprise 2:1 matching condition also appeared in that call. The new notice adds a two-user chip-validation requirement and a distinct adaptive large-model track. It states research goals and solicitation rules, not awarded funding, built systems or measured technical results.
