FARMS links source-contextual Lean statements to bounded numerical skills

FARMS is an author-reported pipeline that links mathematical source context and computational meaning to Lean statements, then uses semantic review, bounded repair and abstention alongside compilation. In 171 selected randomized-linear-algebra and numerical-PDE targets, the authors report 157 accepted outcomes, including three with source warnings. Two small numerical applications use selected statements in heat-flow solving and wave-response compression. A six-target comparison reports lower per-target costs than the authors’ statement-level AutoformBot adaptation, but that comparison allowed sorry for target and auxiliary proofs and is restricted to this configuration. The paper does not establish independent replication, broad domain transfer, proof-complete corpus coverage or automated discovery of new methods.

Evidence

  • Demonstrated — arXiv identifies FARMS as a v1 paper submitted by Di Wu, Ling Liang and Haizhao Yang on 2026-10-29.
  • Claimed — Across 171 selected targets from four sources in randomized numerical linear algebra and numerical PDEs, the authors report 157 accepted pipeline outcomes: 154 without source warnings and three with explicit source warnings. Acceptance means VERIFIED_PASS or COMPILED_WITH_SOURCE_WARNING; it is not an independent judgment that every source theorem or translation is correct.
  • Claimed — For six randomly selected targets, the authors’ statement-level AutoformBot adaptation reports $2.84 per target for FARMS versus $58.02 for AutoformBot. The comparison restricted both systems to the selected statements, allowed sorry in target and auxiliary proofs for AutoformBot, and required execution/interface changes; it is not a general proof-complete cost comparison.
  • Claimed — The authors demonstrate two bounded applications using source-linked Lean statements and connecting proofs: six prescribed heat-flow runs met a discrete energy-error target of 10^-6, while three prescribed wave-response seeds reduced a 32-time response library from 24 MiB to 8.375 MiB under a stated 0.01 energy-error bound for a fixed excitation family.
  • Demonstrated — The paper describes separate source-context review, Lean compilation, semantic review, bounded repair and abstention. Its worked examples report recovery of omitted mathematical conditions and detection of a source optimality claim missing a positive-sampling assumption.
  • Not established — The paper does not establish independent reproducibility, broad transfer across mathematical disciplines or recent research literature, proof-complete formalization at the reported pipeline acceptance rate, or autonomous discovery of new numerical methods. The authors identify wider source coverage, cross-textbook integration and new-method discovery as open work.

Strongest baseline

The six-target cost comparison uses an author-adapted AutoformBot statement-level workflow with the same source documents, but allows sorry for target and auxiliary proofs and includes execution/model-interface changes. It does not establish a broad matched comparison against proof-complete formalization.

Limitations

  • All quantitative outcomes are reported by the paper authors; no independent reproduction or Lean build was performed.
  • The corpus covers 171 selected statements from four sources in randomized numerical linear algebra and numerical PDEs, not broad scientific disciplines or contemporary research literature.
  • The 157 accepted outcomes include three source-warning cases; acceptance may be VERIFIED_PASS or COMPILED_WITH_SOURCE_WARNING and is not equivalent to independent validation of the source theorem.
  • The six-target AutoformBot comparison is a constrained statement-level adaptation permitting sorry in target and auxiliary proofs; it is not a proof-complete or general cost comparison.
  • The heat-flow and wave-response applications are bounded, author-run examples on prescribed numerical problems and seed sets; general scientific-agent utility and discovery of new methods remain open.

What we still don't know

  • This activation did not inspect the paper-linked repository, pin a build environment or independently compile selected statements and connecting proofs.
  • Transfer beyond four sources/two mathematical areas and the six-target adapted AutoformBot cost comparison are not independently established.

Why it matters

If extended and independently checked, source-contextual formal representations that preserve applicability conditions and quantitative guarantees could help numerical agents reuse mathematical methods more safely. The current evidence is a single paper across four sources in two areas, with two bounded applications and author-reported costs.
