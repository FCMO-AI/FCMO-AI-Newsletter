FTC proposes a Section 5 theory for undisclosed AI output steering

On July 1, 2026, the Federal Trade Commission issued a proposed policy statement and requested comments by July 31 on how Section 5 deception principles apply when AI companies steer outputs away from what consumers reasonably expect without adequate disclosure. The proposal argues that undisclosed ideological output steering may deceive consumers and that state requirements that compel conflicting output changes may be impliedly preempted. A separate Colorado law addresses automated decision systems used for consequential decisions; the inspected statutory clauses do not themselves establish the output-steering premise described by the FTC. The proposal is not binding law or an adjudicated federal-state conflict. An October review found the proposal still listed on FTC pages but did not inspect the docket comments or establish a final disposition.
