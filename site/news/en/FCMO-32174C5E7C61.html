EU final text establishes a common sustainability rating scheme for data centres

The European Commission’s final delegated-regulation text C(2026)3472 establishes a common Union sustainability rating scheme for data centres. It provides for electronic labels generated from indicators already reported through the European database, presenting energy and sustainability information to support comparison and transparency. The text describes operator obligations and review provisions while aiming to avoid additional reporting burden by reusing existing data. This establishes the scheme’s design, not measured efficiency gains, changed procurement, or effects on AI compute capacity; publication and implementation status need further checking.
