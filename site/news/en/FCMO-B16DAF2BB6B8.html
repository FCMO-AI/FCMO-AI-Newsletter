GAE reports improved 3D consistency when generation uses geometry-native latent features

The paper proposes GAE, a compact latent derived from geometry-foundation-model features and jointly decodable to RGB, depth, camera parameters and point maps. A conditional flow model generates in that shared representation. In controlled comparisons holding generator and training protocol fixed, authors report FVD reductions of 12.7% on RealEstate10K and 23.1% on DL3DV, and half the camera-trajectory error on RealEstate10K. The paper also reports gains from metric pose scale, clean reference conditioning and representation-shaping ablations. These are author-reported results on two video datasets; no independent reproduction was performed. The measurements concern generated-view quality and geometry consistency, not physical simulation fidelity or downstream planning and control.

Evidence

  • Claimed — GAE reparameterizes geometry-foundation-model features into a compact latent that can be decoded jointly to RGB appearance, depth, cameras and point maps; a conditional flow model generates from this shared state.
  • Claimed — In controlled comparisons holding generator and training protocol fixed, the authors report FVD reductions of 12.7% on RealEstate10K and 23.1% on DL3DV, with camera-trajectory error halved on RealEstate10K versus tested latent representations.
  • Claimed — The authors report ablations in which metric pose scale, clean reference conditioning, representation-shaping losses and text-to-image co-training each improve selected appearance or geometry metrics.
  • Not established — The reported image/video metrics do not establish physical-world simulation fidelity, reliable long-horizon world modeling, or downstream planning and control benefits.

Strongest baseline

The matched-generator alternatives use pixel, semantic or raw-geometry latent representations with the same training protocol. Reported FVD reductions and camera-trajectory error apply to the specified datasets and setup, not all world-generation regimes.

Limitations

  • Results are author-reported in one preprint; no independent reproduction was located or run.
  • The quantitative comparisons cover RealEstate10K and DL3DV under the paper's generator and training protocol.
  • An 81-view rollout and text-conditioned outputs are qualitative capability demonstrations, not controlled evidence of long-horizon accuracy.
  • Image/video and geometry metrics do not establish physical simulation fidelity or benefits for downstream planning and control.
  • Ablation gains are component-specific and do not isolate the value of GAE in other architectures or data regimes.

What we still don't know

  • The reported appearance and camera/geometry results have not been independently reproduced.
  • Transfer beyond two video datasets and the reported generator setup is unknown.
  • No downstream planning, control or physical simulation result measures practical world-model utility.

Why it matters

Visual realism alone does not guarantee that generated views preserve a coherent scene. A shared latent that represents appearance and geometry may improve consistency across camera views and provide a common interface for perception and generation. The reported controlled comparisons support investigating that representation choice, while practical world-model and downstream control value remain unestablished.
