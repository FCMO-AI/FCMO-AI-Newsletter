What changed

A September 2026 preprint describes Co-Fabric, a bus-based xPU interconnect that uses hardware port-ID routing and shadow-device enumeration to present a unified address space across host domains. On a reported 64-xPU 3D-mesh testbed, the authors report lower communication latency and higher bandwidth than their RoCE comparison system, plus faster DeepSeek R1 inference. These are single-paper results on a custom system; independent reproduction and transfer to other hardware are not established.

Evidence

  • Claimed — The authors describe a four-layer bus fabric with cross-domain port-ID routing and shadow-device auto-enumeration to provide unified xPU addressing across host domains.
  • Claimed — On a custom 64-xPU 3D-mesh system compared with the paper’s 64-port RoCE configuration, the authors report communication latency at 10–50% of the comparator for tested packet sizes, 2–15x bandwidth, and DeepSeek R1 inference throughput gains of approximately 30–80%.
  • Claimed — The paper reports an approximately 80% reduction in interconnect cost and 5% reduction in interconnect power for its Co-Fabric link configuration versus its RoCE comparison. These figures describe the compared interconnect components, not whole data-center cost or power.

Strongest baseline

A paper-configured 64-port Ethernet/RoCE system; the paper describes comparable 64-xPU resources but this review did not reconstruct the component-level matching or test an independent system.

Limitations

  • All quantitative system results are reported by the paper authors and have not been independently reproduced.
  • The principal evaluation uses one custom 64-xPU 3D-mesh configuration and a paper-specified RoCE setup; results may depend on topology, link rates, xPU model, workload and software.
  • The reported cost and power reductions apply to interconnect components, not whole-system or facility costs.
  • Expansion to larger clusters is described as potential/future work; the 64-xPU evaluation does not demonstrate thousand-xPU operation.

What we still don't know

  • No independent reproduction or public implementation/hardware artifact was inspected. The reported results use a custom 64-xPU 3D-mesh Co-Fabric system and a specific RoCE comparator configuration.
  • The paper reports component-level interconnect cost and power reductions; full system cost, energy, deployment yield, failure behavior and the sensitivity of results to comparator link rates are not established.

Why it matters

Large-model training and inference can be limited by accelerator-to-accelerator data movement. If the reported cross-host protocol and system results transfer beyond this custom testbed, they could change scale-up fabric design and the cost/performance trade-off between dedicated interconnect and Ethernet/RoCE.
