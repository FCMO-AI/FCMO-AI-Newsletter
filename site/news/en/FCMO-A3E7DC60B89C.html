Insilico reports preliminary Phase 1 activity for AI-designed pan-TEAD inhibitor ISM6331

Insilico Medicine reported preliminary Phase 1 results for ISM6331, an investigational pan-TEAD inhibitor designed using its Chemistry42 generative-AI platform. In the company’s 26 October release, it reported a 40.0% ORR at 130 mg among two of five response-evaluable mesothelioma participants, and an 18.2% ORR across 30–130 mg among four of 22 participants. The company reported 36 enrolled participants and ongoing dose escalation at an August 14 data cutoff. These are issuer-reported early-phase results from small denominators; controlled efficacy, independent interpretation and any causal contribution from AI are not established.
