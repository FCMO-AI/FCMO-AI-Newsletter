Tencent Hy4 preview combines 770B MoE capacity with sparse attention, IndexCache and native MTP

Tencent released Hy4 preview under Apache-2.0 with a 770B-parameter MoE backbone, 49B active parameters per token, 1M context, Gated DeepSeek Sparse Attention, cross-layer IndexCache reuse, four-stream identity Hyper-Connections, and a native MTP layer intended for speculative decoding.

What actually changed

Tencent released Hy4 preview under Apache-2.0 with a 770B-parameter MoE backbone, 49B active parameters per token, 1M context, Gated DeepSeek Sparse Attention, cross-layer IndexCache reuse, four-stream identity Hyper-Connections, and a native MTP layer intended for speculative decoding.

Claim vs. evidence

  • DEMONSTRATED — Tencent released Hy4 preview with a 770B backbone, 49B active parameters per token, Gated DSA with IndexCache, four residual streams, and a native MTP layer.
  • CLAIMED — Tencent reports Hy4 preview slightly ahead of GLM-5.3 and Kimi K3 on its internal 203-task expert evaluation.

Strongest baseline

Tencent's internal blind comparison against GLM-5.3 and Kimi K3; no matched-compute architectural ablation is supplied in the release card.

The caveat that matters

  • The strongest quality comparison disclosed in the model card is vendor-run and narrowly separated from GLM-5.3/Kimi K3; it is not independent evidence.
  • No matched-compute ablation isolates the contribution of sparse attention, IndexCache, iHC or native MTP.
  • 49B active parameters still implies a heavy local serving regime; sparse activation does not make the 770B stored model a small local model.
  • Tencent explicitly notes overlong reasoning and over-verification in this preview.

What remains unknown

  • No matched training/inference budget ablation establishes the isolated gains from Gated DSA, IndexCache, iHC or native MTP.
  • Vendor quality and productivity results lack independent reproduction.

FCMO Lens

Hy4 is a third major open-weight frontier release in the same week emphasizing sparse long-context retrieval and richer residual routing, while also shipping native multi-token prediction. It strengthens the architectural-convergence signal but does not by itself establish that these mechanisms outperform matched dense or hybrid alternatives.
