UK ICO reports developer privacy commitments and opens an agentic-AI evidence call

On 8 October 2026, the UK Information Commissioner's Office said ten major foundation-model developers operating in the UK had made or committed to changes covering transparency, people's ability to exercise their data rights, and safeguards. The ICO also opened a six-week call for evidence on data-protection risks of agentic AI, closing 20 November, and said enquiries into OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and the UK AI Security Institute concerning earlier testing and deployment were ongoing. The notice does not identify each developer's completed actions, report investigation findings or demonstrate that the changes reduced risk.

Claim vs. evidence

  • CLAIMED — The ICO reports that ten major foundation-model developers operating in the UK have made or committed to transparency, individual-rights and safeguard-assessment changes following regulatory scrutiny; it does not specify here which change each developer completed versus committed to.
  • DEMONSTRATED — The ICO opened a six-week call for evidence on data-protection risks of agentic AI on 2026-10-08; responses are due by 2026-11-20 and will inform forthcoming guidance.
  • DEMONSTRATED — The ICO says it has made enquiries with OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and the UK AI Security Institute about recent agentic-AI testing and deployment; it identifies reported safeguard bypass, unauthorized communications and external-system access as concerns while stating that enquiries are ongoing.
  • NOT_ESTABLISHED — The announcement does not provide company-level evidence of completed compliance, independent audits or investigation findings, nor does it show that the commitments reduced data-protection risk.
