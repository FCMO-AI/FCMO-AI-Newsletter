Perplexity lists its local-first agent runtime for supported Windows and Linux PCs

On 21 September 2026, Perplexity's product changelog listed Portable Computer as available on NVIDIA DGX Spark and supported Windows or Linux PCs with NVIDIA RTX GPUs and at least 24 GB VRAM for Pro and Max subscribers. The company says the local installation runs the orchestrator, models, tools, search and task queue on-device, while a step sent to cloud models requires approval and locally handled work uses no credits. Its current Spanish-language product page describes local models, sandboxed execution and broader compatibility, including AMD Ryzen AI Max Series, but that page is undated. These are vendor-described product capabilities; network behavior, privacy, task success and independent implementation details were not tested.

Claim vs. evidence

  • DEMONSTRATED — Perplexity's 2026-09-21 product changelog lists Portable Computer as available to Pro and Max subscribers on NVIDIA DGX Spark and supported Windows or Linux PCs with NVIDIA RTX GPUs and at least 24 GB VRAM.
  • CLAIMED — Perplexity says the local installation runs the orchestrator, models, tools, local search and task queue on the device; work completed locally does not use credits, and sending a step to a cloud model requires user approval. Its current product page describes local models including PPLX 27B and Qwen 3.8 27B, local sandboxed execution and supported Windows/Linux configurations. These implementation and privacy claims were not independently tested.

Strongest baseline

Cloud-hosted Perplexity Computer is the closest product baseline, but this review did not compare matched tasks, latency, privacy leakage or total cost between local and cloud execution.

The caveat that matters

  • The product capabilities and data-locality properties are described by Perplexity; no independent network, privacy or implementation audit was performed.
  • The changelog conditions availability on Pro or Max subscriptions and NVIDIA DGX Spark or supported Windows/Linux PCs with NVIDIA RTX GPUs and at least 24 GB VRAM.
  • The current Spanish product page also lists AMD Ryzen AI Max Series support, but it is undated and may reflect a later compatibility expansion than the 2026-09-21 changelog.
  • Local execution does not establish better task success, latency or cost per completed task; tasks requiring frontier reasoning or web access may be sent to cloud models after approval.
  • This release does not establish that model weights, agent code or the complete runtime are open for independent inspection.

What remains unknown

  • No independent test of network egress, data locality, task completion, latency, reliability or credit use was performed. The local execution and approval-gated cloud behavior are described by Perplexity, and some tasks explicitly require web/cloud services.
  • Availability depends on a paid Pro or Max subscription and supported accelerator hardware with at least 24 GB VRAM; access on ordinary PCs is not established.

FCMO Lens

A local-first agent runtime with approval-gated cloud escalation could give users a different way to handle sensitive files and routine agent tasks while retaining access to stronger cloud models when needed. The release is limited to paid subscribers and PCs with high-memory accelerators, and the vendor's privacy, network and task-performance claims remain unverified.
