China sets out national AI employment policy with worker-transition and job-risk measures

China’s General Office of the State Council published an opinion on 23 October 2026 (document date 16 October) setting out 18 measures in six areas for an employment-focused response to AI development. It calls for AI-related job creation and training while also addressing adoption-related job risk, worker transitions, employment services, labor and social-security protections, income distribution, and monitoring. The text directs risk assessment for potentially high-impact AI applications in market-access areas and calls for cautious pacing in labor-intensive sectors.
