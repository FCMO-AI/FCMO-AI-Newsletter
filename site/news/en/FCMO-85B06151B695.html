DolphinBench evaluates agent memory through task completion, cost and latency

A September 2026 paper introduces DolphinBench, a benchmark for testing agent memory through completion of knowledge-work tasks that depend on long conversation histories. The abstract describes three personas with roughly 500k tokens of messages each and 200 tasks per persona; each task is checked by comparing an agent run with and without the relevant history. The authors require reports of total cost and latency alongside accuracy. Why it matters

If the benchmark's task construction and controls are valid, evaluating task success, cost and latency together could expose memory-system tradeoffs hidden by cued question-answer accuracy alone. The abstract supports the existence and stated design of a new benchmark, but does not establish that its tasks generalize beyond three synthetic knowledge-work personas or that any system has a favorable frontier.
