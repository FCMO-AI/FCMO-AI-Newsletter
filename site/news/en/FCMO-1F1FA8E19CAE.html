Insilico announces generative-biology model partnership with an unnamed frontier lab

In a Simplified Chinese issuer-distributed release dated September 21, Insilico Medicine announced a tens-of-millions USD agreement with an unnamed frontier foundation-model lab. The companies said they would combine the partner’s model architecture with Insilico’s MMAI Gym training framework and proprietary science data, conduct wet-lab validation, and pursue commercialization with revenue sharing. The announcement does not identify the counterparty or report a resulting model, measured biological outcome or realized revenue.
