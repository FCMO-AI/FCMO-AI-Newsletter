MeshKV reports lower on-chip traffic and higher multi-stream throughput on an FPGA decoder

A paper accepted to IEEE ICCD 2026 presents MeshKV, a network-on-chip fabric for moving transformer KV-cache blocks across tiled accelerators. On an 8×8 Alveo U280 FPGA implementation with LLaMA-2-7B and Mistral-7B at 8K–32K context, the authors report up to 58% lower interconnect traffic, 2.1× KV bandwidth utilization versus a shared baseline, and 1.9× throughput for eight concurrent streams versus a centralized baseline at 32K. The paper reports five-run hardware measurements and ablations; its 17% lower energy per token is an activity-based estimate, not direct whole-system power measurement. Independent reproduction and transfer to commercial accelerators remain open.

Evidence

  • Claimed — On an 8×8 FPGA with LLaMA-2-7B and Mistral-7B at 8K–32K context, the authors report up to 58% less interconnect traffic, 2.1× KV bandwidth utilization versus Shared, and up to 1.9× multi-stream throughput.
  • Claimed — For LLaMA-2-7B at 32K context and eight streams, the paper reports 1.90× decode throughput versus centralized routing; five-run hardware throughput confidence intervals are under 3%.
  • Claimed — At 32K context the paper estimates net energy per token decreases by 17%, based on activity; link energy falls 48% while SRAM energy rises 7%. This is not a direct whole-system power measurement.
  • Demonstrated limitation — The paper evaluates one FPGA implementation, two 7B models and matched simulated/implemented configurations; it does not establish performance on commercial GPU/ASIC serving systems or independent workload deployment.

Strongest baseline

Centralized (Cent) and distributed round-robin (Shared) configurations on the same FPGA; the nearest architectural ablation is TaKV-only without full multicast/pipeline.

Limitations

  • All reported outcomes are author-reported; this review did not reproduce the design.
  • The measured implementation uses one Alveo U280 FPGA platform and LLaMA-2-7B/Mistral-7B; throughput/latency cannot be assumed to transfer to GPU or ASIC serving.
  • Net energy per token is an activity-based estimate; link energy decreases while SRAM energy increases.
  • The measured datapath implements attention/KV movement, not a complete production decoder stack.
  • Reported comparisons are against same-platform centralized/shared baselines and are not directly comparable to unrelated accelerator papers.

What we still don't know

  • No RTL, executable harness or independent artifact audit was reviewed; paper reports an Alveo U280 FPGA implementation and five-run measurements.
  • The 17% energy-per-token decrease is estimated from activity rather than direct whole-system power measurement, and transfer to GPU/ASIC production serving is unknown.

Why it matters

Long-context decoder throughput can be limited by movement of KV state across on-chip networks, even when computation and memory capacity are available. MeshKV’s matched FPGA measurements suggest cache placement, multicast and overlap can improve concurrency together. The results are specific to one FPGA design and two smaller models; direct energy measurements and commercial serving transfer are unverified.
