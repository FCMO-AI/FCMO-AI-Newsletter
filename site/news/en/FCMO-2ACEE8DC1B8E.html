Why it matters

The first harmonised AI Act quality-management standard creates a more concrete conformity route for providers of high-risk systems. The official decision says its scope includes provider quality-management systems and post-market control processes, while the Commission describes a conformity presumption for covered Article 17 requirements. The full clauses, provider uptake and compliance outcomes remain unverified.
