An energy-aware model router reports a measured accuracy–energy tradeoff across seven benchmarks

A September 2026 arXiv paper evaluates a learned router that selects among nine open-weight models using accuracy, latency, power and GPU-energy signals. On a held-out replay set of 913 questions across seven benchmarks, the authors report UniRoute accuracy of 0.618 at 2,192.2 J per answer and a KL-GRPO checkpoint at accuracy 0.609 and 1,691.9 J, about 23% lower answer energy. They describe a phase transition near 60% accuracy. The measurements use A100-SXM4-80GB GPUs and GPU energy; the headline comparison does not include router energy, which is discussed separately. This is a paper-reported result for a local-serving regime, not independent reproduction or production deployment evidence.

Limitations

  • Energy comparison is GPU answer energy measured with idle subtraction; it does not represent total system/facility energy.
  • Router overhead is not included in the headline comparison, though discussed separately; net savings after routing overhead are not established by the headline values.
  • Answers are replayed from tournament logs, not sampled stochastically during evaluation.
  • The evaluation uses a specific nine-model pool and A100-SXM4-80GB hardware; transfer to other model pools and serving stacks is unknown.
  • No independent reproduction or production deployment was inspected.

What we still don't know

  • Net energy savings after including router inference and scheduling overhead are not established by the headline comparison.
  • The reported accuracy and energy points have not been independently reproduced.
  • Production serving and transfer across hardware/model pools are untested in the reviewed evidence.

Why it matters

The work gives a concrete energy–quality operating point for routing among open-weight models and makes the omitted router overhead visible as a boundary on the headline comparison. Energy-aware serving decisions may change near an accuracy target, but these GPU-only replay measurements do not establish total facility energy or deployed savings.
