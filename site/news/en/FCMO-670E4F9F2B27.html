Epoch AI revises its Abilene estimate to 1,019k H100e

Epoch AI’s Nov 1, 2026 update raised its estimated current compute for the OpenAI Stargate Abilene site from 764k to 1,019k H100-equivalents, and its estimated IT power from 618 MW to 824 MW.
