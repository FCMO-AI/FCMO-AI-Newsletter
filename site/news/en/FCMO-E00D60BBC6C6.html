EU AI Office opens an EDIH catalogue pilot for deployment-ready European AI providers

On 17 November 2026, the European Commission AI Office opened a call inviting European AI providers with mature, SME-deployable solutions to present them to the European Digital Innovation Hubs (EDIHs). The first edition is limited to up to 100 providers; the Commission plans to list selected solutions in an “EDIH Ready” catalogue on the AI-on-Demand Platform so EDIHs can connect them with small and medium-sized enterprises. The source establishes the call and its intended design, not provider selection, SME deployment, adoption or economic impact.

Evidence

  • Demonstrated — The European Commission AI Office published a call for expression of interest for European AI providers on 17 November 2026.
  • Claimed — The Commission says the first edition invites up to 100 European providers with mature, SME-ready AI solutions; selected solutions are planned for an “EDIH Ready” catalogue on the AI-on-Demand Platform, with EDIHs acting as intermediaries to SMEs.
  • Not established — The call page does not establish which providers will be selected, whether the catalogue will generate SME deployments, or any adoption, productivity or economic effect.

Strongest baseline

The Commission describes the existing EDIH network as serving over 50,000 organisations; no comparable baseline for AI-provider discovery or SME adoption is given.

Limitations

  • This is a call announcement and planned catalogue, not evidence that any provider was selected, listed or deployed by an SME.
  • Eligibility is limited to European AI providers whose solutions are mature and ready for SME deployment; research projects, proofs of concept and MVPs are excluded.
  • The Commission gives the call publication date but no publication time, so the exact event age against a strict 36-hour window is unresolved.

What we still don't know

  • No selected-provider list, populated EDIH Ready catalogue or resulting SME deployments are reported at launch.

Why it matters

This is a concrete EU mechanism to connect AI supply with SME demand through a network of regional intermediaries, complementing general funding and strategy announcements with a provider-discovery route. The pilot is open through 17 December 2026 and targets solutions ready for deployment; whether it changes adoption remains to be measured.
