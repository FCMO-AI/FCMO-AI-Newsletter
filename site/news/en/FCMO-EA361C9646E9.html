California signs seven data-center bills covering utility costs, reporting, water and siting

On September 21, 2026, the California Governor's Office announced the signing of seven bills addressing data-center electricity, water, reporting, environmental review and rate structures. Its summary says the package will require water and electricity reporting, place specified grid-upgrade costs on data centers, require proposed projects to provide water information to local governments and suppliers, and narrow blanket environmental exemptions.
