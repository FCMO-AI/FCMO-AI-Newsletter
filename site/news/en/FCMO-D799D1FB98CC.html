China’s NDRC and five ministries announce 136 major application-scenario projects

China’s National Development and Reform Commission and five other central departments announced a first portfolio of 136 major application-scenario projects on 20 October 2026. The NDRC says the portfolio spans five categories and involves more than 1,700 technologies, 1,200 products and 2,200 standards or specifications; it also describes plans to open eligible validation opportunities to private and smaller firms and to support follow-through. A Beijing government article names five municipal projects in the list, including a 6G validation environment and an industrial-network/data-security agent service. These sources establish the announcement and selected examples, not the complete project list, AI-specific share, project funding, operational status, user access or measured technical and economic outcomes.
