OpenAI and Poland's NASK sign an agreement to explore AI safety research

OpenAI and Poland's NASK National Research Institute announced that they signed an agreement to explore AI safety, security and technical research. NASK's AI Safety Research Center, which serves as Poland's AI Safety Institute, is expected to work with OpenAI on evaluating advanced systems and identifying vulnerabilities. The announcements give no workplan, funding, access terms or completed results, so the development is the new research partnership itself, not demonstrated improvement in AI safety.
