China sets out a central policy to link scientific research with industrial innovation

A joint Central Committee General Office and State Council opinion published by China’s Ministry of Science and Technology calls for closer integration of scientific research and industrial development. It directs stronger enterprise roles in national science programs and commercialization, a pilot opening of National Natural Science Foundation programs to private firms, greater access for companies to major research infrastructure, instruments and national scientific-data centers, stronger R&D incentives for technology SMEs, and “AI+” deployment across industries. The opinion also identifies embodied intelligence among future-industry fields. The document states policy directions; it gives no funding amounts, named program calls, implementation results or measured effects.
