China reports manufacturing AI adoption indicators across 20 industries

China’s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology reported results from a year-long AI+Manufacturing action: more than 1,000 high-value use scenarios across 20 industries, 285 published application cases, more than 5,000 participating companies, and AI adoption penetration above 30% among above-scale manufacturing enterprises. The National Bureau of Statistics separately reported that AI application in key above-scale manufacturing scenarios exceeded one-third and that Q3 digital-product manufacturing value added grew 13.6% year over year. These are distinct official aggregates: neither account defines its adoption denominator here, and the output-growth figure is not attributed causally to AI. MIIT also reported over 500 high-quality datasets and more than 100 industrial agents across 20 key sectors. The accounts do not establish sustained deployment quality or productivity gains.

Limitations

  • The inspected source provides no definition for the >30% denominator or adoption threshold.

What we still don't know

  • Neither inspected official account defines the denominator, survey design, observation period or operational threshold for MIIT's >30% enterprise-level AI adoption penetration and NBS's separate >1/3 key-scenario application penetration; the two measures are not interchangeable.
  • The reported case, company, dataset and industrial-agent counts do not establish sustained production use, quality, counterfactual benefit or productivity effects.

Why it matters

Separate official indicators from MIIT and the National Bureau of Statistics suggest AI use is present across a substantial share of China's manufacturing enterprises and key scenarios, while digital-product manufacturing output is also growing. These measures have different denominators and do not establish that AI caused the output growth. The source accounts omit measurement details, sustained-use evidence and productivity outcomes, so the figures remain attributed government claims.
