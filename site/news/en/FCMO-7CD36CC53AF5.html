China's 2026–2030 plan links efficient AI development with compute-facility renewable-power targets

On 2026-09-04, China's Cyberspace Administration and six other central agencies issued a 2026–2030 plan for coordinated digital and green transformation. The plan calls for more efficient AI training and inference, research in technologies including high-bandwidth memory, low-power computing, model sparsity and compression, and a lifecycle resource-consumption assessment system for generative AI with guidance for providers to disclose model-resource use. It also sets a 2030 target for compute facilities' renewable-electricity consumption to reach the responsibility-weight level assigned to their province. These are policy directions and targets, not evidence of implementation, measured AI efficiency gains or facility compliance.
