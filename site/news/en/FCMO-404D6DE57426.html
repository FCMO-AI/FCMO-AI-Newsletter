EmbodiedMemory-Bench reports simulation results for action-outcome memory

The EmbodiedMemory-Bench preprint evaluates memory methods over 2,554 interactive episodes in four embodied-task families. The authors report matched-backbone comparisons for external action-outcome memory and show that adding maximal historical RGB context can reduce Gemini-3-Flash average success by 10.4 percentage points in the reported comparison. The results are primarily from simulation; the Robotics-ER subset is limited to 10% because of API instability. The paper does not establish physical-robot performance, independent reproduction or deployment benefit.

Why it matters

The benchmark makes a concrete comparison between structured action-outcome memory and raw multimodal history in embodied-agent tasks. If the task-scoped results reproduce, they could guide memory-system design and evaluation. The result is simulation-bounded and does not support a physical deployment claim.
