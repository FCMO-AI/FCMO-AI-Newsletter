Nineteen EU Member States design the first AI Important Project of Common European Interest

On 2026-09-16, the European Commission welcomed the design by 19 Member States of the first Important Project of Common European Interest in AI. The initiative aims to develop decentralized, commonly accessible AI services and covers the AI stack, compute-management technologies, applications, industrial products and services. The Commission said 11 participating states that would grant aid to direct participants had announced plans to begin pre-notifying their projects in September 2026. This is a design-stage policy initiative; the announcement does not establish completed notifications, state-aid approval, budgets, selected beneficiaries or deployed systems.
