Beijing seeks proposals for AI cyber-defense and adversarial-testing projects

On Oct 20, the Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission and Zhongguancun Administrative Committee opened a call for 2026 reserve projects in two areas: AI-enabled cybersecurity products and a high-fidelity adversarial testing and validation platform. The original Chinese notice describes multi-agent security operations, malicious-component detection, attack-surface management and unknown-attack defense as research directions; it calls for automated adversarial testing and high-simulation cyber ranges in the second area. It limits the first topic to enterprise applicants or enterprise-led consortia and the second to universities and research institutes. Each enterprise may submit one project and must provide matching funds at least 2:1 relative to public science funding. The notice sets a project period generally no longer than 1 year and an application window from Oct 20 through Oct 29. It is a solicitation, not evidence of selections, funding totals, deployed systems or measured cybersecurity outcomes.
