HARNESSEVO localizes useful harness optimization to control logic on ALFWorld and exposes a budget-splitting floor

HARNESSEVO decomposes an LLM-agent harness into role, task strategy, tool/format rules and reflection/control, then optimizes those components under a frozen 7B backbone. On held-out ALFWorld, the full structured method does not significantly beat stock or flat-string evolution, but a leave-one-in ablation shows the reflection/control component alone produces a statistically significant +11.9 percentage-point success gain. The paper also exposes a discrete optimization-budget failure: splitting 64 rollouts uniformly across four components leaves only 16 per component, below the reported accept-and-rescore optimizer's usable search floor, so all components freeze at their seeds. Concentrating budget on the load-bearing control component recovers the gain. WebShop is an explicit negative control where neither decomposition nor targeted control optimization significantly helps, bounding the result to tasks with recurrent verbalizable control failures rather than establishing a universal harness law.

What actually changed

HARNESSEVO decomposes an LLM-agent harness into role, task strategy, tool/format rules and reflection/control, then optimizes those components under a frozen 7B backbone. On held-out ALFWorld, the full structured method does not significantly beat stock or flat-string evolution, but a leave-one-in ablation shows the reflection/control component alone produces a statistically significant +11.9 percentage-point success gain. The paper also exposes a discrete optimization-budget failure: splitting 64 rollouts uniformly across four components leaves only 16 per component, below the reported accept-and-rescore optimizer's usable search floor, so all components freeze at their seeds. Concentrating budget on the load-bearing control component recovers the gain. WebShop is an explicit negative control where neither decomposition nor targeted control optimization significantly helps, bounding the result to tasks with recurrent verbalizable control failures rather than establishing a universal harness law.

Claim vs. evidence

  • DEMONSTRATED — On held-out ALFWorld with the frozen 7B backbone, the full structured HARNESSEVO method does not significantly beat stock or flat-string evolution in binary task success.
  • DEMONSTRATED — The reflection/control leave-one-in component yields a +0.119 success gain over stock with p=0.0046, while the other three individual components are null in the reported ALFWorld evaluation.
  • DEMONSTRATED — Uniformly splitting 64 optimizer rollouts into 16 per component leaves every slot below the reported accept-and-rescore search floor, while concentrating 32 rollouts on control recovers the significant +0.119 gain.
  • DEMONSTRATED — A 64-rollout control-only run remains positive but is borderline/non-significant at p=0.0725, so the exact effect size is not stable enough to treat as deterministic.
  • DEMONSTRATED — On WebShop, decomposition and targeted control optimization are null, contradicting any universal claim that the control slot is always the load-bearing harness component.
  • INFERRED — For budget-constrained agent self-optimization, component credit estimation and optimizer budget floors should be measured before allocating search uniformly across editable subsystems.

Strongest baseline

The strongest matched baselines are the unchanged stock harness and flat-string evolution under the same frozen backbone, both 0.642 on ALFWorld, plus leave-one-in component ablations and the WebShop task where the targeted-control hypothesis fails. These controls make the negative result as important as the positive one.

The caveat that matters

  • The positive component result is one 7B backbone on ALFWorld; cross-backbone and realistic tool-agent transfer are untested.
  • The full four-slot method does not significantly improve aggregate binary success over stock or flat evolution, so decomposition itself is not demonstrated as beneficial.
  • The 64-rollout control-only replication is positive but p=0.0725, showing meaningful stochastic variance at n=134.
  • WebShop is null, so control-slot dominance is task-contingent rather than universal.
  • The minimum viable rollout floor is specific to this accept-and-rescore optimizer and may move or disappear under a different search procedure.
  • Full token, evaluator-call, wall-clock and dollar economics were not recovered, so targeted harness search has not yet been shown superior to spending the same budget on stronger inference.
  • The complete structured HARNESSEVO configuration is only +0.015 over stock on ALFWorld and is not significant.
  • The larger 64-rollout control-only run is weaker than the 32-rollout control run and is not conventionally significant, preventing a simple monotonic-more-search interpretation.
  • WebShop shows no significant gain from any component or targeted control optimization, bounding the mechanism to environments where the harness can encode recurrent useful control corrections.

What remains unknown

  • No independent reproduction of the ALFWorld component attribution or reported optimizer floor was recovered.
  • It is unknown whether load-bearing harness credit stays localized as backbone capability or task complexity changes.
  • End-to-end economics versus stronger direct inference are unavailable.

FCMO Lens

This changes a concrete design choice in self-improving agents: do not allocate scarce optimization budget uniformly just because the harness has multiple editable components. First estimate which component has marginal causal credit and whether the optimizer has a minimum viable search quantum; then concentrate budget only where a matched control shows value, and allow a no-op outcome. The result also warns that 'more structured self-evolution' can fail simply because decomposition starves every subproblem of enough search. For priority downstream consumer/Hermes, that supports credit-first self-modification and budget-aware control-plane design rather than optimizing every prompt, skill or memory subsystem equally.
