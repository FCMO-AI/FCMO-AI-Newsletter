DeepSeek releases V4.1-Flash-1017 open weights with claimed benchmark and runtime gains

DeepSeek dated V4.1-Flash-1017 to October 17, 2026, reporting deployment to its web, app and API services. DeepSeek's official Hugging Face organization also hosts a distinct repository for the 1017 checkpoint under the MIT license, with about 510 GB of model files and a 1-million-token recommended context. The model card compares the new version with September's V4.1-Flash and reports gains on several agent and coding benchmarks, including DeepSWE v1.1 (74.2 to 77.0), NL2Repo-Bench (65.4 to 68.2) and Agents' Last Exam (31.8 to 35.4). These are provider-reported results; the linked technical report is the September report, not a 1017-specific report, and this activation did not independently evaluate the model.

Limitations

  • Benchmark scores and the reported performance-cost Pareto improvement are provider claims; no independent evaluation was performed in this activation.
  • Benchmark names, versions, harnesses and task regimes differ across rows. Terminal-Bench 4.0 should not be compared directly with earlier Terminal-Bench 2.1 or 3.0 scores.
  • No 1017-specific technical report, training-change description, full evaluation protocol, raw results or uncertainty estimates were found in the inspected first-party materials.
  • The approximately 510 GB artifact is not a low-resource model; its public availability does not establish affordable or widely accessible self-hosting.
  • The official change log supplies only the calendar date 2026-10-17, with no release time or timezone.
