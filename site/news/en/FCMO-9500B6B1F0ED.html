China proposes new technology export catalog entries and interim registration for AI exports

On 2026-10-16, China's Ministry of Commerce and Ministry of Science and Technology issued Announcement No. 48, proposing changes to the technology export catalog and opening consultation through 2026-11-15. The proposal covers 40 technologies across AI, biomedicine and photovoltaics, including 23 AI-related entries. Draft Annex 1 names categories spanning malware generation/evasion, vulnerability discovery, models and agents, chip design and compute clusters, with technical indicators for multiple entries. China’s official 2023 catalog already restricted some AI-related or adjacent technologies, including language-specific AI interaction interfaces, intelligent grading, recommendation systems, vulnerability discovery/mining and malicious-code programming; this partial baseline does not establish exact overlap with every 2026 proposal. The 2026 announcement also states interim pre-export registration pending formal revision. MOFCOM's Q&A describes public-domain exclusions, licensing paths and distinctions between covered overseas local deployment and API/app/SaaS/lightweight on-device services. Annex classifications, complete crosswalk, final adoption, case-specific applicability and implementation effects remain unresolved.
