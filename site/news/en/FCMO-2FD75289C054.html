OpenAI manuscript claims the irrationality exponent of π is exactly 2

OpenAI’s 23-page manuscript claims that for every ν>2 there is a threshold Q(ν) such that |π−p/q|≥q^-ν for all integers p and q≥Q(ν), and concludes that π has irrationality exponent 2. If correct, this would sharply improve the prior upper bounds discussed in the paper. The threshold is ineffective; the manuscript does not prove a uniform c/q² lower bound or bounded continued-fraction partial quotients. OpenAI’s public family-017 note describes a Lean statement covering the exponent claim, but no independent build, semantic audit or number-theory review was performed here. Treat the theorem as claimed, not established.

  • CLAIMED — OpenAI’s family-017 Lean scope note says its selected formal statement covers the irrationality exponent result; the research desk has not rebuilt or semantically audited the formalization.
  • CLAIMED — The manuscript cites prior upper bounds <7.101862832357 and 7.103205334137…; source papers are the comparison baseline, not proof that the new result is correct.

The separate ComparatorChallenges/PiExponent.lean contains sorry as a challenge scaffold; neither fact establishes that the proof builds or is mathematically correct.

Strongest baseline

The paper cites prior upper bounds 7.103205334137… (Zeilberger–Zudilin, 2020) and <7.101862832357 (Bai, 2026). The new claim, if valid, is a large improvement; these baselines are themselves published mathematical results and are not independently audited here.

The caveat that matters

  • The claim is from a first-party OpenAI manuscript; the research desk did not prove or independently verify it.
  • The threshold Q(ν) is non-effective; no explicit threshold is provided. The result does not claim a uniform c/q² bound or bounded partial quotients.
  • No independent mathematician proof review, pinned Lean build, complete dependency audit, or paper-to-code semantic correspondence audit was completed.

  • The pinned Lean entrypoint statically declares the exponent theorem and a Flint–Hills helper, while the family-017 scope note says the Flint–Hills consequence is outside its selected statement; the exact build/scope relationship was not resolved.
  • The ComparatorChallenges/PiExponent.lean file contains sorry as a challenge scaffold and is distinct from the OAI/NumberTheory/PiExponent/Main.lean proof entrypoint. Neither file was built or audited transitively.

What remains unknown
