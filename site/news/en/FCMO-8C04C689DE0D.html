Odisha announces ₹1.429T proposed IT and data-centre pipeline in wider project approvals

A 23 September 2026 press release from the Chief Minister of Odisha says the state’s 46th High-Level Clearance Authority approved 27 project proposals totaling ₹2,47,115.70 crore across 15 districts, with 50,162 potential jobs. Four proposals in IT, IT-enabled services and data centres account for ₹1,42,941 crore; named projects include an HCL AI-optimized data centre and a Sentraforge AI data centre with a global capability centre. These are government-reported proposed investments and potential employment, not evidence of financing, construction, energized capacity, delivered compute or realized jobs.
