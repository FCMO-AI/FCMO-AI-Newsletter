Council of Europe bureau discusses draft privacy guidance for LLM systems and agents

The Council of Europe’s Convention 108 Bureau reported that its 66th meeting in Paris on September 16–17, 2026 discussed draft guidelines on data protection in LLM-based systems, including agentic systems, with a view to finalization and adoption during 2026 and no later than the June 2027 plenary.
