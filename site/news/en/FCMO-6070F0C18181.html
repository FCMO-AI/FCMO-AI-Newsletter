Shanghai prioritizes computing facilities in its green-power direct-connection pilot plan

Shanghai’s Development and Reform Commission issued a city implementation plan for single- and multi-user direct connections between renewable generators and electricity users. The original Chinese notice prioritizes computing facilities, sets source-load matching and self-use conditions, requires grid-connected projects to pay transmission and system-operation fees, and establishes an application, evaluation and project-library process. The plan targets 500 MW of generation-side project development by 2030. A bounded official-index check did not retrieve the first selected-project library promised after the 15 Aug 2026 application deadline; project selection, data-centre economics and operating outcomes remain unknown.

Strongest baseline

The notice implements national green-power direct-connection guidance locally; it distinguishes the policy pathway from ordinary grid supply but provides no matched tariff or operating baseline.

The caveat that matters

  • The first-batch selected-project library was not retrieved in the bounded Shanghai DRC index/search check after the stated 15 Aug 2026 submission deadline. Do not infer non-publication or no compute selection.
  • No project-specific contract tariff, transmission/system charges, storage/balancing costs, metered load, renewable matching or operational performance was inspected.
  • The 2030 figure is a policy target, not realized generation or direct-supply capacity.

What remains unknown

  • Shanghai DRC stated it would publish selected projects after first-batch review; this bounded check did not retrieve the project library.
  • No project-level delivered cost or metered compute-facility operation was inspected.

FCMO Lens

The plan gives compute facilities explicit priority in a local procurement/infrastructure pathway while retaining balancing, grid-fee and performance conditions. It creates a route worth tracking for Shanghai compute sourcing, but does not show cheaper or greener electricity at any facility.
