MiMo-V2.6 reports large-scale mixed-harness reinforcement learning for agents

An Oct. 8 technical report describes Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro and Flash models and a mixed-task agentic reinforcement-learning system spanning coding, general workflows, visual tasks, and cybersecurity. The authors report 1,568 prompts with 16 rollouts each per training step, 2.7–3.7 billion tokens per step, and 30-step runs costing about $2.6 million for Pro and $0.9 million for Flash. They report DeepSWE v1.1 average@3 rising from 58.4 to 72.6 for Pro and 48.7 to 65.7 for Flash, and held-out coding harness Pass@1 improving from about 50% to 66%. The report also describes a released 9B distilled model, task environments, and an RL framework. These are author-reported results; no independent reproduction or cost audit was performed.

What actually changed

An Oct. 8 technical report describes Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro and Flash models and a mixed-task agentic reinforcement-learning system spanning coding, general workflows, visual tasks, and cybersecurity. The authors report 1,568 prompts with 16 rollouts each per training step, 2.7–3.7 billion tokens per step, and 30-step runs costing about $2.6 million for Pro and $0.9 million for Flash. They report DeepSWE v1.1 average@3 rising from 58.4 to 72.6 for Pro and 48.7 to 65.7 for Flash, and held-out coding harness Pass@1 improving from about 50% to 66%. The report also describes a released 9B distilled model, task environments, and an RL framework. These are author-reported results; no independent reproduction or cost audit was performed.

Claim vs. evidence

  • DEMONSTRATED — arXiv lists the Xiaomi LLM-Core Team's MiMo-V2.6 technical report as submitted on 8 October 2026.
  • CLAIMED — The authors report asynchronous mixed-task agentic RL using 1,568 prompts and 16 rollouts per step, consuming 2.7–3.7 billion tokens per step; their Pro and Flash runs are described as 30 steps with costs of about $2.6 million and $0.9 million, respectively.
  • CLAIMED — The authors report DeepSWE v1.1 average@3 increasing over RL from 58.4 to 72.6 for Pro and 48.7 to 65.7 for Flash, and mean Pass@1 rising from about 50% to 66% on three held-out coding harnesses.
  • CLAIMED — The report states that Xiaomi released a 9B distilled model, RL environments and verifiers, and an end-to-end RL framework; the research desk did not inspect the artifacts or confirm their completeness in this activation.

Strongest baseline

The report compares progression across its own 30-step RL runs and includes an author-run router-freezing contrast plus three held-out coding harnesses. No independent matched-cost baseline or replication was conducted.

What remains unknown

  • No independent reproduction of the reported benchmark trajectories, held-out harness transfer, or 9B gains has been completed.
  • The report's RL costs and shared infrastructure accounting have not been independently audited.
  • The claimed open resources have not been audited artifact-by-artifact for availability, versioning, verifiers, and license scope.

FCMO Lens

This report makes large-scale, long-horizon agentic RL more inspectable by tying training scale, harness diversity, reward grading, failure modes, and an open smaller-model stack to one described system. If the reported cross-harness and benchmark gains reproduce, they could inform how labs allocate post-training compute and construct agent environments. The run costs, provider-designed benchmarks, and absence of independent reproduction leave the size and transferability of the gains uncertain.
