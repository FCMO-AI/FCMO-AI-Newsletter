Peng Cheng Laboratory reports PEGA-SUS1 launch with integrated satellite AI and 5G NTN payloads

Peng Cheng Laboratory's Chinese report dated Sep 20, 2026 says its PEGA-SUS1 satellite entered its planned orbit after launch aboard a Lijian-1 Y18 rocket. The lab describes the satellite as integrating a 5G non-terrestrial-network base station and core network, onboard AI compute, laser communications and microwave communications into an orbital technology-validation platform. The report says in-orbit tests, including a planned 100 Gbps laser-communications experiment and multi-payload verification, would follow. It provides no measured on-orbit 5G, AI-compute or communications performance; the launch and payload configuration are lab-reported claims.
