Anthropic opens a Singapore office and signs an AI safety and adoption MOU

Anthropic announced on 14 October that it opened a Singapore office and signed a memorandum of understanding with Singapore’s Ministry of Digital Development and Information. The company says the MOU plans technical exchanges on AI safety, security, model evaluation, testing standards and incident response; research on AI adoption and its effects; and support for responsible AI adoption in public and private sectors. CNA independently reports that the MOU was signed on 13 October. The MOU text and implementation results were not located, so the described work remains planned cooperation rather than a demonstrated evaluation or policy outcome.
