Japan's first AI and digital reform council meeting sets out a broad proposed governance agenda

At its first meeting on October 22, Japan's AI and Digital Reform Promotion Council published a ministerial issues paper proposing broad AI and digital reform. The paper recommends considering review of Japan's AI Act and related rules; a system-wide safety approach for AI agents that includes execution harnesses, identity, guardrails, authority management, evaluation and audit logs; common infrastructure for agents to securely access data and carry out actions; and government and sector-specific deployment plans. Japan's AI Act is already in force; the paper proposes review but does not identify or adopt a specific amendment. It is a discussion proposal, not evidence of implementation.
