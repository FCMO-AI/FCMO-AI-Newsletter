ANEEL reports approval of phased rules for sharing renewable generation curtailment in Brazil

On 6 October, Brazil’s electricity regulator ANEEL reported that its board approved a rule organizing mandatory generation cuts during renewable-energy oversupply and some electrical constraints. The agency summary says eligible generation cuts will be shared across sources, with hydroelectric generation associated with spillable energy phased in at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% over four years. It also summarizes a boundary for self-producers, a shadow period beginning 180 days after publication and lasting 12 months, and definitive application 18 months after publication. This is the regulator’s announcement of the decision; the signed resolution, publication date and realized cost allocations were not recovered.
