GSA and Anthropic announce 40% consumption-based Claude discount under OneGov

GSA and Anthropic announced an expanded OneGov agreement on October 27, 2026, stating a 40% discount on token-based use of Claude models available to government, with no minimum orders and eligibility for federal, state, local and tribal governments. The announcements describe several direct, reseller and cloud purchase routes. Anthropic estimates $1 billion in government savings, but publishes no calculation in the release. GSA previously announced a separate 50% token-use discount for OpenAI; these different offers do not establish matched prices or comparative value.
