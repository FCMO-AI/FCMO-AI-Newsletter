LayerRoute: enrutamiento de capas intermedias condicionado por acciones para políticas VLA

Qué cambió realmente

LayerRoute amplía políticas visión-lenguaje-acción con una mezcla condicionada por acciones sobre estados intermedios VLM cacheados, más Action-State Reread. En las evaluaciones comparables de los autores mejora dos arquitecturas VLA en LIBERO, SimplerEnv, RoboCasa-GR1, cuatro backbones VLM y 150 pruebas con robots físicos, con un crecimiento modesto de parámetros pero una penalización medible de latencia de inferencia.

Afirmación vs. evidencia

  • DEMOSTRADO — Under the authors' matched protocol, LayerRoute improves both tested VLA policy families across the principal simulation benchmarks and improves all three reported physical-robot tasks.
  • DEMOSTRADO — The routing analysis suggests that action stages and task types prefer different VLM depths, with earlier action computation tending shallower and later stages tending deeper.
  • INFERIDO — Adaptive intermediate-layer access will generalize as a useful control interface across substantially different VLA architectures and real-world regimes.

Baseline más fuerte

The same StarVLA-pi and pi0.5 backbones under the authors' matched training/evaluation protocol without LayerRoute; component ablations, fixed-depth routing, and action-independent routing provide additional attribution controls.

La salvedad que importa

  • The main architecture evidence covers two VLA policy families rather than a broad independent set of production robot stacks.
  • The method is not free: reported batch-1 inference latency increases by roughly 10% for StarVLA-pi and 25% for pi0.5.
  • Physical-robot evidence is limited to three tabletop tasks, one embodiment and 50 trials per task; no independent reproduction or multi-site evaluation was found.
  • Evaluation repetitions do not establish robustness across independent training seeds, data mixtures or substantially different robot embodiments.
  • An authoritative released-code repository/commit was not recovered during this activation despite the paper's code-release statement.

Qué sigue sin saberse

  • Paper states that code is released, but the authoritative repository and exact evaluated commit were not recovered.
  • No unaffiliated reproduction was found for the simulation or physical-robot gains.

Lente FCMO

Si el efecto se generaliza, los sistemas VLA no necesitan tratar la última capa VLM como la única interfaz de control útil: el estado de acción puede seleccionar adaptativamente la profundidad semántica durante la ejecución. Eso cambia una decisión arquitectónica concreta para políticas corporizadas de largo horizonte, aunque las ganancias actuales permanecen acotadas y no reinician el campo.
