NVIDIA underwrites up to $105B of OpenAI-linked 4.25 GW data-center leases as it expands into AI infrastructure finance

NVIDIA, OpenAI and SB Energy announced on August 17, 2026 a PORTS-Pike infrastructure arrangement under which OpenAI has agreed to secure approximately 8 GW of IT capacity in Pike County, Ohio. NVIDIA is the exclusive AI-compute infrastructure provider, will invest $1.5B in SB Energy, and provides land/power/shell credit support for the initial 4.25 GW; NVIDIA's later fiscal Q2 2027 Form 10-Q discloses guarantees capped at $105B for those initial obligations and an option for additional support covering roughly 3.8 GW as the site scales. The nine-phase site uses 20-year OpenAI leases; OpenAI says the first 800 MW is expected in 2028, while NVIDIA's filing describes service beginning in fiscal 2029. The same 10-Q shows a broader strategic shift: $279B of supply/capacity commitments, $36B of AI-cloud agreements and August MOUs intended to mobilize more than $500B of third-party AI-infrastructure capital, although those MOUs are explicitly preliminary.

真正发生了什么变化

NVIDIA, OpenAI and SB Energy announced on August 17, 2026 a PORTS-Pike infrastructure arrangement under which OpenAI has agreed to secure approximately 8 GW of IT capacity in Pike County, Ohio. NVIDIA is the exclusive AI-compute infrastructure provider, will invest $1.5B in SB Energy, and provides land/power/shell credit support for the initial 4.25 GW; NVIDIA's later fiscal Q2 2027 Form 10-Q discloses guarantees capped at $105B for those initial obligations and an option for additional support covering roughly 3.8 GW as the site scales. The nine-phase site uses 20-year OpenAI leases; OpenAI says the first 800 MW is expected in 2028, while NVIDIA's filing describes service beginning in fiscal 2029. The same 10-Q shows a broader strategic shift: $279B of supply/capacity commitments, $36B of AI-cloud agreements and August MOUs intended to mobilize more than $500B of third-party AI-infrastructure capital, although those MOUs are explicitly preliminary.

主张与证据

  • 已证实 — NVIDIA 的 10-Q 披露了 2026 年八月签订、上限为 $105B 的担保，用于支持 SB Energy PORTS-Pike 园区约 4.25 GW、与 OpenAI 相关的 IT 负载。
  • 已证实 — OpenAI 宣布签订协议，通过 20 年租赁结构在 PORTS-Pike 获得约 8 GW IT 容量；SB Energy 建设、拥有并运营数据中心，NVIDIA 则供应该园区的 AI 算力基础设施。
  • 已证实 — NVIDIA 已签署的初始支持覆盖 4.25 GW；随着园区扩展，NVIDIA 还拥有为约 3.75-3.8 GW 提供额外支持的选择权。
  • 声称 — NVIDIA 八月签订的融资平台 MOU，旨在随时间动员超过 $500B 的第三方 AI 基础设施资本。
  • 推断 — 除加速器供应商外，NVIDIA 正成为重要的基础设施融资协调者，这可能改变前沿实验室获得算力的方式和行业纵向依赖。

最强基线

传统 hyperscaler/模型实验室采购：客户自行获得土地、电力、建筑外壳、融资和加速器，而没有供应商提供的实质信用支持。

最重要的限制

  • OpenAI 的约 8 GW 是已签约的未来 IT 容量，不是当前运行算力；容量将分阶段交付，仍取决于施工就绪、基础设施、许可、环境审查和融资。
  • NVIDIA 的 $105B 上限是担保风险敞口，并不表示 NVIDIA 将支出或支付 $105B；风险敞口会随租户履约下降，而且只覆盖规定部分，不覆盖园区全部成本。
  • NVIDIA 已签署的信用支持适用于初始 4.25 GW。其余约 3.75-3.8 GW 的支持是一项选择权，并非同等的当前担保风险敞口，尽管 OpenAI 表示已同意获得园区总计约 8 GW 容量。
  • >$500B 以上数字涉及旨在动员第三方资本的初步 MOU，可能永远不会成为最终协议。
  • 以 GW 签约的电力不能直接换算为已交付的加速器 FLOPs；硬件代际、利用率、PUE、网络和租户工作负载组合均未知。
  • OpenAI 给出的首批 800 MW 在 2028 年交付日期是预期，不是已交付容量。

仍然未知的部分

  • PORTS 尚无任何阶段可以投入服务；实际交付时间、通电 IT 负载和硬件部署仍是未来事实。
  • 公开披露没有完整说明加速器代际、利用率、租赁经济性或项目总资本结构。
  • >$500B 以上融资平台 MOU 属于初步协议，可能不会形成最终承诺。

FCMO 视角

The AI compute bottleneck is increasingly land, power, construction and financing rather than accelerator supply alone. A GPU vendor using its balance sheet and ecosystem contracts to de-risk multi-gigawatt customer infrastructure can materially change who can finance frontier-scale training/inference and can tighten vertical coupling among chip vendor, cloud/intermediary, capital provider and model lab. OpenAI's primary announcement says it has agreed to secure approximately 8 GW at the campus, but the full capacity is phased and conditional; NVIDIA's signed credit support is for the initial 4.25 GW, with the remaining roughly 3.75-3.8 GW covered by an option rather than equivalent current guarantee exposure.
