ASPIRE exposes a gap between autonomous update loops and retained capability gains from vague goals

ASPIRE evaluates whether an agent can self-evolve when given only a broad natural-language capability goal rather than a human-specified task and metric. The agent must operationalize the goal, create data and local validation signals, choose weight or harness updates, and decide when to evaluate while the task-level evaluator remains hidden. The benchmark uses 520 expert-authored hidden items across six goals and supports both model-weight and harness evolution. The authors find that current systems routinely complete training and editing loops, but hidden base-relative weight gains are sparse and unstable; local self-evaluations often fail to transfer, later training can erase earlier improvements, and the strongest evolved harness remains below an engineered Qwen-Agent reference. The most important result is therefore negative: executing an optimization loop is materially easier than defining, measuring and retaining the intended capability improvement.

真正发生了什么变化

ASPIRE evaluates whether an agent can self-evolve when given only a broad natural-language capability goal rather than a human-specified task and metric. The agent must operationalize the goal, create data and local validation signals, choose weight or harness updates, and decide when to evaluate while the task-level evaluator remains hidden. The benchmark uses 520 expert-authored hidden items across six goals and supports both model-weight and harness evolution. The authors find that current systems routinely complete training and editing loops, but hidden base-relative weight gains are sparse and unstable; local self-evaluations often fail to transfer, later training can erase earlier improvements, and the strongest evolved harness remains below an engineered Qwen-Agent reference. The most important result is therefore negative: executing an optimization loop is materially easier than defining, measuring and retaining the intended capability improvement.

主张与证据

  • 已证实 — ASPIRE 只向智能体提供自然语言能力目标，同时隐藏下游评估任务，并使用覆盖六个目标的 520 个专家编写项目。
  • 已证实 — 当前受测智能体通常能完成训练和 harness 编辑循环，但在隐藏评估上，相对于基础模型的权重增益仍然稀少且不稳定。
  • 已证实 — 智能体可能信任狭窄的自评或不匹配的训练数据，导致局部改进无法迁移到隐藏能力评估，而继续训练或搜索还可能抹掉早期增益。
  • 已证实 — 在 benchmark 聚合结果上，报告的最强演化 harness 仍低于工程化 Qwen-Agent 参考。
  • 推断 — 目标操作化应作为独立于任务执行的自治轴来测量，因为成功的更新循环不能证明能力得到了保留。
  • 推测 — ASPIRE 证明当前模型通常无法自主改进自己。

最强基线

进入时的基础模型是权重演化的关键基线，因为相对直接父模型的改进可能掩盖累积退化。对 harness 演化而言，工程化 Qwen-Agent 参考强于报告的自演化后继者。任务明确的后训练 benchmark 提供较低自治度比较，其中目标定义和评估由外部给出。

最重要的限制

  • 证据由 benchmark 作者产生；本次运行未找到独立 end-to-end 复现。
  • 六个目标以及受测创建者/backbone/harness 配置，不能证明自我演化具有普遍能力或普遍无能。
  • 有限的自适应聚合反馈可以影响 checkpoint 选择；不应把同一固定隐藏目标切片视为完全未触碰的选择后确认集。
  • 权重级增益稀少且不稳定，因此不应把正面样例推广为可靠递归自我改进的主张。
  • harness 演化只与一个工程化参考和固定运行时权重条件比较；更强搜索或不同脚手架可能改变排序。
  • 自生成代理指标的质量与模型能力、工具访问、prompt 设计和算力预算相互纠缠。
  • 该基准确实包含一些局部或临时改进；结果不是自我演化永远无效，而是保留下来的隐藏迁移并不可靠。
  • 任务明确的自动后训练系统可以表现好得多，因为目标由外部提供。这些证据支持执行自治，但不反驳 ASPIRE 更难的目标操作化条件。
  • 能力更强的未来行动者或更好的验证架构，可能把当前负面结果转化为可靠增益；ASPIRE 是当前能力测量，不是理论不可能性证明。

仍然未知的部分

  • 缺少对 ASPIRE 权重和 harness 轨迹的独立复现。
  • 在自适应目标级反馈之后，该 benchmark 缺少完全未触碰的选择后确认评估器。
  • 目标操作化失败如何随模型能力和验证架构扩展，目前尚不明确。

FCMO 视角

Automated R&D and self-improving agents are often credited for successful training jobs, generated experiments or child systems that beat an immediate predecessor. ASPIRE shows why those are insufficient endpoints when the objective itself is underspecified. A system can optimize a coherent proxy, damage the original model, then recover relative to the damaged parent and still remain below its incoming base. For higher-autonomy AI, target operationalization must therefore become a first-class measurable capability: define what the goal means, preserve alternative interpretations, use diverse adaptive proxies, reserve independent confirmation evidence, and promote only base-relative gains that survive retention checks. This boundary is directly relevant to distinguishing an automated research intern executing well-defined work from a more autonomous scientist that must decide what should count as scientific progress.
