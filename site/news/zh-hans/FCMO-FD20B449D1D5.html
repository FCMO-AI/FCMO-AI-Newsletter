Meta Muse Voice Transcribe unifies streaming ASR, diarization, endpointing and learned adaptive delay

Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Voice Transcribe, an autoregressive Muse Spark-family real-time audio model that consumes 80 ms audio chunks, decides whether to listen longer or emit text, and jointly emits speaker and speech-boundary tokens. Meta says reinforcement learning jointly rewards word error rate and delay so the model learns per-word adaptive latency; an independent Artificial Analysis evaluation reported 3.1% English WER at 0.16 s after end-of-speech in its September 1 streaming comparison.

真正发生了什么变化

Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Voice Transcribe, an autoregressive Muse Spark-family real-time audio model that consumes 80 ms audio chunks, decides whether to listen longer or emit text, and jointly emits speaker and speech-boundary tokens. Meta says reinforcement learning jointly rewards word error rate and delay so the model learns per-word adaptive latency; an independent Artificial Analysis evaluation reported 3.1% English WER at 0.16 s after end-of-speech in its September 1 streaming comparison.

主张与证据

  • 已证实 — Muse Voice Transcribe 处理 80 ms 音频块，并通过结合 WER 与延迟的奖励学习逐词自适应等待。
  • 声称 — 该系统联合执行流式 ASR、说话人分离和端点检测，并支持 20+ 位说话人、70+ 种训练语言以及一小时以上的音频。
  • 已证实 — Artificial Analysis 在其九月 1 日的比较中独立测得：英语 WER 为 3.1%，说话结束后延迟为 0.16 秒。
  • 推断 — 自适应等待可能是一种可迁移的、按计算价值为多模态智能体调度算力的实例。

最强基线

Artificial Analysis 比较中的当代流式转写系统，包括 ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Realtime、AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro Realtime 和 Cartesia Ink-2。

最重要的限制

  • 没有发布模型权重。
  • 参数量、训练算力、训练数据规模和音频数据来源均未披露。
  • 多数能力主张仍来自第一方；本次运行只找到一个范围较窄的流式英语基准测试的独立证据。
  • 托管 API 无法对固定等待策略与学习式等待策略进行受控消融。

仍然未知的部分

  • 尚未提供模型权重和可复现的训练方案。
  • 自适应等待机制尚未通过独立消融，与匹配条件下的固定等待对照进行比较。

FCMO 视角

The technically interesting piece is not transcription alone but learned value-of-waiting: the model spends extra temporal context selectively on difficult words instead of using a fixed latency policy. That is a concrete multimodal instance of compute-on-demand and may transfer to agents deciding when another observation, retrieval, rollout, or reasoning step is worth its latency.
