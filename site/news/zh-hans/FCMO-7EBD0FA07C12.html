SQD disaggregates subquadratic-attention inference around heterogeneous memory and compute regimes

SQD (SubQuadratic Disaggregation) argues that serving systems designed around dense-attention assumptions leave efficiency on the table for sparse, linear and sliding-window attention models. It splits decode according to which subquadratic components require full-KV indexing versus static-memory compute, then maps those components onto heterogeneous DRAM/SRAM resources. In the authors' adjusted 8xB200 system proxy, reported average tokens/J gains over their strongest GPU-only baselines are 53% on GLM 5.2, 31% on Nemotron 3 Ultra and 56% on Gemma 4 31B. An analytical Rubin+LPX fixed-power model reports 1.2x-1.5x tighter achievable latency and up to 3.6x throughput versus attention-FFN disaggregation.

真正发生了什么变化

SQD (SubQuadratic Disaggregation) argues that serving systems designed around dense-attention assumptions leave efficiency on the table for sparse, linear and sliding-window attention models. It splits decode according to which subquadratic components require full-KV indexing versus static-memory compute, then maps those components onto heterogeneous DRAM/SRAM resources. In the authors' adjusted 8xB200 system proxy, reported average tokens/J gains over their strongest GPU-only baselines are 53% on GLM 5.2, 31% on Nemotron 3 Ultra and 56% on Gemma 4 31B. An analytical Rubin+LPX fixed-power model reports 1.2x-1.5x tighter achievable latency and up to 3.6x throughput versus attention-FFN disaggregation.

主张与证据

  • 已证实 — 在作者调整后的 8xB200 异构系统代理中，相比其最强的纯 GPU 基线，SQD 在 GLM 5.2、Nemotron 3 Ultra 和 Gemma 4 31B 上的平均 tokens/J 分别提升 53%、31% 和 56%。
  • DEMONSTRATED_IN_MODEL — 在作者的 Rubin+LPX 固定功率分析模型中，相比注意力-FFN 解耦，SQD 可实现的延迟缩短 1.2x-1.5x，throughput 最高达到 3.6x。
  • 推断 — 在真实网络争用和调度器开销下，类似增益会在实际交付的异构生产系统中持续存在。

最强基线

论文为调整后的 B200 代理描述了最强的纯 GPU 基线，并在 Rubin+LPX 分析比较中采用注意力-FFN 解耦作为基线。

最重要的限制

  • 调整后的 8xB200 结果是系统代理，并非无关联方在生产集群上的干净复现。
  • Rubin+LPX 的醒目比较是分析结果，因此取决于对内存、互连、功率和调度参数的假设。
  • 报告的增益可能随 batch 大小、序列长度、KV 占用、稀疏模式、互连争用和加速器利用率而显著变化。
  • 证据只是在所研究条件下证明一种有前景的服务设计，并不代表每一种亚二次模型或工作负载都具有普遍优势。
  • 本次活动未找到独立复现。

仍然未知的部分

  • 尚未找到无关联方在真实硬件上的复现。
  • Rubin+LPX 结果对内存带宽与容量、链路带宽、排队和工作负载组合的敏感性，仍是迁移效果的关键问题。

FCMO 视角

If these gains survive hardware-real reproduction, subquadratic model architecture changes not only FLOP complexity but the optimal serving topology: memory-heavy selection and compute-heavy attention/FFN can be provisioned separately. That could materially change inference-cluster design as hybrid and sparse attention become common.
