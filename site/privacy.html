Privacy

FCMO AI Newsletter is published as a static website. The generated pages do not intentionally embed first-party analytics, advertising trackers, account systems, forms, or newsletter cookies.

The site may load selected story-specific images directly from cited paper, project, laboratory or publisher hosts. Those requests use no-referrer where supported, but the remote host may still receive routine request metadata under its own terms. Following an external source link likewise takes you to a third-party service whose policies apply there.

The hosting and network providers used to deliver the site may process routine request metadata under their own terms and privacy policies.

The public publication is designed to contain only material intended for public distribution. Operational credentials, private workspaces, private project context and non-public publishing metadata are outside the publication contract.
