Method

Evidence, importance & publication

FCMO AI Newsletter follows an evidence-first editorial process. Research claims are separated by evidence quality, confidence, and potential consequence so that an important claim is not presented as a proven one merely because its implications are large.

Importance is not confidence. A potentially transformative weak signal can carry a high impact score while remaining explicitly unconfirmed. A well-established incremental result can carry a lower impact score.

Evidence and confidence

Where practical, reporting prioritizes primary papers, official technical material, source artifacts, model or data cards, benchmark artifacts, standards and direct attributable statements. Limitations, contradictory evidence and open evidence gaps are preserved when they materially affect interpretation.

Importance scale

The 1–10 Alien Evidence scale describes potential consequence if a central claim is true in the stated regime. It does not measure truth, source prestige, or certainty. A 10/10 is reserved for genuinely field- or civilization-resetting consequences rather than ordinary state-of-the-art progress.

Historical editions and corrections

Dated editions are historical snapshots. Later evidence may change the current assessment of a topic without silently rewriting what an earlier edition said. Material corrections or status changes are surfaced through the corrections page.

Automation

Pages and feeds are produced through a deterministic publishing process and pass privacy and release checks before publication. Editorial judgments remain visible through evidence labels, confidence, limitations and source links.
