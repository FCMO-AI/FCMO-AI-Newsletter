Evaluation & Science

TasteVal reports a 2.30× experimental-compute multiplier for Opus 5.5 on eight bounded AI R&D tasks

Evidence Acredible_unconfirmedImpact 7/10 · Very majoractive

Jaffe and Sherburn introduce TasteVal, an author-built benchmark of eight text and vision tasks intended to measure experimental design and interpretation when a fixed research problem is given. They report that Opus 5.5 (max) reaches a 2.30× experimental-compute multiplier against the best expert attempt per task (95% CI 1.15–4.37). The authors also estimate that frontier-model multiplier performance doubled about every 3.0 months after December 2025, while final normalized performance shows no corresponding trend break. These are preprint results on withheld tasks, not evidence that models choose valuable research questions or outperform experts across broad research work.

Reasoning & Post-training

Guidance-TTT trains a compact strategic guide while keeping the solution executor frozen

Evidence Acredible_unconfirmedImpact 6/10 · Majoractive

A 5 October 2026 preprint proposes Guidance-TTT, which updates a compact 8B or 14B model to suggest high-level changes while a stronger GLM-5.2 executor remains frozen and implements each proposal as a complete candidate. An executable verifier scores candidates; an archive and PUCT selection supply parents for later attempts. Authors report results on Polyomino Packing, Lasso path optimization, AHC058 and TriMul, including 91.89 on Polyomino, 0.1739 on Lasso, an AHC058 score of 850,082,731, and 1,129 μs TriMul latency. These are author-reported outcomes against heterogeneous prior references, not matched replications. Key model-scale comparisons use one trajectory, implementation failures affect verifier rewards, and full code/data artifacts have not been independently audited here.

Evaluation & Science

Preprint reports a visual-grounding refusal gap across five vision-language models

Evidence Acredible_unconfirmedImpact 6/10 · Majoractive

A 5 October 2026 preprint evaluates five vision-language models on 15,401 matched harmful visual requests, varying whether each request asks for a text answer or a spatial grounding such as a point or box. The authors report that grounding refusal trails text-answer refusal by 31–59 percentage points depending on the domain, and that safety system prompts do not close the gap. A fine-tuning recipe combining grounding-form refusals, capability data and benign examples reportedly improves refusal on two open models while tracking over-refusal and grounding capability. This is a single author-run preprint scored by one LLM judge; 14,578 of 15,401 pairs are from the social-bias dataset, code is not released, and real-world harm incidence is not established.

Reasoning & Post-training

On-policy power distillation trains a model to absorb sequence-level answer sharpening

Evidence Acredible_unconfirmedImpact 6/10 · Majoractive

Potraghloo and colleagues present on-policy power distillation (OPPD), which uses sequential Monte Carlo samples from the model being trained and a frozen teacher’s sharpened sequence distribution to reweight a maximum-likelihood update. The authors report single-generation accuracy increases of up to 23.0 points on MATH500 and 27.3 points on GSM8K over the untrained model at the same temperature, and report gains in a same-checkpoint GRPO comparison. Their abstract also claims performance above published 64-candidate power-sampling results; the existing candidate review found that this comparison uses a different evaluation protocol, while the same-paper 16-candidate comparison is close and does not show OPPD ahead. These are author-reported preprint outcomes, not independent validation or a matched total training-plus-inference cost result.

Agents & Memory

A study finds LLM aggregators can treat repeated reports as partly independent evidence

Evidence Acredible_unconfirmedImpact 6/10 · Majoractive

Gao and colleagues study how four language models count repeated reports across controlled sensor logs with an exact Bayesian oracle, web documents, and generated multi-agent transcripts. Their abstract reports that a forwarded copy counts as 0.06–0.42 of an independent reading, and that repeated statements can imply premature commitment on 5%–40% of logs in the tested settings. The authors report that a declaration about copy contribution lowers copy weight to 0.08 or less on controlled logs, while a worker-side rule to refer to readings rather than restate them lowers belief-implied early commitment from 11.2% to 1.1% in their team-log protocol. This is one author-reported preprint, not an operational error rate or independent replication.

Evaluation & Science

A mechanics benchmark finds models can flag an impossible premise yet return a solved status

Evidence Acredible_unconfirmedImpact 6/10 · Majoractive

Yang and Wang report testing 14 models on 30 pairs of mechanics problems, each with a valid version and a version made impossible by changing an input or assumption. In 12 of 90 replies from three recent models, the system did not reject the flawed problem; in 11 of those replies, raters and numerical checks found that the model described the flaw, answered a corrected problem, and still marked the original as solved. A later retest found that warning/status prompts raised rejection for three of four models but lowered valid-problem solving for three. These are author-reported results on a small mechanics task set, not an operational failure rate.

Agents & Memory

Recursive video retrieval reports higher frozen-VLA success on perturbed simulation tasks

Evidence Acredible_unconfirmedImpact 5/10 · Notableactive

A 5 October 2026 preprint reports Recursive Video In-Context Learning (RV-ICL), which indexes one task demonstration into four read-only levels—keyframes, phases, moments and short clips—and lets a planner retrieve detail for its current subgoal. The authors report HarnessVLA success rising from 92.6% to 96.5% on LIBERO-PRO and 86.7% to 95.8% on LIBERO-Plus. These are author-reported simulation results: on LIBERO-PRO the RV-ICL arm used 50 episodes while prior-work baselines used 100, and the paper’s reported significance statistic does not yet have a recovered matched-data audit. On LIBERO-Plus the methods were compared on a stratified 120 of 10,030 variants. The paper reports 1.8x wall-clock time and 1.4x input tokens on that comparison; physical-robot transfer is untested.

Evaluation & Science

ECB staff analysis reports AI-intensive euro-area sectors shifting toward market-based debt finance

Evidence Acredible_unconfirmedImpact 5/10 · Notableactive

In an ECB staff blog dated 6 October 2026, authors classify euro-area sectors by AI adoption using Eurostat enterprise surveys and by AI development using patent shares. Sectoral balance-sheet data through April 2026 show high-AI sectors, especially developers, with lower leverage and a recent shift toward market-based debt financing. A quarterly Bayesian VAR through 2024Q4 reports a stronger peak credit and investment response to an AI-patent-share shock in the United States than in the euro area. The authors suggest deeper US capital markets as one possible explanation; this is staff analysis, not an ECB Governing Council position or an independently reproduced causal estimate.
