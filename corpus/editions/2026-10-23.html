Desk: evaluation_science Evidence: A · credible_unconfirmed · active · importance 6/10 (Major) Importance rationale: If the reported mismatches hold, a machine-checked Lean result cannot be treated as verification of the specific natural-language proof without a semantic bridge. What changed: Bastounis, Circelli and Hansen compare OpenAI’s natural-language Navier–Stokes proof with cited Lean declarations. The authors’ code guide also says their highlighted real-robot correlation uses an exploratory horizon; at the paper-prespecified horizon it is weaker. — FCMO-5ABCEA930CA1

The authors report that transfer gains depend on agreement with source examples; under fully conflicting rule quartets, results differ in sign across two source splits and joint correctness is low. In tests of TypeSafe's Jev service, answers change more across equivalent wordings than across identical-text repeats and often follow altered table values or mappings. These are author-reported results on synthetic finite-domain tasks and one service version; code and data are stated as forthcoming, and this activation did not independently reproduce the analyses. — FCMO-85DE30B70CE3
